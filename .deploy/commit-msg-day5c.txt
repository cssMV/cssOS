unified-entry: 5/5 万能入口 routed through cssmvUnifiedEntry

All five universal entrances now go through one labeled helper that
prints [entry:source] in the console, short-circuits on a fresh
cssmvPipelineLastResult, and otherwise kicks the MV Pipeline panel.

Wired:
- [entry:logo-longpress]   dock-watch hold      (app.dock-runtime)
- [entry:dock-mic]         mic dock click       (app.dock-runtime)
- [entry:dock-watch]       watch dock click     (app.dock-runtime)
- [entry:context-menu-mv]  right-click "一键MV" (app.context-menu)
- [entry:apply-render]     Advanced "Apply&Render" (app.advanced-panel-settings-render)

Critical bug fix in apply-render: was calling BOTH
openMvPipelinePanel AND legacy startCreation in parallel, causing the
"走一遍旧流程" double-pipeline behaviour the user reported. Dropped
the legacy startCreation call when MV Pipeline kicks off
successfully.

cssmvUnifiedEntry helper exposed in mv-pipeline-panel:
- Reads cssmvPipelineLastResult; if fresh (default 10min window)
  short-circuits to openWatchPreviewFlow which adopts the result and
  skips the new run.
- Otherwise opens the MV Pipeline panel via openMvPipelinePanel and
  fires runAll, with the entry source visibly logged for triage.
