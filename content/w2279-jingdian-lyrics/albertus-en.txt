[Style: High medieval classical, pipe organ and bowed vielle with male chant underneath, Cologne cathedral acoustics, patient and observational, slow triple time]
[Vocal: baritone lead with a small male schola answering, Latin-rite diction, straight tone]
[Language: English. One Latin motto of his own in the Chorus, one line of Old High German charm in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Under the Stars, Lauingen]
Under the stars I speak, and speak it low;
the moonlight falls, the night is mild and wide.
I was a Swabian boy who liked to know
the names of things, and went outside to find
whether the book was right about the moth.

[Verse 2: Padua, and the Black and White]
In Padua the Dominicans took me in
against my family's wish, and gave me time.
A shadow of longing circles in the mind;
whenever something calls, it calls by name.
I answered, and I walked to Cologne on foot.

[Chorus 1: Experimentum Solum]
Experimentum solum certificat in talibus —
experiment alone makes sure of such things.
Not Aristotle, not the gloss, not us:
the hawk you watch, the plant that actually springs.
Like moonlight on the dark — go out and look.

[Verse 3: De Animalibus]
I opened fish, I counted out the legs
of insects, I drew the crow's nest in the rain.
I said the earth is round and gave my reasons.
I said whales are not fish, and said again.
Twenty-one volumes. Mostly: I went and saw.

[Verse 4: The Ox from Naples]
A heavy silent boy sat at the back
and the others called him dumb, and I said no —
this ox will bellow for the world. I packed
him off to Paris with my letters. So
he wrote the Summa. I had taught him to look.

[Bridge: Kirst, Imbi Ist Hucze]
[Old High German incantation, the Lorsch Bee Blessing, tenth century]
Kirst, imbi ist hucze! Nu fluc du, vihu minaz,
hera, fridu frono in godes munt heim zi comonne gisunt.
Christ, the swarm is out — now fly, my cattle, home.
Older than my books, this was already German:
a charm to call the bees back to the hive.

[Chorus 3: Experimentum Solum]

[Chorus 4: Doctor Universalis]
They made me bishop and I begged to stop;
two years, and then I walked back to the school.
A shooting star goes over, and I drop
whatever I was holding, as a rule.
I only ever wanted one more year of looking.

[Outro: Cologne, 15 November 1280]
Under the stars I speak, and speak it low;
the moonlight falls, and I am old and done.
They say I lost my memory at the end
and sat in the lecture hall with nothing in my hand.
Let the patron of the sciences be that man.
