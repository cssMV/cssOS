[Style: Medieval Latin sacred, male schola in plainchant over a sustained organ pedal, Dominican office of readings, five rising phrases per chorus, austere and cumulative, very slow]
[Vocal: male schola in unison with one solo cantor, Latin-rite diction, straight tone, no vibrato]
[Language: English. One Latin principle of his own in the Chorus, one archaic Latin prayer-formula in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Question Two, Article Three]
Whether God exists. I put the question third
in the second question of the first part,
and gave two objections first, in their own words:
there is evil in the world, and nature's art
needs no one else. Then: on the contrary.

[Verse 2: The First Way]
Whatever moves is moved by something else,
and that by something else, and so on back.
It cannot go back forever, or the shelf
has nothing holding it, and the whole stack
falls. Therefore a first mover, moved by none.

[Chorus 1: Gratia Non Tollit Naturam]
Gratia non tollit naturam, sed perficit —
grace does not destroy nature, it completes it.
That is the whole of me in seven words:
reason is not the enemy, it fits.
Faith and reason walking. Neither blind.

[Verse 3: The Other Four]
Cause, and the chain of causes. Then the fact
that things need not have been, and yet there is something.
Then degrees: a hotter and a hottest. Then
the arrow of an arrow-less thing flying
somewhere. Five ways. Not proofs. Five open doors.

[Verse 4: Siger of Brabant, and the Lamp]
The Averroists said the world had no beginning
and that one intellect thinks in all of us.
I fought them — and I fought the men who banned them,
because they wanted Aristotle burned
and I had just spent twenty years translating him.

[Bridge: Mars Pater, Te Precor]
[Archaic Latin prayer-formula, Cato, De Agri Cultura 141, the oldest Roman state prayer]
Mars pater, te precor quaesoque uti sies
volens propitius mihi domo familiaeque nostrae.
Father Mars, I pray and I beseech you.
That is the oldest Latin asking anything,
and I asked in the same tongue, of someone else.

[Chorus 3: Gratia Non Tollit Naturam]

[Chorus 4: Paris, 7 March 1277]
Three years after I died, the Bishop of Paris
condemned two hundred nineteen propositions
and some of them were mine. The Dominicans
appealed for fifty years against that list.
It was lifted in thirteen twenty-five. Then the halo.

[Outro: Doctor Angelicus]
Canonised in twenty-three, Doctor of the Church
in fifteen sixty-seven, and in eighteen seventy-nine
the Pope said: teach him everywhere. The straw
I said it all was turned into a spine
for eight hundred years of argument. Fine. Argue.
