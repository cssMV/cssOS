[Style: Viennese classical symphonic, full orchestra with timpani and massed choir, minor-key sturm und drang opening into a blazing D major chorale, heroic and granitic, allegro con brio]
[Vocal: bass-baritone soloist with a large four-part chorus, operatic and open, German-choral diction]
[Language: English. One line of the German original from the Ninth in the Chorus, one line of Old High German in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Hearing in a Silent World]
In a world gone silent I still heard the music —
that was the thing you brought us and still bring.
A heartbeat under every rise and fall of it
and nothing in my ears but the one ringing.
Musica movet animum. Let it move.

[Verse 2: Heiligenstadt, 6 October 1802]
I wrote a letter to my brothers once
and never sent it: how I nearly went,
how art alone held back my own two hands.
Six weeks in a village, and the long descent.
Sunt lacrimae rerum. There are tears in things.

[Chorus 1: Freude]
Freude, schöner Götterfunken, Tochter aus Elysium —
joy, bright spark of the gods, Elysium's daughter.
Beethoven, Beethoven, we are still coming,
your music is the melody we are after:
in a world gone silent we still hear the music.

[Verse 3: The Torn Page, 1804]
I wrote his name across the Eroica's head
and when they told me he had crowned himself
I tore the title page right through and said
he is no better than the rest. The shelf
still holds the copy with the hole in it.

[Verse 4: Four Notes]
Three short, one long. Not fate knocking at a door —
that was my secretary, and he lied.
It is a motif, and it will not let you go,
and I built four movements on it and I tried
to make the dark give up and it gave up.

[Chorus 2: Freude]
Freude, schöner Götterfunken, Tochter aus Elysium —
joy, bright spark of the gods, Elysium's daughter.
Beethoven, Beethoven, we are still coming,
your music is the melody we are after:
in a world gone silent we still hear the music.

[Bridge: Dat Ero Ni Uuas Noh Ufhimil]
[Old High German incantation, the Wessobrunn Prayer, around 790]
Dat gafregin ih mit firahim firiuuizzo meista,
dat ero ni uuas noh ufhimil.
I learned among men the greatest of wonders:
that there was no earth yet, and no heaven above.
Out of that nothing, sound. Then everything.

[Chorus 3: Freude]
Freude, schöner Götterfunken, Tochter aus Elysium —
joy, bright spark of the gods, Elysium's daughter.
Beethoven, Beethoven, we are still coming,
your music is the melody we are after:
in a world gone silent we still hear the music.

[Chorus 4: 7 May 1824]
I stood with my back to the hall and beat the time
against an orchestra I could not hear.
Caroline Unger took my arm and turned me round
to a house on its feet, and the hats in the air.
Muss es sein? Es muss sein. It must be.

[Outro: Vienna, 26 March 1827]
A thunderclap over the Schwarzspanierhaus,
one fist, they said, and then the room was still.
Twenty thousand in the street. Schubert held
a torch. Time flies, but the number stands.
Fiat lux. And it was always light.
