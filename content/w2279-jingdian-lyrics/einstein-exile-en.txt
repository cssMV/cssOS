[Style: Modern classical, solo cello with low strings and a distant upright piano, Central European emigre melancholy opening into American brass, wide and displaced, andante]
[Vocal: baritone lead, weary and level, a mixed choir answering from far off, no ornament]
[Language: English. One line of the German original in the Chorus, one line of ancient Hebrew in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Pasadena, January 1933]
I was in California when the thing happened
and I did not go home. I said so to the press
on the boat: as long as I have any choice,
only a country with civil liberty, tolerance
and equality before the law. Not that one.

[Verse 2: The Raid, the Bounty, the Books]
They searched the cottage at Caputh for weapons
and found a bread knife. They took the sailboat.
The bank account, the papers, and the house.
Twenty thousand marks on my head, a photograph
in a magazine captioned: not yet hanged.

[Chorus 1: Mir Fehlt Die Begabung]
Es fehlt mir die natürliche Begabung —
I lack both the natural aptitude
and the experience to deal with people.
I am deeply moved, and saddened, and ashamed
that I cannot accept. Find a better man.

[Verse 3: Princeton, October 1933]
They offered three thousand dollars. I asked
whether I could live on less. I could not
do the arithmetic of an American year.
A small house on Mercer Street, a boat,
a sweater, and no socks. Forty years of work left.

[Verse 4: The Affidavits]
I signed and signed: for cousins, for strangers,
for a violinist, for a chemist, for a child.
Every signature was a visa, and the quota
was a wall with a slot in it. I filed
hundreds. It was the most useful thing I did.

[Chorus 2: Mir Fehlt Die Begabung]
Es fehlt mir die natürliche Begabung —
I lack both the natural aptitude
and the experience to deal with people.
I am deeply moved, and saddened, and ashamed
that I cannot accept. Find a better man.

[Bridge: Ashira La-Adonai]
[Ancient Hebrew verse, the Song of the Sea, Exodus 15, oldest Hebrew poetry]
Ashira la-Adonai ki gaoh gaah,
sus ve-rochvo ramah va-yam.
I will sing, for He has triumphed; horse
and rider He has thrown into the sea.
The oldest song my people have is a crossing.

[Chorus 3: Mir Fehlt Die Begabung]
Es fehlt mir die natürliche Begabung —
I lack both the natural aptitude
and the experience to deal with people.
I am deeply moved, and saddened, and ashamed
that I cannot accept. Find a better man.

[Chorus 4: Lincoln University, 1946]
I went to a Black college in Pennsylvania
and said it plainly: racism is a disease
of white people, and I do not intend
to be quiet about it. Nobody filmed me.
The papers that week were busy with the bomb.

[Outro: Mercer Street, 18 April 1955]
The aorta went. They offered me the surgery
and I said no: it is tasteless to prolong
a life artificially. I have done my share.
A page of calculation on the table, wrong,
unfinished, and the river took the rest.
