[Style: Modern classical, prepared piano and detuned strings in restless dialogue, two motifs arguing and never agreeing, cold chamber acoustics, unresolved ending, allegretto inquieto]
[Vocal: baritone lead answered by a second solo voice as Bohr, conversational and argumentative, no ornament]
[Language: English. One line of the German original in the Chorus, one line of ancient Hebrew in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Solvay, Brussels, 1927]
Twenty-nine of us in a photograph
and seventeen would take the prize in time.
Every morning I would bring a case
at breakfast; every evening Niels would climb
out of it by dinner. Five days of that.

[Verse 2: The Photon Box, 1930]
I built him a box with a clock and a shutter
and weighed the light that left it, and I said:
now you have the time and the energy together,
and your uncertainty is dead.
He did not sleep. By morning he had my own gravity.

[Chorus 1: Spukhafte Fernwirkung]
Spukhafte Fernwirkung — spooky action
at a distance, acting through no field.
Physics should describe a real world
in space and time. I will not let it yield
to a theory that only tells me what I'll see.

[Verse 3: EPR, 1935]
Podolsky wrote it up and I disliked the paper.
Two particles that part, and measuring one
tells you the other instantly, however far.
Either the world is not there till it is seen,
or the theory is incomplete. I said: incomplete.

[Verse 4: Thirty Years, Nothing Published That Held]
The unified field. Every few years a paper
and every few years a retraction after.
Kaluza, Klein, bivectors, five dimensions, teleparallel —
I have become, in the eyes of my colleagues,
an obstinate heretic. They were kind about it.

[Bridge: Ha-Shamayim Mesaprim]
[Ancient Hebrew verse, Psalm 19, in the Hebrew numbering verse 2]
Ha-shamayim mesaprim kevod El,
u-maaseh yadav maggid ha-rakia.
The heavens tell the glory, and the sky
declares the work of hands. My people looked up
and assumed there was something to be read.

[Chorus 3: Spukhafte Fernwirkung]
Spukhafte Fernwirkung — spooky action
at a distance, acting through no field.
Physics should describe a real world
in space and time. I will not let it yield

[Chorus 4: Bohr Was Right About the Measurements]
He was right about every measurement
and I was asking a different question: is there
a world when nobody is in the room?
Bell wrote it down as something you could test
nine years after I died. The answer was: spooky.

[Outro: The Last Letter to Besso]
Michele went in March and I wrote his family:
he has preceded me a little in leaving
this strange world. For us believing physicists
the distinction between past, present and future
has only the meaning of a stubborn illusion.
