[Style: Modern classical chamber, solo violin over slowly rotating strings, a single timpani heartbeat, wide and weightless, building to one luminous resolution, adagio]
[Vocal: baritone lead, close and unhurried, a wordless choir opening in the last chorus, no vibrato]
[Language: English. One line of the German original in the Chorus, one line of ancient Hebrew in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Bern, 1907 — A Man Falling]
Still at the patent desk, still writing claims,
and the thought arrived while I was in the chair:
a man in free fall does not feel his weight.
There is no gravity for him. It is not there.
Everything after that was eight years' work.

[Verse 2: Grossmann, Zurich, 1912]
Marcel, you must help me or I shall go mad.
I needed geometry I did not have —
Riemann, Ricci, the tensor, the curved frame.
He put the books in front of me. The math
was ninety years old and waiting on a shelf.

[Chorus 1: The Happiest Thought]
Der glücklichste Gedanke meines Lebens —
the happiest thought I ever had, and short:
that falling and floating are the same thing,
and therefore gravity is not a force
but the shape of the room. Mass bends the room.

[Verse 3: The Wrong Theory for Three Years]
The Entwurf was wrong and I defended it
through 1914, published, argued, taught,
and Mercury came out at eighteen seconds
when the sky says forty-three. I had thought
it close enough. It was not close enough.

[Verse 4: November 1915 — Four Papers in Four Weeks]
I threw it out and started from the covariance
and sent the Academy a paper every Thursday.
On the eighteenth the perihelion came out right —
forty-three arcseconds, from nothing, no fit —
and I had palpitations for three days.

[Bridge: Shema Yisrael]
[Ancient Hebrew incantation, the Shema, Deuteronomy 6:4]
Shema Yisrael Adonai Eloheinu
Adonai echad. Hear, Israel: the Lord is one.
One. Older than the field equations, older
than every unity I tried to prove —
my people said it first, and said it short.

[Chorus 3: The Happiest Thought]

[Chorus 4: Hilbert, Five Days Earlier]
Hilbert in Goettingen had the action too
and dated his submission before mine.
We were two weeks from a priority war
and he withdrew the claim and signed the line:
the physics was mine. The mathematics his.

[Outro: Ten Equations]
Ten coupled equations, nonlinear, and nobody
had solved one when I published. Then Schwarzschild
solved it in a trench in Russia, in the winter,
and sent it to me, and was dead by June.
The room still bends. I never saw it bend.
