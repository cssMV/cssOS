[Style: German Baroque, chamber organ and muted strings in a long slow passacaglia, library acoustics, paper and dust, patient and unresolved, adagio]
[Vocal: tenor lead, quiet and archival, a distant choir on the choruses only, straight tone]
[Language: English. One Latin motto in the Chorus, one line of the oldest German heroic verse in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Librarian, Wolfenbuettel and Hanover]
Forty years a librarian to a duke
who wanted a family history written out.
I catalogued, invented the subject book,
proposed a shelf-mark system nobody took,
and read my way through everything they had.

[Verse 2: The House of Brunswick, Unfinished]
The Guelph history: I went to Austria,
to Rome, to Modena, read charters, copied seals,
and got the family back to the ninth century,
and never finished it. Three volumes. Reels
of notes. The duke outlived his patience first.

[Chorus 1: Theoria cum Praxi]
Theoria cum praxi — theory with practice.
I gave the Berlin Society that line
and asked for mills and mines and insurance maths
beside the pure geometry. Combine
them, or the academy is a drawing room.

[Verse 3: Sixteen Thousand Letters]
Eleven hundred correspondents, and I wrote
to every one of them, in four languages:
to Peking about hexagrams, to Rome
about the churches, to a dozen sages
about a tooth, a mine, a pump, a proof.

[Verse 4: Two Hundred Thousand Sheets]
What I left behind is in a vault in Hanover:
two hundred thousand pages, mostly loose,
half of it in a shorthand of my own,
and the best of my philosophy still unused
because I filed it and then wrote the next.

[Bridge: Ik Gihorta Dat Seggen]
[Oldest German heroic verse, the Hildebrandslied, ninth-century manuscript]
Ik gihorta dat seggen, dat sih urhettun
aenon muotin, Hiltibrant enti Hadubrant.
I heard it told that two men met alone,
a father and a son, as enemies —
and the poem breaks off. It has no ending either.

[Chorus 3: Theoria cum Praxi]
Theoria cum praxi — theory with practice.
I gave the Berlin Society that line
and asked for mills and mines and insurance maths
beside the pure geometry. Combine
them, or the academy is a drawing room.

[Chorus 4: The Universal Library]
I wanted one encyclopedia for everyone,
all the sciences reduced to one notation,
an academy in Dresden, Vienna, Petersburg,
one church again, one script, one federation —
I proposed all of it. I got the shelving.

[Outro: Unread for Two Hundred Years]
They buried me as if I were a thief
and the archive sat unopened, box on box.
The Monadology came out after my death.
The rest of it is still being transcribed.
Read me slowly. I filed it all for you.
