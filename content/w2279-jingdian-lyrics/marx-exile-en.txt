[Style: European orchestral, restless strings with accordion and a distant brass band, minor key modulating through four keys without settling, migratory and unresolved, moderato]
[Vocal: baritone lead, travelling and plain, a scattered chorus answering from different distances, no ornament]
[Language: English. One line of the German original in the Chorus, one line of Old High German charm in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Cologne, 1843 — the Paper Is Shut]
I edited a paper for fifteen months
and said what I thought about the censors twice.
They shut it in March. I had a wife to keep,
a doctorate no university would take,
and a country that had written down my name.

[Verse 2: Paris, Brussels, Cologne, London]
Paris expelled me in forty-five. Brussels
until the barricades. Then Cologne again
for one more paper and one more tribunal —
acquitted by a jury of merchants, then
expelled for good. I landed. I never left.

[Chorus 1: The Workers Have No Country]
Die Arbeiter haben kein Vaterland —
the workers have no country of their own.
No passport, no renaturalisation,
no government that answers to the sound
of a man who writes. So be at home in all of it.

[Verse 3: Stateless for Thirty-Four Years]
Prussia would not take me back; Britain
would not make me British; I applied
in 1874 and they refused
on the grounds that I had not been loyal. I died
a citizen of nowhere, in a chair.

[Verse 4: The International, 1864]
Saint Martin's Hall, a meeting of the trades,
and they asked a German nobody to draft
the address. I wrote it mild, on purpose, for
the English unions — then put the shaft
in the last paragraph, where they would sign it.

[Chorus 2: The Workers Have No Country]
Die Arbeiter haben kein Vaterland —
the workers have no country of their own.
No passport, no renaturalisation,
no government that answers to the sound
of a man who writes. So be at home in all of it.

[Bridge: Christ Uuart Gaboran Er Uuolf Ode Diob]
[Old High German charm, the Vienna Dog Blessing, tenth century]
Christ uuart gaboran er uuolf ode diob,
do uuas sancte Marti Christas hirti.
Christ was born before the wolf or thief;
Saint Martin was his shepherd. The oldest German
charm I know is for keeping a flock alive.

[Chorus 3: The Workers Have No Country]
Die Arbeiter haben kein Vaterland —
the workers have no country of their own.
No passport, no renaturalisation,
no government that answers to the sound
of a man who writes. So be at home in all of it.

[Chorus 4: Paris, May 1871]
Seventy-two days, and then the week of blood,
and twenty thousand shot against the walls.
I wrote it up in two months while the mud
was fresh and they were still reading the rolls.
The whole world learned my name from that pamphlet.

[Outro: Highgate, the Soft Ground]
From the rubble, hand in hand — I wrote that too,
and did not live to see one brick of it.
A stateless man, a borrowed plot, a few
umbrellas, and a speech that Engels writ.
Tomorrow gets composed. Somebody write it.
