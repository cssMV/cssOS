[Style: French classical orchestra, military march in a minor key, snare and massed brass, tragic grandeur collapsing into a slow funeral tread, allegro then largo]
[Vocal: dramatic baritone lead with a male soldiers chorus answering, declamatory, open and bitter]
[Language: English. One line of the French original in the Chorus, one line of the oldest French in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: A Still Night Sky]
Under a still night sky the small stars show
above the old wall and the empty square.
The great king rode out everywhere, and so
the glory and the pride went off like air.
I look at the wall and think of everything.

[Verse 2: Toulon to Austerlitz]
A Corsican lieutenant with a map,
a battery of guns laid low and true.
Then Austerlitz, and the sun through the gap,
and forty centuries looking down at you.
Soldiers, the pyramids are watching. March.

[Chorus 1: One Step from the Sublime]
Napoleon — the dream a horseman had,
the colours flying, the unyielding year.
Du sublime au ridicule il n'y a qu'un pas:
one step from the sublime to the absurd.
I took the step. I took it with the world.

[Verse 3: Moscow, the Cold, the Betrayal]
Cloud over everything, the drums like thunder.
Moscow was ash before we got inside.
Betrayal grew in the heart, and the long plunder
ran backwards down the Berezina tide.
Six hundred thousand out. Some tens came home.

[Verse 4: Waterloo, 18 June 1815]
The hour of the last battle, fate on fate;
Grouchy did not come, and Bluecher did.
The Guard went up the ridge, and it was late,
and history came back and shook the lid.
The Guard dies and does not surrender. Then it died.

[Chorus 2: One Step from the Sublime]

[Bridge: Pro Deo Amur — the oldest oath in our tongue]
[Oldest French, the Oaths of Strasbourg, 14 February 842]
Pro Deo amur et pro christian poblo
et nostro commun salvament, d'ist di in avant.
For the love of God and for the Christian people:
two brothers swore it, and they broke it after.
That was already French. So was the breaking.

[Chorus 3: One Step from the Sublime]

[Chorus 4: The Code Outlasts the Cannon]
At the edge of despair I called out loud
for the soul of some hero to answer me.
Nothing answered. What answered was the crowd
of clerks who kept the Code, the lycee,
the metre and the bank. Those did not fall.

[Outro: Longwood, Saint Helena]
Under a still night sky the small stars show
above a wet house on a rock at sea.
The echo of all history goes low
and stays cut into something under me.
Two thousand miles of ocean. And the damp.
