[Style: English Baroque orchestra, solemn and processional, strings with trumpets and chamber organ, Purcell funeral-march gravity opening into a bright major close, largo maestoso]
[Vocal: bass-baritone lead with a cathedral choir answering, English Restoration diction, straight tone]
[Language: English. One Latin motto in the Chorus, one line of Old English incantation in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Woolsthorpe, Plague Years]
In the huge dark of space, who hears a call?
A boy sent home from Cambridge by the plague
sits in his mother's orchard by the wall
and in two years invents the coming age.
He tells nobody. He puts it in a drawer.

[Verse 2: The Apple, and the Causes]
He thought it quietly; the past went by like smoke,
and the law of cause came clear inside his head.
Away from all the quarrels, he awoke
the unknown gently, and it was not dread —
why should an apple always fall straight down?

[Chorus 1: Hypotheses Non Fingo]
Oh, Newton, lighthouse standing in the void,
you used your light to show the dark its road.
Hypotheses non fingo — I feign none.
I give the law and not the reason. Hold
to nature's rules. You are the hymn of reason.

[Verse 3: Principia, 1687]
Halley paid for it out of his own purse
because the Royal Society had spent
its money on a book about the fish.
Three books, one law, the whole firmament
set out in geometry, and signed.

[Verse 4: Shoulders of Giants]
If I have seen further than other men,
it is by standing on the shoulders of giants —
I wrote that to a man I would then burn
through thirty years of patient, filed defiance.
Hooke was short. I never let it go.

[Chorus 2: Hypotheses Non Fingo]
Oh, Newton, lighthouse standing in the void,
you used your light to show the dark its road.

[Bridge: Gemyne Thu, Mucgwyrt]
[Old English incantation, the Nine Herbs Charm, Lacnunga, tenth century]
Gemyne thu, mucgwyrt, hwaet thu ameldodest,
hwaet thu renadest aet Regenmelde.
Remember, mugwort, what you made known,
what you arranged at the great proclamation —
I kept a furnace too, and spoke to leaves.

[Chorus 3: Hypotheses Non Fingo]
Oh, Newton, lighthouse standing in the void,
you used your light to show the dark its road.
Hypotheses non fingo — I feign none.
I give the law and not the reason. Hold
to nature's rules. You are the hymn of reason.

[Chorus 4: A Boy on the Shore]
I do not know how I appear to the world;
to myself I have been a boy on a beach
finding a smoother pebble, a prettier shell,
while the whole great ocean of truth lay undiscovered
in front of me. Time is a river. Go.

[Outro: Westminster, 1727]
Under the stars I will remember the pursuit,
and follow Newton toward the coming light.
Nature and nature's laws lay hid in night;
God said, Let Newton be — and all was light.
They put him with the kings. He was a king.
