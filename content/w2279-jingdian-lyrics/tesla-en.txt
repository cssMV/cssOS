[Style: Modern electronic, analogue synthesizer arpeggios over high-frequency sine drones, Tesla-coil crackle percussion, Serbian folk modal in the bridge, cold and ascending, driving mid-tempo]
[Vocal: tenor lead, processed and clear with a wide reverb tail, a synthetic choir answering, no vibrato]
[Language: English. One verbatim line of his own in the Chorus, one line of Old Church Slavonic in the Bridge.]

[Verse 1: Smiljan, and the Light in the Dark]
A glimmer in the darkness, and it held:
a cat's back in the winter throwing sparks,
and a boy in a Serbian priest's house, spellbound,
asking his father what the fire was.
Is nature a cat? And who, then, strokes her back?

[Verse 2: Budapest City Park, February 1882]
Walking with Szigeti at the end of the day
I quoted Faust aloud and stopped mid-line:
the field was rotating. There was no delay,
no commutator, no brush, no spark. The design
came whole. I drew it in the dust with a stick.

[Chorus 1: The Future Is Mine]
Tesla — you opened up the door ahead,
the radio waves go dancing in the sky.
Let the present be theirs; I do not mind.
The present is theirs; the future, for which
I really worked, is mine. It always was.

[Verse 3: The War of the Currents]
Edison paid me nothing and I left.
Direct current cannot cross a county line;
alternating current crosses a continent.
Niagara, eighteen ninety-five: the turbines turn
and Buffalo lights up. That argument is closed.

[Verse 4: Colorado Springs, 1899]
I built a coil and made lightning indoors,
one hundred thirty feet of it, and the town
went dark because I burned the generator out.
The thunder was heard fifteen miles down.
And I heard something else. I think I did.

[Bridge: Iskoni Be Slovo]
[Old Church Slavonic, Cyril and Methodius, ninth century, the oldest Slavic verse]
Iskoni be slovo, i slovo be u Boga,
i Bog be slovo. Vsa tem bysha.
In the beginning was the word, and the word
was with God. Older than every wire,
my people wrote that first. I only sent it.

[Chorus 4: Wardenclyffe]
A tower on Long Island, and a shaft
driven a hundred feet into the ground,
to put the power through the earth itself
and charge the whole of it, and ask for nothing.
Morgan asked: where do I put the meter? Then he stopped.

[Outro: Hotel New Yorker, Room 3327]
Current running through my hands, and then
no hands, no coil, no tower, and no debt paid.
Eighty-six, alone, and the one friend
a white pigeon that came to the window ledge.
In the flashing light, go on. Keep going on.
