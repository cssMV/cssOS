# CSS Studio — Pitch Deck Outline (10–12 slides)

Design notes: dark cinematic theme, one idea per slide, lead with the live product.
Each slide below = title + the 1–3 lines that go on it + speaker note.

---

### Slide 1 — Hook
**CSS Studio — open the app, a music video plays. With sound. Instantly.**
- (Background: a 10-second autoplaying MV loop, audible.)
- *Note:* don't explain — let it play 5 seconds, then talk.

### Slide 2 — The problem
**AI media is split in half. Nobody owns the whole experience.**
- Suno/Udio: music, no video. · Sora/Kling: video, no sound, no song.
- Audiences get *files to download*, not a place to *enjoy*.

### Slide 3 — The insight (the moat)
**Never burn the layers together.**
- Lyrics + audio + video kept as separate layers → any work is infinitely re-voiceable and
  re-languageable. Burned-in output (everyone else) can't do this.

### Slide 4 — The product
**Zero-friction watch. In-cinema create.**
- Open → audible full-screen MV feed. Type one line → it generates and plays in the *same* panel.
- Multi-language / multi-voice on every work, one tap.

### Slide 5 — Why now
**Generation got cheap; the destination layer is empty.**
- Everyone's building tools; no one owns AI-video-with-sound *consumption + distribution*.

### Slide 6 — Market & roadmap
**MV today → the long-form ladder.**
- MV → short-drama (微短剧, multi-billion proven) → series → multi-thread 3D film.
- Same layered pipeline scales up each rung. TAM grows with each step.

### Slide 7 — Business model
**Multiple revenue surfaces, already wired.**
- Credits/generation · paid background queue · in-panel works marketplace ·
  per-work "add a language" upsell · subscription. Future: ad-tier + creator rev-share + IP licensing.

### Slide 8 — Status & early signal *(be honest)*
**Product-complete, pre-launch.**
- Built: 2,153 works · 1,270 audio stems · 1,534 language tracks · 66.7k frames · self-healing infra.
- Early commerce signal: 66 payment intents / 29 paid orders — pre-marketing.
- The raise funds: launch + first real users + retention proof.

### Slide 9 — Moat
**Layered pipeline + civilization-aware generation + in-cinema retention loop.**
- Hard to copy: it's an architecture choice + a consumption surface, not a model.

### Slide 10 — Team
**Founder who ships.** (Jing — fill bio: what you've built solo, speed, domain insight.)
- *Note:* at pre-seed, investors bet on the founder. Make this concrete.

### Slide 11 — The ask
**Raising $0.5–1.5M pre-seed (SAFE).**
- 18 months runway → launch, prove D1/D7/D30 retention, ship one long-form format.
- Non-dilutive first: $100–350k cloud/model credits + accelerator.

### Slide 12 — Vision
**The home for AI cinema — where the world watches, creates, and trades stories in every language.**
- (Close on the live app again.)
