## 1. THE NAME

**INT. OBSERVATION STATION — RECORDS ROOM — DAY**

Boxes stacked to the ceiling now, all stamped **FOR CONSOLIDATION**. Two men in
coveralls are carrying them out, cheerfully, two at a time.

Ethan works against them. Photographing, fast, sloppy, whatever he can reach
before it goes.

He gets to a thin folder. **MINUTES — SOUNDS OF EARTH COMMITTEE — 1977.**

He photographs page one. Page two. Page three — the argument, verbatim, the one
we watched.

Page four is the attendance sheet.

He stops.

CLOSE ON four signatures. The fourth reads: **D. VANCE.**

**COVERALLS #1**
*(passing, friendly)*
That one going?

**ETHAN**
*(not looking up)*
No.

## 2. THE COUPON

**INT. OBSERVATION STATION — SIDE ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Ethan with the lead-lined case open. The dull metal coupon in his palm.

He turns it over. On the back, stamped small, a batch number he has looked at
ten thousand times and never once read.

He puts it beside the photographed minutes. Beside a line in the equipment
schedule: the plating batch used on the record jacket.

Same number.

He sits down on the floor. Not dramatically. His legs just stop.

**CLARA (O.S.)**
Ethan?

She's in the doorway. He holds the coupon up without turning round.

**ETHAN**
He gave me this when I was nine. He said, *if you ever need to know what's true,
don't ask a book.*
*(beat)*
I thought it was a lesson. It was a **tool**. He cut it off the same sheet.

**CLARA**
*(carefully)*
Your father was on that committee.

**ETHAN**
He was the one who lost.

**CLARA**
He didn't lose. You said the contents list —

**ETHAN**
*(over her)*
Why don't I *know* this, Clara?

That stops her.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
I'm one of three people on this planet who can see an edit. And I have gone
thirty-one years not knowing what my own father did in 1977. Nobody hid it. It
was in a folder in a building I work in.
*(beat)*
So either I never looked —

**CLARA**
Or you did.

Silence.

> **NOTE (bible §2.2–2.3):** the horror here is not that xÈth erased the father.
> It is that Ethan cannot tell the difference between having been edited and
> having simply never asked. That uncertainty is permanent and is never resolved
> in any beat. Do not resolve it.

## 3. THE THING THAT CANNOT BE STOPPED

**INT. CLARA'S FLAT — KITCHEN — NIGHT**

Small. Warm. Radio on low. Clara is making food with the total concentration of
someone not thinking about food.

Ethan is at the table with the shoebox of index cards, sorting them into two
piles.

**CLARA**
How many now?

**ETHAN**
Four hundred and nine.

**CLARA**
And how many can we prove?

He looks at the piles. One is very tall. One is empty.

**ETHAN**
Nothing. Not one. Every proof I have is a document, and documents are theirs.

He pushes both piles together. It doesn't matter.

**CLARA**
Then we go public.

**ETHAN**
With what? A man says the flood killed more people than the record says.
*(beat)*
Tomorrow the record says something else again and there was never a man.

**CLARA**
Then we take the Office. Physically. Servers, master index, all of it.

**ETHAN**
And he rewrites the raid.

She turns round.

**CLARA**
*(flat)*
So you're telling me he's already won.

**ETHAN**
I'm telling you he can't be beaten *inside* the record.
*(beat)*
So we stop trying to win the record.

He stands.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Clara — what's the one thing on this earth he has never been able to touch?

**CLARA**
*(after a beat)*
Light that's already left.

**ETHAN**
Light that's already left.

> **NOTE (bible §2.3 · 不可改写清单第一项):** this is the hinge of the entire
> film and it is a piece of physics, not a device. Once a photon is past the
> dish it is outside every archive, every server, every skull, forever. The plan
> that follows is the only unrewritable act available to a human being.

## 4. THE PLAN

**INT. CLARA'S FLAT — CONTINUOUS**

She is already ahead of him and doesn't like where it goes.

**CLARA**
You want to *broadcast*.

**ETHAN**
Everything. Every card in that box. The real numbers, the real dead, the real
grief. Read out loud, in order, at a dish big enough to push it clear.

**CLARA**
To who? Nobody's listening.

**ETHAN**
Doesn't matter. It isn't a message. It's a **deposit**.
*(beat)*
The second it clears the dish it stops being ours. It stops being anyone's. It
just travels, and it keeps being true at every point along the way, forever, and
there is no pen in this universe long enough to reach it.

Clara sits down.

**CLARA**
And Voyager?

**ETHAN**
We aim at Voyager.

**CLARA**
Why, if it can't hear —

**ETHAN**
It *can* hear. It can't change what's on the disc, but it can hear, and it can
answer. Twenty-four hours out. Twenty-four back.
*(beat)*
If it comes back, we know the light left. That's our receipt.

**CLARA**
*(quietly)*
Two days.

**ETHAN**
Two days.

> **NOTE (bible §八):** the 24-hour one-way delay is the film's metronome from
> here to the end. It is real physics and it is free. Do not shorten it for
> pacing under any circumstances.

**CLARA**
And him? He just watches us do it?

**ETHAN**
He watches us do it *once*.

## 5. THE DISH

**EXT. DEEP SPACE NETWORK COMPLEX — DAY**

Desert. Chain-link. And beyond it, absurd against the scrub: a seventy-metre
dish, tipped up, doing nothing at all.

Ethan and Clara at the fence, two figures, tiny.

> **BLOCKING (mandatory):** keep them small in frame and keep the dish *idle* —
> not sweeping, not tracking. The scale does the work. See production notes.

**CLARA**
Scheduling's public. Anyone can read who's got the dish and when.

**ETHAN**
Then so can he.

**CLARA**
There's a window Thursday. Ninety minutes, uncommitted.
*(beat)*
Ethan, ninety minutes is the whole box. Barely.

**ETHAN**
Then we don't stop to breathe.

She looks up at the thing.

**CLARA**
My father used to say you can't put a fence round the sky.
*(beat)*
He was wrong. It's right there. It's got a *gate*.

## 6. WHAT SHE IS ACTUALLY GUARDING

**INT. CAR — NIGHT — DESERT ROAD**

Driving back. Dark. Dashboard light. Clara at the wheel, Ethan with the box on
his knees.

**CLARA**
Read me one.

**ETHAN**
Clara —

**CLARA**
If I'm going to stand in a room for ninety minutes and say them out loud, I want
to know what one of them sounds like in my mouth.

He takes a card off the top.

**ETHAN**
*(reading)*
"Marion Bell. Sixty-one. Drowned in her kitchen, ninth of April, holding the
back door shut against the water because her dog was still inside."
*(beat)*
"The record says she moved to Ohio."

Clara drives. Nothing happens on her face for a long time.

**CLARA**
Give me the box.

**ETHAN**
You're driving.

**CLARA**
*(steady)*
Give me the box, Ethan.

He puts it on her lap. She keeps one hand on the wheel and one flat on the lid,
the way you hold something that is trying to get out.

> **NOTE (bible §3.1):** this is Clara's scene and it is the Druyan hour in a
> person — she is taking custody of other people's pain on purpose, knowing what
> it will cost. **No one comments on it. Not now, not ever.**

## 7. OUT

**INT. OBSERVATION STATION — RECORDS ROOM — NIGHT**

Empty shelves. The consolidation has been through. One desk lamp.

Ethan alone, packing the last of the cards. He stops at the folder of 1977
minutes and looks, one more time, at the fourth signature.

**ETHAN**
*(to it)*
You knew somebody was going to try.

He puts the minutes in the box with the dead.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
You just thought it'd be a government.

He turns off the lamp.
