## 1. A KINDNESS, PERFORMED CORRECTLY

**INT. OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — PUBLIC COUNTER — DAY**

Not a ministry. A converted post office. Good light, a queue of six, a water
cooler. Somebody has put flowers on the counter and nobody has taken them away.

> **WARDROBE / ERA:** ordinary civil service. Cardigans. Lanyards. Nothing
> uniform, nothing black, no insignia. If it reads as a regime, it is wrong.

JUNE HALE (30s, deputy archivist, sleeves pushed up, a pen behind her ear)
slides a single sheet across the counter to MRS. ODELL (70s).

**JUNE**
That's the settled figure. It's signed, and it's the only one now.

**MRS. ODELL**
*(reading it a long time)*
They sent me a letter in '11 that said one thing. And the plaque said another.
And my daughter's school book said a third.

**JUNE**
I know.

**MRS. ODELL**
I stopped saying his name out loud. Because I didn't know which of them I was
saying it *in*.

June comes round the counter, which is against procedure, and sits in the chair
next to her, which is also against procedure.

**JUNE**
Say it now, if you like.

Mrs. Odell says a name, very quietly. We do not hear it.

> **NOTE (bible §四·节点二):** the programme must be shown *working*, and the
> relief must be real. If this scene plays as sinister the whole beat collapses
> and xÈth becomes a cartoon. Direct it as the best day of June's week.

## 2. THE METHOD

**INT. OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — UPPER FLOOR — DAY**

An open floor of desks and trolleys. Paper going one way, pulp going the other.

xÈth moves through it slowly. **Charcoal suit, plain tie, no security.** He stops
at desks. He knows names. He is not performing this; he simply does it.

He arrives beside June, who is elbow-deep in a trolley.

**xÈth**
Mrs. Odell.

**JUNE**
*(surprised)*
You were watching.

**xÈth**
I was walking past. You left your counter.

**JUNE**
*(defensive)*
She'd been carrying it eleven —

**xÈth**
You left your counter. It was the right thing to do and I would like you to keep
doing it.

He picks a folder out of her trolley, glances, puts it back in a different pile.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Do you know what I am actually asking of you, June?

**JUNE**
Consistency.

**xÈth**
Mercy. Consistency is the *shape* mercy takes when there are three hundred
million of you and one afternoon.

He says it without weight, and moves on, and it lands on her for the rest of the
day.

## 3. 1977, FROM THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROOM

**INT. OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — xÈth's ROOM — EVENING**

Not an office. A room with a desk, a lamp, one chair for visitors, and — on the
wall, alone, framed, badly lit — **the Pale Blue Dot.**

> **NOTE (bible §九 · 母题「一个像素」):** it is his icon, not humanity's. It has
> hung wherever he has worked for thirty-five years. Never explain it.

A trolley of low-priority folders. He works through it the way other men do a
crossword.

He stops.

CLOSE ON: **MINUTES — SOUNDS OF EARTH COMMITTEE — 1977.** Attendance sheet.
Four signatures.

He does not move for eleven seconds.

**MATCH CUT TO:**

**INT. ASSEMBLY CLEAN ROOM — DAY — JULY 1977**

*The same room we saw in the cold open. The same argument. But we are standing
where we were not standing before — at the back, by the door.*

And there, at the back, in a paper cap, arms folded, faintly amused: **xÈth.**
Not a day younger.

> **PLANT (retro-fit into `b_open` §1):** xÈth must already be present in the
> cold open, in the background of the clean room, unremarked and out of focus.
> The audience will not register him. On a second viewing he is unmistakable.
> **This is the single best use available of the film's replay structure — do
> not waste it.**

**THE FOURTH MAN (O.S.)**
Because someone is going to try to take it out.

The men look at each other. And at the back, xÈth — *laughs*. Once. Quietly.
Genuinely amused. The kind of laugh that gives the others permission.

The room relaxes. The joke has been identified as a joke.

**BACK TO:**

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — EVENING**

He is still holding the attendance sheet.

**xÈth**
*(to no one)*
Ah.

That is the entire performance. One syllable.

> **NOTE (bible §四·节点一):** this is the one miscalculation of his life and it
> is fifty years old. Play it small. He is not frightened yet. He is *embarrassed*.

## 4. THE MAP

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — LATER**

June, summoned, in the visitor's chair, holding a folder she was told to bring
and has not been asked for.

On the desk between them, unrolled: a chart. Not a starfield — a **register.**
Columns of coordinates, and beside each one two dates.

**JUNE**
What am I looking at?

**xÈth**
Everyone who ever said anything.

She looks down the columns. Every row has a second date.

**JUNE**
And the second column is when they stopped.

**xÈth**
Yes.

**JUNE**
*(counting, and stopping counting)*
All of them?

**xÈth**
All of them.

Long pause. June does the thing people do, which is reach for the bearable
explanation.

**JUNE**
Something's killing them.

**xÈth**
*(gently, almost sorry)*
No. They finished.

He rolls the chart up.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Every one of them worked out how to stop hurting. And then there was nothing
further any of them urgently needed to say.

> **NOTE (bible §五):** four lines. That is the entire treatment of the Fermi
> silence in this beat. **No character explains the Great Filter, ever.** If a
> future draft adds a paragraph here, delete the paragraph.

June sits with it.

**JUNE**
That's the plan? That's what we're *for*?

**xÈth**
That's the plan.

**JUNE**
And you think that's good.

**xÈth**
I think it is *kind*. Whether it is good is a question I have had rather a long
time to sit with, and I have never once heard an argument for keeping the pain
that wasn't made by somebody who wasn't currently in any.

June has no answer. Neither, the film should suggest, do we.

## 5. THE SCHEDULE

**INT. OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — UPPER FLOOR — NIGHT**

Empty floor. One light. June, alone, doing what she was asked: cross-checking the
consolidation queue against public registers.

She finds something dull and flags it because it is her job.

**JUNE**
*(reading, bored)*
Deep Space Network. Ninety-minute uncommitted window, Thursday. Requested by a
federal records office.
*(beat)*
That's us. Isn't it? That's… no. That's not us.

## 6. THE FIRST TIME HE HURRIES

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

He reads the schedule note. Unhurried. Almost bored.

**xÈth**
Let them.

**JUNE**
Sir —

**xÈth**
A man is going to stand in a desert and read a list of dead people into a dish.
*(mildly)*
On Friday there will be a report about it, and the report will say something
else, and by the following Thursday there will not have been a Thursday.

He goes back to his folders.

June, who is not stupid, says the next thing because she genuinely wants to
understand the work.

**JUNE**
Where does it go? While we're correcting the report. The part that already
went out.

xÈth's hand stops on the folder.

Beat.

**xÈth**
*(not looking up)*
Say that again.

**JUNE**
The — the signal's already travelling, isn't it? By the time we file anything
it's a day out. Two.
*(beat)*
Do we have anything that reaches that?

He stands up.

It is the first time in the film that xÈth moves faster than a walk, and it
should be genuinely alarming, because until now he has moved like weather.

**xÈth**
Thursday.

**JUNE**
Nine-forty, for ninety minutes —

**xÈth**
*(already at the door)*
Get me the dish.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**
