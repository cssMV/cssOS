# EMPIRE OF TIME — ENDING 1

## 「时间被守住」 · TIME IS SAVED

**Draft:** 2 · **2026-08-12** · **约 10 分钟** · 5 场

> 从第三部 §14 岔口进入。已发布设定锁定的两个结局之一
> （"In one, time is saved."）。
>
> **本结局的死规矩：不许是胜利。**
> 没有一件东西被还回来。Kellerman 还是六十一。那座山谷里还是没有镇。
> Daniel Vance 还是保险理赔员。**被守住的只有以后。**

## 1. WITHDRAWN

**INT. THE GREAT HALL — EVENING**

Empty of people. The chairs still out. The quartet's stands still up. A cleaner
at the far end, unhurried.

The **CLERK OF THE COUNCIL** waits by the lectern with his folder, because he was
told to wait.

xÈth comes in, still in his coat, with desert dust on his shoes.

**CLERK**
Sir. We've — the instrument's engrossed, it only wants the seal, I can have the
Chamberlain here in —

**xÈth**
Item four is withdrawn.

The Clerk does not understand. He waits for the rest of the sentence. There isn't
any.

**CLERK**
Withdrawn.

**xÈth**
Withdrawn.

**CLERK**
*(and he is a man who has spent forty years on procedure, and this is the only
thing he can think of to ask)*
On what grounds do I minute it?

xÈth thinks about that for a moment. It is a fair question and he wants to give
him a usable answer.

**xÈth**
*(finally)*
On the grounds that there is now a copy I can't reach.

He goes.

The Clerk stands there with the folder. Then, because he is good at his job, he
opens it and writes that down, word for word, in ink.

> **NOTE —— 帝国不是被打败的，是被【撤回】的，而撤回它的是他自己。**
>
> 理由不是良心，是**算术**：他的整个工程建立在「最后只剩一份记录」上，
> 而今天早上那个前提永久失效了。他不是认输，他是**结项**。
>
> **禁止**：不许有人欢呼；不许有音乐；不许给他任何悔意的反打。
> 那位老书记员一丝不苟地把话记下来 —— **那才是这场戏的落点。**

## 2. NOTHING IS GIVEN BACK

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — LECTURE HALL — WEEKS LATER**

Two hundred nineteen-year-olds. Ethan at the front.

**ETHAN**
Nineteen thirty-one. The mine at Kellerman. How many.

**A STUDENT**
Sixty-one.

Beat.

**ETHAN**
*(and he does not correct it, because he cannot)*
Sixty-one.
*(beat)*
Write it down. And write down that a man once told you it was eighty-four, and
that he couldn't prove it, and that he was right.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — CORRIDOR — DAY**

Clara walks out past the security desk carrying the same cardboard box she
carried out in Part Two — mug, cardigan, a photograph of nobody in particular.

**BREWER** catches up with her, wretched, holding a letter.

**BREWER**
There's a review. It could be six months. Clara, it could go either way —

**CLARA**
*(kindly, and she keeps walking)*
Bill. My name's in a log book in a desert.
*(beat)*
It's going to go the way it goes.

**EXT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — STEPS — CONTINUOUS**

She comes out into ordinary daylight, and stops on the step, and looks up at the
weather.

> **NOTE —— 台阶上抬头看天。** `mars` 线的观众在第二部见过一模一样的一格
> （Audrey Leigh 走出记忆办公室）。**同一个机位、同一个动作、
> 完全相反的原因。**
>
> 从 `earth` 线进来的观众没见过那一格 —— 不要紧。**这里不需要它。**

## 3. THE FOURTEENTH, AGAIN

**EXT. A CITY SQUARE — MIDDAY — THE FOURTEENTH**

The siren. The country stops. The same thousands of faces, the same grief,
the same nobody knowing why.

It has not changed. It is not going to change.

Ethan and Clara stand in it.

**CLARA**
*(without turning)*
It's still happening.

**ETHAN**
Yes.

**CLARA**
Then what did we do?

**ETHAN**
*(and this is the thesis of the ending)*
We didn't get anything back.
*(beat)*
We stopped him getting anything more.

The siren again. The square empties around them.

> **NOTE —— 第一部埋、`earth` 第二部兑现、本结局【第三次】使用的同一场戏。**
> 三次的画面完全相同。**变的只有观众知道什么。**

## 4. RETAIL

**INT. A PUBLIC COUNTER — A SMALL TOWN — DAY**

Not the Office of Remembrance. A room in a municipal building with six chairs, a
radiator, and a hatch.

Behind the hatch, in a cardigan, with a pen and a ledger and no security anywhere
in the building: **xÈth.**

An OLD MAN is sitting across from him with a plastic wallet of papers.

**OLD MAN**
—and the pension people say one date and the hospital says another and I've been
at this two years —

**xÈth**
*(reading, unhurried, and he has all afternoon)*
Nineteen sixty-eight.

**OLD MAN**
That's what I keep telling them.

**xÈth**
*(writing it, and signing it, and turning it round)*
Then that's what it says. It's signed, and it's the only one now.

The old man looks at it for a long time. Then he folds it into his coat like
money, and shakes xÈth's hand, and goes.

xÈth watches him out. Then he pulls the next folder toward him.

Somewhere behind him, out of focus, on the wall, badly lit: **a small framed
picture we have seen before.**

> **NOTE —— 结局一唯一一次也是最后一次见到他，而他在做一件好事。**
>
> 帝国被撤回了。**整批处理的那个版本死了。零售的那个版本没有。**
> 他还有一百四十年，还有一支笔，还有一个接一个的人。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许**把这一场拍成流放、惩罚、或落魄。这不是降职 —— 这是他喜欢的工作。
> - 那位老先生**必须真的被帮到**，而且必须走得比进来时轻。
> - 挂画**不许**给特写，**不许**对焦。它就在那儿。
> - **不许有台词提到过去。不许有音乐。**

## 5. NINETY SECONDS

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — EARLY MORNING — MONTHS LATER**

Grey light. The kettle not yet on.

Ethan takes the battered spiral notebook out of the drawer with the batteries and
the string, and opens it, and reads three lines.

He picks up his phone and looks up three things.

The first one matches. Tick.

The second one matches. Tick.

He looks at the third one.

It matches.

He makes the third tick.

He looks at the page for a moment — three ticks, no crossings-out, for the first
time in twenty years.

Then he closes the notebook and puts it back in the drawer, and puts the kettle
on, and it is a completely ordinary morning, and it is going to go on being one.

**CLARA (O.S.)**
Anything?

**ETHAN**
*(getting cups down)*
No.

> **NOTE —— 全片最小的一个胜利镜头，而这是唯一一种这部片能给出的胜利。**
>
> **禁止**：不许配乐；不许特写那三个勾；不许有人笑；不许有人说「我们赢了」。
> **他只是把水壶放上去。**
