## 1. YEAR ONE, DAY ONE

**EXT. A CITY — MORNING — 1 JANUARY, YEAR ONE**

Frost. Sun. A city waking up.

No parades. No banners. No portraits on buildings. A man scrapes ice off a
windscreen. A bakery opens. Two dogs meet on a corner and are pleased about it.

On a newsstand, the front pages are about football and the weather.

> **NOTE —— 帝国的第一天长这样。** 不许有一面旗。

---

## 2. THE THING THAT WAS DISCONTINUED

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — CORRIDOR — MONTHS LATER**

Ethan walks past the noticeboard where the Day of Observance notice used to hang.

In its place, among a hundred others:

> **NOTICE — THE DAY OF OBSERVANCE (14th) IS DISCONTINUED FROM THIS YEAR,
> THERE BEING NOTHING FURTHER TO OBSERVE.**

He reads it. He reads it the way you read a notice about parking.

Then he walks on.

> **NOTE —— 全片最恐怖的一张纸，而 Ethan 看它的表情和看停车通知一样。**
>
> **禁止**：不许他停下超过两秒；不许推镜；不许配乐；不许有反打。
> **观众要独自承受这一格。**

---

## 3. A GOOD YEAR

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — LECTURE HALL — DAY**

Ethan teaching, and he is **good** — better than we have ever seen him, loose,
funny, unhurried. Two hundred students actually listening.

**ETHAN**
—so the number matters. It always matters.
*(beat)*
Nineteen thirty-one. The mine at Kellerman. How many.

**A STUDENT**
Sixty-one.

**ETHAN**
Sixty-one. And it was in every paper in the country for a week, and the town
never got over it, and there's a memorial you can go and stand at.
*(beat)*
Right. Chapter nine.

He turns to the board.

**Nothing moves. Nothing is going to move. He has not felt the floor shift in
eleven months and he does not know that this is unusual, because he does not
remember that it used to.**

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — MORNING**

Clara and Ethan, breakfast, an ordinary weekday, an ordinary argument about
whose turn it is to ring the plumber.

They are **happy.** Visibly, uncomplicatedly happy, in a way we have not seen
them for two hours.

---

## 4. THE FLOOR

**INT. THE FLOOR — GALLERY — DAY**

The hall the size of a stadium, and it is still full of desks, and they are still
working.

But the ranks at the near end are **empty** now — a third of them, chairs pushed
in, lamps off.

**JUNE**, older, with a lanyard she has had a long time, comes along the gallery
with a folder and stops beside a colleague at the rail.

**COLLEAGUE**
Three floors down to two next quarter.

**JUNE**
Already?

**COLLEAGUE**
*(nodding at the hall, contentedly)*
There's just less of it. Every year there's less of it.
*(beat)*
That's the job, isn't it. Doing yourself out of one.

June looks out over the hall.

**JUNE**
*(and she means it entirely)*
Yes.

> **NOTE —— 那个大厅正在【清空】，而它是这个结局最有力的论据。**
> 工作量在减少，因为痛苦在减少。**这不是反讽。这是真的。**

---

## 5. THE SAME PLATFORM

**INT. A COMMUTER STATION — PLATFORM — EVENING**

Ethan stands with a paper cup among sixty other people, going home.

Somebody comes and stands beside him. An overcoat. No security anywhere.

**xÈth**
Vance.

**ETHAN**
*(genuinely pleased, the way you are when you run into someone you like)*
Oh — hello.

They stand together, looking at the departure board, the way two people do.

**xÈth**
They've moved the library.

**ETHAN**
*(laughing)*
Nineteen years ago.

**xÈth**
Nineteen years!
*(shaking his head at himself)*
I'm still walking to the old one.

A train comes in on the far platform and goes again. Nobody looks at it.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
How's Clara?

**ETHAN**
She's well. She's very well. She's taken up the — the thing with the boat.

**xÈth**
The rowing.

**ETHAN**
The rowing.

**xÈth**
*(with real warmth)*
Good.

Their train comes in. They get on it together, still talking, and the doors close,
and it goes.

> **NOTE —— 与 `earth` 线第二部 §7 的镜像，而它是这个结局的最后一记。**
>
> 那一场里，两个人隔着四条轨道看着彼此，其中一个点了一下头，然后火车开过来
> 把他们隔开。**这一场里他们站在同一个站台上，聊图书馆，一起上车。**
>
> 走 `earth` 线的观众会在这一秒明白发生了什么。走 `mars` 线的观众不会 ——
> **他们只会看见两个老同学在下班路上碰见，而且都很高兴。**
> **两种体验都是对的，而且第二种更可怕。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 两个人都必须**真心高兴**。不许有一丝勉强、试探、或言外之意。
> - **不许**让 Ethan 有任何一闪而过的疑惑。他没有疑惑。他没有可以拿来疑惑的
>   东西。
> - **不许配乐。** 站台广播、人群、车进站。

## 6. THE DRAWER

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — EVENING**

Ethan looking for a fuse. He opens the drawer with the batteries and the string.

Under them: a battered spiral notebook, soft at the corners.

He takes it out. He does not recognise it, and then he does — his own
handwriting, twenty years of it, hundreds of lines, most of them crossed out and
rewritten.

He turns pages, mildly baffled, half-smiling at himself.

He stops on one.

> **INSERT — THE NOTEBOOK**
>
> *Charlotte. Born in April.*

He looks at it for a while.

**CLARA (O.S.)**
Did you find it?

**ETHAN**
*(still looking at the page)*
…Yeah.

He turns two more pages, finds nothing that means anything, and closes it, and
puts it back in the drawer under the batteries and the string, and shuts the
drawer.

And goes to fix the fuse.

> **NOTE —— 结局二的最后一击，而它是一个男人合上一本自己的旧笔记本。**
>
> **禁止**：不许有音乐进；不许特写他的眼睛；不许让他皱眉；
> 不许让他再打开一次。**他一点也不难过。他只是不知道那是什么。**
>
> **这是全片对「他给的止痛是真的」这条铁律最后一次、也是最狠的一次兑现。**
