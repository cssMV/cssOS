## 1. THE MASTER

**INT. A SMALL PRINT SHOP — THREE DAYS EARLIER — DAY**

Ordinary. Loud. A woman in an apron running a duplicator.

A courier drops a thin envelope in the tray: routine corrections, the sort every
print shop gets, from an office that issues them by the thousand.

She opens it, glances, and does what she has done ten thousand times: she pulls
the master sheet, lays the correction beside it, and retypes one line.

She does not read it. There is no reason to read it.

She runs eleven thousand copies.

> **NOTE —— 全片最重要的一场戏由一个从不出场的配角完成，
> 而她做的是一件完全正确的事。**
>
> **禁止**：不许给她任何反打；不许让她犹豫；不许有音乐；
> 不许让观众此刻明白这是什么。**这一场必须无聊。**

## 4. THE CORRECTION SLIP

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — NIGHT**

He is clearing the trolley of low-priority folders for the last time. On the wall,
alone, badly lit: the Pale Blue Dot.

He works fast, the way a man does when he has stopped caring about the outcome.

And then his hand goes still on a folder.

CLOSE ON: a routine schedule of corrections issued three days ago. Ten thousand
lines. His initials at the bottom of every page.

And on page four, line one hundred and six, an entry about a print job for a
private club magazine.

He reads it.

He reads it again.

Then he sits back in the chair and puts one hand over his mouth and stays like
that for a long time, and what is on his face is not triumph and it is not relief.

**It is horror.**

**xÈth**
*(to no one, and it is the last thing he says in this ending)*
…Ah.

> **NOTE —— 全片第三次、也是最后一次那个音节。**
>
> 冷岛那次，他白赢了一场。1977 那次，他发现自己白输了一场。
> **这一次，他发现自己赢了，而且他宁愿没有。**
>
> **禁止**：不许有音乐；不许有反打；不许让他做任何事。
> 他就那样坐着，然后画面切走。

## 5. AND THEN THERE WAS NOTHING FURTHER

**MONTAGE — YEARS**

The Empire works. It works very well.

**A hospital corridor**, quiet, people sitting comfortably.
**A courtroom**, half empty, an usher reading a paperback.
**A city square on the fourteenth of the month** — traffic going through it,
nobody stopping, because there is nothing to stop for.
**The floor**, three-quarters of the desks dark.
**A public counter**, one applicant, no queue, flowers nobody has taken away.

And the last of them: **a radio telescope, in the rain, pointed at nothing, with
grass growing through the service road.**

Nobody is unhappy. Nobody is transmitting. There is nothing further that anybody
urgently needs to say.
