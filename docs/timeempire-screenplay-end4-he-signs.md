# EMPIRE OF TIME — ENDING 4

## 「他签下自己的名字」 · HE SIGNS HIS OWN NAME

**Draft:** 1 · **2026-08-12** · **约 14 分钟**

> 从第三部 §14 岔口进入。
> **五个结局里唯一一个有人改变的。**
>
> 核心设计：他对自己执行了他对 Holbrook 执行过的那条规矩。
> 第二部 `mars` §3 他拒绝了一个人，理由是
> 「一个正确地背着它的人是我唯一帮不了的 —— 伤你的不是记忆，
> 是你对自己的判断是对的，而那个我不拿」。
>
> **这个结局里，他把那句话用在自己身上。**
>
> **走 `earth` 线的观众没看过 Holbrook 那一场** —— 不要紧。
> 这个结局里他会把那条规矩当场说出来，而且他是说给自己听的。

## 1. NINE METRES

**EXT. THE DESERT — GATE — LATE AFTERNOON**

The two men, nine metres apart. Clara at the car. June a few steps behind him.

xÈth does not go to the car.

He crosses the nine metres.

**xÈth**
I want the microphone.

**ETHAN**
The window closed at eleven-ten.

**xÈth**
Then open it again.

**ETHAN**
It isn't mine to open. There's a duty officer at the gate and a name in a log
book and it isn't mine.

xÈth turns and looks at Clara.

**CLARA**
*(evenly, across the yard)*
The name in the log book is mine.

Beat.

**xÈth**
Mrs Sinclair. I am asking you for ninety seconds of a dish.

**CLARA**
*(and she does not move)*
Why?

**xÈth**
*(and he answers her honestly, because he almost always does)*
Because there is one entry missing from his list.

---

## 2. THE RULE, APPLIED

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — DUSK**

The four of them. Merritt in the doorway with the log book under his arm, having
decided, on no authority whatsoever, to allow this.

xÈth sits down at the desk. He looks at the microphone for a while without
touching it.

**JUNE**
Sir. You don't have to.

**xÈth**
No.
*(beat)*
That's rather the difficulty.

He turns in the chair and speaks to her, and to nobody else, because she is the
only person in the room who has earned it.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
There was a man in a room with me. Nineteen years he'd been carrying something,
and he asked me to take it, and I refused him.
*(beat)*
And I told him why. I said: what's hurting you isn't the memory. It's that you
are **right about yourself.** And I don't take that. I've never taken that.

**JUNE**
*(quietly)*
I was there.

**xÈth**
I know.
*(beat)*
I have been right about myself since about four o'clock this morning.

Silence.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
For one hundred and forty years there has been no true account of me anywhere,
and so there has been nothing to be right or wrong about. I was not a good man
and I was not a bad man. **I was a procedure.**
*(beat)*
And this afternoon a man in this room made one copy of everything, and left me
out of it, and the moment I understood that, I understood that I could still
be left out of it **for ever.**

**ETHAN**
And you don't want that.

**xÈth**
*(and he looks up, and it is the only time in the film he is frightened)*
No. I find I don't.

## 3. ONE ENTRY

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Clara closes the breaker with her hand. It throws with a sound like a door bolt.

Something enormous outside begins, very slowly, to move.

xÈth pulls the microphone toward him. He does not make a speech.

**xÈth**
My name is xÈth. I am not from here.
*(beat)*
I have done this eleven times.

And he begins to read, from memory, without notes, in a flat administrative voice
— names, places, dates, worlds — the way a man reads a list he has known by heart
for a very long time and has never once said out loud.

**MONTAGE — THE LAST NINETY MINUTES**

We do not hear most of it. We see:

**The room**, and the four of them, and the light going.

**Merritt** at the gate, sitting on the bonnet of his car in the cold, listening
to the dish, not going home.

**June** standing against the wall with a clipboard she is finally writing on —
one line, then another, then another. Nobody asked her to. She is minuting it.

**Ethan** at the back, not looking at him, because it turns out that this is not
a thing you look at.

**Clara** holding the sheet steady for a man she has no reason on earth to help.

And outside, the dish, and above it the sky going from orange to green to black.

> **NOTE —— 全片最长的一个段落，而观众听不清内容。**
>
> 我们要的不是那份名单的内容 —— 第三部已经给过一次完整的名单了。
> 我们要的是**一个从不解释自己的人，解释了九十分钟**，
> 而且屋里没有一个人打断他。
>
> **禁止**：不许配乐；不许闪回；不许剪进任何一个他做过的事的画面。
> **只有一个人在说话，和四个在听的人。**

## 4. NOW WHAT AM I

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — NIGHT**

Finished. He opens the breaker himself.

The dish comes to rest.

Nobody in the room says anything for a while.

**xÈth**
*(finally, and he sounds exactly like a man at the end of a long day's work)*
Well.

He stands and picks his coat up off the back of the chair.

**ETHAN**
Nothing's going to happen to you.

**xÈth**
No.

**ETHAN**
There's no court. There's no record of it here. Tomorrow you're still —

**xÈth**
Tomorrow I'm still the President, and the instrument is still engrossed, and the
Clerk is still waiting with the seal.
*(beat)*
I know.

**ETHAN**
Then what was that?

xÈth thinks about it. He wants to get it right.

**xÈth**
This morning you asked me what I thought happened to me now, and I said I didn't
know, and that was true.
*(beat)*
It isn't any more.
*(beat)*
In about nine hundred years, somebody is going to hear a man admit what he did.
And they will be able to disagree with him.
*(and this is the last line he speaks in this ending)*
Nobody has been able to disagree with me since eighteen ninety-four. I had
forgotten it was even **possible.**

He puts his coat on and goes out.

June looks at Ethan. Then she picks up her clipboard, and follows him, because
that is what she decided a long time ago and nothing tonight has changed it.

---

## 5. THE MORNING AFTER

**INT. THE GREAT HALL — MORNING**

The chairs are out. The quartet is tuning. Four hundred people in good coats.

The Clerk at the lectern, folder open, waiting.

The chair on the dais is empty.

And then it isn't.

He comes in and sits down in it, and nods to the Clerk, and the Clerk begins to
read item four.

> **NOTE —— 这个结局【不给赦免】。**
>
> 他签了自己的名字，而且他还是要加冕。**两件事都是真的，而且互不抵消。**
>
> **禁止**：不许有人阻止典礼；不许有反转；不许有人在最后一秒站起来。
> **唯一变了的东西，正在以光速离开这颗星球，而屋里没有一个人知道。**
