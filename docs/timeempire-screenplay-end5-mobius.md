## 4. SEVEN YEARS

**TITLE CARD:**

> **SEVEN YEARS LATER**

**EXT. A SMALL CIVIC BUILDING — MORNING**

A converted library on an ordinary street. Good bones. Someone has put a bench
outside it.

A brass plate by the door, small and unshowy:

> **OFFICE OF THE RECONCILED RECORD**

A queue of six. A woman with a folder. A man with a boy. Nobody looks frightened.

---

## 5. THE WORK

**INT. THE OFFICE OF THE RECONCILED RECORD — PUBLIC HALL — CONTINUOUS**

High windows. A tiled floor. Light coming down in slabs.

Somebody has put flowers on the counter and nobody has taken them away.

A YOUNG CLERK (20s, sleeves pushed up, a pen behind her ear) slides a single
sheet across to an OLD WOMAN.

**YOUNG CLERK**
That's the settled figure. It's signed, and it's the only one now.

**OLD WOMAN**
*(reading it a long time)*
The plaque says one thing. My brother said another.

**YOUNG CLERK**
I know.
*(beat)*
There's no form. I'll write it up after.

She comes round the counter, which is against procedure, and sits down in the
chair next to her, which is also against procedure.

> **NOTE —— 逐句、逐动作**沿用第二部 `mars` §1 与 §2 的开场。
> 走 `mars` 线的观众会在这一秒僵住。
> 走 `earth` 线的观众不会 —— **他们会在第 6 场僵住。**
> 两种都对。

## 6. THE BACK OFFICE

**INT. THE OFFICE OF THE RECONCILED RECORD — INNER ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Not an office. A room with a desk, a green-shaded lamp, one chair for visitors, a
trolley of low-priority folders, four white walls.

And on one of them, alone, framed, badly lit: **the Pale Blue Dot.**

**ETHAN** — seven years older, grey all through now — works through the trolley
the way other men do a crossword.

An AIDE puts a folder down in front of him.

**AIDE**
The Ardenne valley. Third submission.

**ETHAN**
*(not looking up)*
The town or the number?

**AIDE**
The town.

**ETHAN**
*(reading, and initialling, and moving it to a different pile)*
Then it's the town. Settle it and tell them it's the only one now.

The aide goes.

Ethan works.

After a moment he stops, and looks up at the picture on the wall for a second or
two, the way you look at something you have looked at every working day for
years, and then goes back to the folders.

## 7. THE OTHER SIDE OF THE STRIP

**INT. THE OFFICE OF THE RECONCILED RECORD — INNER ROOM — LATER**

**CLARA** comes in with two coffees and puts one down beside him. She looks well.
They are fine. This is a good life.

**CLARA**
The Chamberlain's people rang again.

**ETHAN**
*(still working)*
About the succession clause.

**CLARA**
About the succession clause.

**ETHAN**
Tell them the same as last time. If there's going to be one office that decides
which account is true, there has to be somebody in it who remembers what it cost
to build it.
*(beat)*
And there has to be a rule about who comes after.

**CLARA**
That's what they're asking. Who comes after.

Ethan puts the pen down and rubs his eyes and thinks about it properly, because
it is a fair question and he wants to give a usable answer.

**ETHAN**
*(finally)*
Determined by the holder.

Clara nods and writes it on a pad and takes it out.

Ethan sits alone in a room with a green lamp and a trolley of folders and one
picture on the wall.

Then he pulls the next folder toward him, and opens it, and gets on with the
work, because the work is good and it is necessary and somebody has to do it.

> **NOTE —— 「Determined by the holder.」**
>
> 那是第三部加冕**第二遍**里书记员念出的那一句 —— 观众听过它，
> 并且看着一屋子人礼貌地点头通过了它。
>
> **禁止**：不许有人指出这一点；不许有反打；不许有音乐；
> 不许 Ethan 停顿超过一拍。**他不知道。他真的不知道。**
>
> **这就是莫比乌斯：他走了一整圈，回到了另一面，
> 而他从未跨过任何一条边。**
>
> **电影不证实它。** 也许这只是一个必要的机构和一个疲惫的好人。
> 也许 xÈth 一百四十年前也是这样开始的。**永远不说是哪一种。**
