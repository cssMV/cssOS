## 1. NINETY SECONDS, EVERY MORNING

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — EARLY MORNING**

Grey light. A kettle not yet on. **ETHAN**, dressed for work, stands at the
counter with a battered spiral notebook open in front of him.

He does not read the whole thing. He turns to a page near the front — the paper
has gone soft from being turned — and reads three lines. His lips move.

Then he picks up his phone and looks up three things.

The first one matches. He makes a small pencil tick.

The second one matches. Tick.

He looks at the third one for slightly longer than the other two.

Then he crosses out what is written in the notebook, and writes the new thing
underneath it, and dates it.

He closes the notebook, puts it in the drawer with the batteries and the string,
and puts the kettle on.

The whole thing has taken ninety seconds. **He has done it every morning for
twenty years.**

**CLARA (O.S.)**
Anything?

**ETHAN**
*(getting cups down)*
Charlotte.

**CLARA**
*(coming in, doing her earring)*
What about her.

**ETHAN**
She was born in April.

**CLARA**
She was born in April.

**ETHAN**
She's always been born in April.

Beat. Clara stops doing her earring.

**CLARA**
Whose Charlotte?

**ETHAN**
*(and this is the whole marriage in one line)*
Exactly.

He hands her a cup. She takes it. Neither of them says anything else about it,
because there is nothing else to say about it, and they have both known that for
a long time.

> **NOTE —— 全片对「代价」的唯一一次正面处理，而且它必须是家常的。**
> 不许闪回、不许崩溃、不许有人解释这是什么。观众只需要看见：
> **一个人每天早上花九十秒确认世界还是不是昨天那个**，而且这已经是他生活里
> 最普通的一项家务。
>
> 「Charlotte」是谁，**全片不解释**。她不再存在，Clara 想不起来她是谁，
> 而 Ethan 记得她是四月生的。这一格就够了。
>
> **NOTE (铁律 5):** 划掉与重写那一下必须 i2v —— 铅笔在纸上动。

## 2. THE OATH

**EXT. THE CAPITOL — INAUGURATION DAY — MORNING**

Cold, bright, enormous. A crowd that goes back further than the frame.

**xÈth**, bare-headed in the cold, puts his hand down and takes the oath. He says
it perfectly. He does not stumble on a syllable of it.

**xÈth**
—that I will faithfully execute the Office of President of the United States,
and will to the best of my ability **preserve, protect and defend** the
Constitution of the United States.

Applause like weather.

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — FACULTY LOUNGE — CONTINUOUS**

A dozen people watching it on a wall screen with coffee. **ETHAN** at the back,
not sitting down.

On screen, the network's caption crawls under the picture. It reads:

> …WILL TO THE BEST OF MY ABILITY **PRESERVE, PROTECT AND CORRECT** THE
> CONSTITUTION…

Ethan's head comes up.

He looks around the room. Nobody else has moved. The caption is already gone.

**ETHAN**
*(to the man beside him, carefully)*
What did he say? The last part.

**COLLEAGUE**
*(not looking away from the screen)*
Preserve, protect and defend.

**ETHAN**
Right.

> **NOTE —— 全片第一次改写，故意做得极小、极便宜、而且无害。**
> 观众可能没看清，可能以为自己看错了。**这正是目的。** 我们在这里教的不是
> 「他很邪恶」，是「**这件事发生时的样子就是这么小**」。
>
> **禁止**：不许配音效强调；不许推镜到字幕；不许让 Ethan 说出他看到了什么。
> 字幕在画面上停留不超过一秒。

## 3. A HISTORY LECTURE, INTERRUPTED

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — LECTURE HALL — DAY**

Two hundred nineteen-year-olds. Ethan down at the front, no notes, walking.

**ETHAN**
—so the number matters. It always matters. Whenever anybody tells you a number
went **down**, ask who counted it the second time.
*(beat)*
Nineteen thirty-one. The mine at Kellerman. How many.

**A STUDENT**
Eighty-four.

**ETHAN**
Eighty-four. It was in every paper in the country for a week. Now —

And then the floor moves.

It does not move. Nothing moves. But Ethan stops in the middle of a word, one
hand slightly out, like a man who has just heard a door close somewhere behind
him in an empty house.

He turns and looks at the screen behind him.

The slide says **SIXTY-ONE**.

He turns back to two hundred faces, all of whom are writing down *sixty-one*.

**ETHAN**
*(and his voice is completely normal)*
Sixty-one. Which was in every paper in the country for a week.

He keeps teaching. He teaches for another eleven minutes and he does not get
anything else wrong.

> **NOTE (铁律 5 —— 必须 i2v):** 全片最重要的一个「变化本身」。
> 静帧拍不了这场戏：**恐怖在于八十四变成六十一的那一瞬间，以及两百支笔
> 毫无迟疑地写下新数字。** 一定要有笔在动。
>
> **NOTE —— 表演铁律：他必须继续讲课。** 这个人独自扛着这件事已经很多年了，
> 他早就有了一整套应对流程。惊恐、颤抖、摔粉笔 —— 全错。**他只是接着讲。**

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — CORRIDOR — LATER**

Ethan walks out. On the wall, a printed notice among a hundred others:

> **UNIVERSITY CLOSED — TUESDAY 14th — DAY OF OBSERVANCE**

He stops. Reads it. Reads it again.

**ETHAN**
*(to a passing student, lightly)*
Hey — the fourteenth. What is that, do you know?

**STUDENT**
*(shrugging, friendly, already walking)*
Day of Observance.

**ETHAN**
Right, but for what?

The student turns round, walking backwards, genuinely trying to answer, and
finding nothing there.

**STUDENT**
…Huh.

She shrugs and goes.

> **PLANT —— 这是第一部最重要的一个伏笔，第 10 场兑现。**
> 全国性的默哀日，没有一个人说得出是为了什么。埋的时候要**完全随手**，
> 不给任何强调，不给音乐，不给反打。

## 4. THE CUSTODIAN

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — SUB-LEVEL — DAY**

Cold. Dim. The hum of chillers. Shelf after shelf of **film cans, magnetic reels,
glass plates, bound ledgers** — everything that is a *shape* rather than a number.

**CLARA SINCLAIR** walks a trolley down an aisle with the unhurried competence of
someone in her own house.

**ETHAN**
*(following)*
It happened again. During the lecture.

**CLARA**
Which one.

**ETHAN**
Kellerman. Eighty-four to sixty-one.

**CLARA**
*(not stopping)*
And nobody.

**ETHAN**
Nobody.

She stops at a shelf, pulls a flat grey box, opens it. Inside: a contact sheet.
Black and white. Men at a pithead in 1931. Rows of covered shapes on the ground.

She hands it to him. He counts. He counts again.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Eighty-four.

**CLARA**
Eighty-four.
*(she takes it back, and boxes it, and shelves it)*
That's why I'm down here and not up there.

**ETHAN**
Clara —

**CLARA**
I took an oath. It's four sentences long and one of them says *in the condition
received.*
*(beat)*
Everybody upstairs thinks that's about humidity.

> **NOTE (圣经 §2.3 —— 模拟形状不可改写):** 这场戏是全片的军械库介绍。
> **不要让任何人解释这条规则。** 让 Clara 拿出一张照片、数一遍、放回去。
> 观众自己会明白：他改得了数字，改不了形状。

She moves on. Ethan doesn't.

**ETHAN**
He asked us to come and see him.

**CLARA**
*(a beat, back still turned)*
I know. It's on my calendar. **He** put it on my calendar.

## 5. MARGOT

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — DEPARTMENT OFFICE — DAY**

Late afternoon, low sun, two desks, a kettle. **MARGOT** (60s) is at hers with a
mug, reading glasses up on her head, doing the crossword she does every day.

Ethan comes in and sits on the edge of the other desk and asks her something he
has clearly asked before.

**ETHAN**
Margot. The '09 flood. How many died?

**MARGOT**
*(not looking up)*
Two hundred and eleven.

**ETHAN**
You're sure.

**MARGOT**
Ethan, I was **there.** I was standing on the ridge road with your mother
watching the water come up the valley. Two hundred and eleven.
*(beat, and now she does look up, fond and exasperated)*
You ask me that about once a month. Why do you ask me that about once a month?

**ETHAN**
*(getting up)*
Because you always know.

He goes to the kettle. His back is turned. Nothing happens.

Nothing happens **at all** — no sound, no light change, no camera move.

**MARGOT**
*(behind him, mildly, going back to her crossword)*
Then it's a hundred and forty-nine, love.
*(beat)*
And I misremembered.

Ethan stands with the kettle in his hand.

He does not turn round for a long moment. When he does, his face is completely
normal.

**ETHAN**
Tea?

**MARGOT**
Please.

> **NOTE —— 全片最恐怖的一下，而且它必须拍得毫无戏剧性。**
> 已拍素材 `S2-3` / `S2-4`（`docs/timeempire-plates/b_open-coldopen/`）可直接沿用。
>
> **铁律 5 —— `S2-4` 必须是 i2v。** 恐怖在于**转变本身**：她说完前一句到说出
> 后一句之间，脸上什么都没变。静帧拍不了这个。
>
> **禁止**：不许有音乐进；不许推镜；不许让她露出困惑。**她一点都不困惑 ——
> 她只是在纠正一个笔误。**

## 6. THE COUNTRY, AND THE WOMAN WHO CAME TO ASK

**EXT. A STREET — MIDDAY**

An ordinary town in an ordinary year. Not dystopian. Nothing is broken, nobody is
frightened, the coffee is fine.

At a bus stop, TWO WOMEN in their fifties, mid-conversation.

**WOMAN A**
—no, because they closed it the year my mother went in.

**WOMAN B**
It never closed.

**WOMAN A**
*(entirely certain)*
It closed for two years.

**WOMAN B**
*(equally certain)*
Sandra. It has never once closed.

Beat. Both of them look at the road.

**WOMAN A**
*(shrugging, and it is a shrug we will see all over this country)*
Huh.

The bus comes. They get on it and talk about something else.

> **NOTE —— 近未来美国的质感，用二十秒说完。**
> **禁止**：不许有无人机、不许有全息广告、不许有士兵、不许有宵禁。这个国家
> **一点问题都没有**，除了没有人能确定任何事情，而且大家都已经习惯了。
> 那个耸肩是全片的国民表情。

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — ETHAN'S OFFICE — AFTERNOON**

Books, papers, a window. A knock, and **JUNE HALE** (30s) is already halfway in,
because she has forty minutes and three more people to see.

**JUNE**
Professor Vance? June Hale, from the President's office. I know. I'm sorry.
I've been apologising all day.

**ETHAN**
*(standing)*
For what?

**JUNE**
For being from the President's office.
*(she puts a folder down and does not sit)*
Thursday. The old university, the quadrangle, twenty minutes, no press, no
photographs. He asked for you by name and he asked me to say that if you say no
that is a complete answer and I'm not to come back a second time.

**ETHAN**
*(a beat)*
He said that.

**JUNE**
He wrote it out for me. He was quite firm about it.
*(she almost smiles)*
He's firm about odd things.

**ETHAN**
And your wife —
*(catching himself)*
Sorry. **My** wife.

**JUNE**
Mrs Sinclair's invited too. I sent hers first.
*(beat, and now she does sit, on the arm of the chair, for eight seconds)*
Can I say something out of turn? I've had this job eleven days. Everybody told
me what he'd be like.
*(beat)*
He's not like that.

**ETHAN**
No. He isn't.

She stands, collects the folder, and is at the door.

**JUNE**
Thursday. Nine-forty.
*(and she means it kindly)*
Bring a coat, it's a long lawn.

She goes.

> **PLANT —— June Hale。第二部 `mars` 线的视点人物。**
> 她在第一部**只出现这一次**，而且她必须让观众喜欢她。她高效、诚实、
> 有点累、对老板有真实的好感 —— 而那份好感是**有根据的**。
>
> **禁止**：不许把她写成走狗；不许让她说任何暗示阴谋的话；不许给她
> 「若有所思」的反打。她只是一个把工作做好的人。

## 7. THE OLD SCHOOL

**INT. A CAR — PARKED OUTSIDE THE OLD UNIVERSITY — MORNING**

Engine off. Ethan and Clara, not getting out. Through the windscreen, a long
brick frontage and a lawn with frost still on the shaded half.

**CLARA**
What do I call him.

**ETHAN**
Whatever you want. He genuinely won't mind.

**CLARA**
That's worse.
*(beat)*
Ethan. What was he like. Then.

**ETHAN**
*(and he has to think about it, which is itself an answer)*
Kind.

**CLARA**
Don't.

**ETHAN**
I'm not being clever. He was the kindest man in that year. He sat up three nights
with a boy in our hall who'd had bad news and none of the rest of us went near it.
*(beat)*
I've had thirty years to find the moment where he turned into something else and
I have never found it. **There isn't one.** He's been doing the same thing the
whole time.

**CLARA**
Then what changed?

**ETHAN**
He got the country.

She looks at the lawn a moment longer.

**CLARA**
*(unbuckling)*
Right.

**EXT. THE OLD UNIVERSITY — QUADRANGLE — DAY**

Brick, elms, a long lawn. Motorcade discreetly parked on the road outside;
two agents at fifty metres and no closer.

**xÈth** walks the quad with **Ethan** and **Clara**, in an overcoat, hands behind
his back, entirely at ease. He is showing them around a place all three of them
already know.

**xÈth**
They've moved the library.

**ETHAN**
Nineteen years ago.

**xÈth**
*(delighted)*
Nineteen years! And I'm still walking to the old one.

They pass students who do double-takes and then decide they must be wrong.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(to Clara, and it is not small talk)*
He'll have told you what I am.

**CLARA**
He has.

**xÈth**
And what he is.

**CLARA**
*(evenly)*
He's my husband.

**xÈth**
*(a real laugh)*
Yes. Good.

They walk.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Ethan. On this lawn, in the spring of the year we were both twenty-two, you
argued for two hours that a fact is a thing that happened, and I argued that a
fact is a thing that is **written down**, and neither of us moved an inch, and
then it rained.
*(beat)*
I'm going to make my version true. Not win the argument — **make it true.**
And I'd like you to come and work on it.

**ETHAN**
On what, exactly.

**xÈth**
On the largest project in the history of your species. There are four hundred
million people in this country and every one of them is carrying something that
happened to them, or to their parents, or to some strangers a hundred years
before they were born, and **not one of those things can be undone.**
*(he stops walking)*
I can undo them.

**CLARA**
You can **hide** them.

**xÈth**
*(turning to her, and this is the crux, and he is not remotely angry)*
Mrs Sinclair — what is the difference, to the person carrying it?

Silence on the lawn.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
I'm not being clever. It's a real question and I have never once been given an
answer to it by somebody who was, at that moment, in any pain.
*(gently)*
Every argument for keeping the wound has been made by people who weren't
bleeding. Show me one that wasn't.

Clara opens her mouth. Nothing comes.

**ETHAN**
*(quietly)*
Kellerman.

**xÈth**
Eighty-four men.

**ETHAN**
*(he looks up)*
Sixty-one.

**xÈth**
*(after a beat, and with something almost like respect)*
Ah. You saw that.

**ETHAN**
Twenty-three men, and their families, and everyone who ever grieved for them.

**xÈth**
No. **Twenty-three deaths.** The men were never in there — they went home, they
had lives, they died in their beds at eighty.
*(beat)*
You're mourning people I **saved.**

Ethan has no immediate answer. **The film should not give him one.**

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(to Ethan, unhurried)*
Ask me the other one. The one you've been carrying since the lounge.

**ETHAN**
Where does it go.

**xÈth**
Where does what go.

**ETHAN**
The twenty-three. You didn't put them somewhere else, you took them **out.**
So where does it go — the fact. It existed. Where is it now.

**xÈth**
*(and for the first time he takes a moment)*
Nowhere. It's the only genuinely clean thing I do.

**ETHAN**
That's not an answer, that's an assertion.

**xÈth**
*(pleased)*
Yes. It's my department.

**CLARA**
And when you get it wrong?

**xÈth**
I don't.

**CLARA**
Ever?

xÈth stops walking. It is a very small pause and both of them see it.

**xÈth**
Once.
*(and then he walks on, and the subject is shut, and it is shut so smoothly that
Clara does not realise until later that she has been moved past a door)*

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Come and see the numbers before you decide. That's all I'd ask. Come and see the
numbers.

**ETHAN**
And if I come and see them and still say no?

**xÈth**
*(genuinely)*
Then you'll be the only person in the world who's looked. That's worth something
to me on its own.
*(beat)*
Ethan — I'm not going to hurt you. I want you to hear that in the open air with a
witness. Whatever happens, that isn't on the table, and it never was.

**ETHAN**
I believe you.

**xÈth**
*(nodding, satisfied)*
Good. Because it's the last true thing anybody's going to be able to say about
this and I'd like it said early.

He claps Ethan once on the shoulder and goes toward the road, and the agents
close in around him, and he is a president again.

> **NOTE —— 这是圣经铁律「反派必须堂堂正正赢下一场」的兑现场次，
> 而且它是全片最重要的一场戏之一。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - xÈth **一次都不许威胁**。他从头到尾都是在邀请。
> - Clara 答不上来，而且**电影不许替她答**。观众得自己憋着。
> - 「你在为我救下来的人哀悼」这句必须给它完整的空气。它是本片最强的一句
>   反方论证，若被急着反驳，全片就变成宣传。
> - **不许配阴森音乐。** 这一场的配乐（如果有）要暖。

## 8. THE BOOK ON THE SHELF

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — CLARA'S OFFICE — NIGHT**

Small, warm, a desk lamp, walls of ordinary books.

Ethan pulls one down without looking — the cloth spine from the prologue.

**ETHAN**
When did you last read this?

**CLARA**
School. Everybody read it at school.

**ETHAN**
*(opening it, reading)*
"Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls
the past."
*(he closes it)*
That's not a metaphor, Clara. That's a **method statement.**

**CLARA**
*(taking it from him, and putting it back, and she is not being flippant)*
Everybody's read it. That's the problem.
*(beat)*
It's been read so many times it can't be true.

## 9. THE LAST PERSON IN THE ROOM

**EXT. A CARE HOME — DAY**

Low brick, a car park, a flowerbed somebody looks after properly. Ethan sits in
the car with a folder on his knee and doesn't get out for a while.

He is looking at a photocopy: the 1977 attendance sheet. **Three signatures.**

He puts it in the folder and gets out.

**INT. A CARE HOME — DAY ROOM — DAY**

Good light. A television nobody is watching. **DR. HELEN REESE**, 80s, in a
cardigan, entirely sharp, doing nothing at all and enjoying it.

**ETHAN**
Dr Reese. I'm sorry to —

**HELEN**
You're the historian. You wrote to me twice.

**ETHAN**
Three times.

**HELEN**
Twice arrived.

She likes him immediately.

**ETHAN**
July of seventy-seven. The clean room.

**HELEN**
*(lighting up completely)*
Oh, **that.**

And she tells it, fast, delighted, in the voice of a woman who has waited forty
years for somebody to ask.

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
Four of us in there that afternoon. Marion had the plating. Baz wanted the
whales in, and Ackerman said it was a greeting card, and they went round and
round —
*(she laughs)*
— and then the fourth one said the thing that shut everybody up.

**ETHAN**
What thing?

**HELEN**
He said: someone is going to try to take it out.
*(beat)*
And we all **laughed.** God forgive us, we laughed at him. Somebody at the back
laughed first and then we all did.

**ETHAN**
*(very carefully)*
Who was he?

**HELEN**
*(without any hesitation at all)*
Daniel Vance.

The name goes through Ethan like cold water. He does not react.

> **CANON —— Ethan 的父亲。** 圣经 v2：「一个选了我们这边的不朽者的儿子」。
> 观众此刻**不知道**这是他父亲，只知道姓氏相同。
> **禁止**：不许推镜到 Ethan 脸上；不许配乐；不许让 Helen 注意到姓氏。

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
*(pushing herself up)*
I've got the picture. Everyone always thinks I haven't got the picture.

**INT. CARE HOME — HELEN'S ROOM — MOMENTS LATER**

She takes a framed photograph off the wall and puts it in his hands.

Black and white. Beige clean-room wall. A steel bench, a gold disc. **Four people
in white coats and paper caps**, squinting, arms round each other, 1977.

**HELEN**
That's me. That's Marion. That's Baz.
*(her finger moves to the fourth)*
And that's —

She stops.

Her finger is on him. She is looking directly at him. Nothing about her face has
changed.

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
*(mildly, curious)*
Who's that?

**ETHAN**
*(after a moment)*
I don't know.

**HELEN**
*(peering, entirely untroubled)*
Well, he's in it, so he must have been there.
*(she takes the frame back, looks once more, shrugs, and hangs it up)*
There were only three of us that afternoon, though. I'd remember.

**INT. CARE HOME — DAY ROOM — LATER**

She is back in her chair with tea somebody brought her. Ethan sits down opposite,
and he asks it as lightly as he has ever asked anything.

**ETHAN**
Dr Reese — the man who said the thing. About someone trying to take it out.

**HELEN**
*(pleasantly)*
Which man?

**ETHAN**
You told me his name about four minutes ago.

**HELEN**
*(and she is not defensive, she is simply correcting a young man who has got
something muddled)*
No, dear. There were three of us. Marion, Baz and me.
*(beat)*
Somebody said it, though. Somebody definitely said it. I remember laughing.

**ETHAN**
You remember laughing.

**HELEN**
*(and she smiles, and it is the worst thing in the scene)*
Oh, we roared.

Beat. Ethan looks at her for a long moment. She looks back, comfortable, a
pleasant old woman having a pleasant afternoon.

**ETHAN**
*(standing, and he keeps his voice completely level)*
Thank you. You've been very helpful.

**HELEN**
Come back. Nobody asks about the good part.

**INT. CARE HOME — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**

Ethan walks out along a corridor with handrails on both sides.

Halfway down it, he stops. Puts one hand flat on the rail. Stands there.

Then he takes out his phone and photographs the photocopy of the attendance
sheet, and then, after a moment, he writes something in the spiral notebook —
the first new entry we have seen him make that is not a correction.

**HE WRITES:** *Daniel Vance. 1977. Four in the room.*

He looks at it. Then he underlines it twice, which he has never done.

> **NOTE —— 铁律 5：写字与画线必须 i2v。** 这是全片第二次看见那本笔记本
> （第一次在 §1），而这一次他不是在划掉，他是在**存证**。
>
> 「Daniel Vance」这个姓氏观众已经见过一次了（`ETHAN VANCE`）。
> **电影此刻仍然一个字都不解释。**

> **NOTE —— 这是全片最重要的一场戏，因为它把两条规则钉在同一格画面里
> （圣经 §2.3）：**
>
> **照片没有变。** 上面还是四个人。**照片改不了 —— 模拟形状不可改写。**
> 变的是她。
>
> 于是我们得到全片最恐怖的一个画面：**一个老太太手里拿着一个她再也想不起来
> 的人的实物证据，而且她一点也不困扰。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 照片**逐像素不变**。这是确定性道具（铁律 7.6），不进生成，前后两次
>   出现必须是同一份文件。
> - Helen **不许**表现出困惑、恐惧或努力回想。她只是在看一张有个陌生人的
>   旧照片，这种事很正常。**她的平静就是这场戏的恐怖。**
> - Ethan **不许**告诉她。

## 10. THE WAR THAT WAS DELETED

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — NIGHT**

Ethan comes down the aisle fast, with the photograph under his arm. Clara is
already up, already reading his face.

**ETHAN**
If you can take a man out of a room — can you take a room out of a country?

**CLARA**
What?

**ETHAN**
Your catalogue. What's in here that **isn't** in it?

**CLARA**
Nothing's in here that isn't in it. That's what a catalogue **is.**

**ETHAN**
Clara. What's in here that isn't in it.

Beat. And she turns and walks, fast, and he goes after her.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEEP STACK — CONTINUOUS**

She pulls a drawer of index cards — paper, handwritten, decades old — and sets it
on the bench beside a terminal.

**CLARA**
Cans go in by accession number. The number is stamped into the rim at intake, by
a machine, in metal.
*(she taps the screen)*
And it's typed into this. Two records of the same thing. One of them is a shape.

**ETHAN**
And if they don't agree.

**CLARA**
They always agree. That's the entire job.

She starts. Card, screen. Card, screen. Card, screen.

**MONTAGE — THE COUNT**

Hours. The overhead lights on a timer clunk off in one aisle and she goes and
punches them back on without stopping. Ethan carries cans two at a time. Coffee
appears. Coffee goes cold.

Card, screen. Card, screen.

And then Clara's hand stops on a card.

She reads it. She reads the screen. She reads the card again. She types the
number a second time, slowly, in case it was her.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
*(quietly)*
Bring me 4471.

He brings it. She turns the rim to the light and reads the stamp with her thumb
on it like a blind woman.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
It's here. It's in my hand. It weighs eleven hundred grams.
*(she turns to the screen)*
And this says that number was never issued.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEEP STACK — LATER**

Eleven film cans in a row on the bench. Clara stands looking at them with her
arms folded, and she has gone somewhere else.

**ETHAN**
Eleven.

**CLARA**
*(and now she is frightened, and it is the first time in the film)*
Ethan, there are **eleven numbers stamped into metal** that the record says were
never issued.
*(beat)*
Somebody came down here and corrected the catalogue. And then they went home.
Because they'd finished. Because as far as the record is concerned, **that's what
finished looks like.**
*(she puts a hand on the nearest can)*
They didn't think to check whether the thing was still on the shelf.

**ETHAN**
Why would they.

**CLARA**
*(and this is the line that arms the whole film)*
Because they've never once had to. Nobody has ever checked them.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — SCREENING ROOM — NIGHT**

A small room. Two chairs. A projector she has to switch on with a physical
breaker, which throws with a sound like a door bolt.

**CLARA**
*(threading it, not looking up)*
If this is a training film about fire safety I'm going to be very embarrassed.

**ETHAN**
I hope it is.

**CLARA**
*(and she means it)*
So do I.

She kills the lights.

The leader runs. Numbers, scratches, dust. Ten seconds of nothing, which is
exactly long enough for both of them to relax.

And then:

**A CITY.** Aerial, black and white, and it is burning from one edge of the frame
to the other.

Cut. **A COLUMN OF PEOPLE** on a highway, carrying what they could carry,
going away from something, thousands of them, and the road behind them full for
as far as the lens can hold.

Cut. **A DEFENSIVE LINE** — ordinary men and women behind a wall of vehicles,
in ordinary coats, holding rifles the way people hold rifles when nobody has ever
taught them.

Cut. **A CHURCH HALL**, handheld, jerking: rows of camp beds, children asleep in
coats, a woman writing names on a blackboard in chalk. The camera pans the
blackboard slowly, deliberately, because whoever was holding it understood that
this was the only place those names now existed.

Cut. **A HARBOUR**, wide, static, held for a very long time: a line of small boats
going out loaded and coming back empty, over and over, until the light goes.

Cut. **A MAN** in a doorway, close, saying something directly into the lens with
great urgency. **There is no sound.** He says it three times to be sure. Then he
nods once, and steps back inside, and closes the door.

Cut. **A DEFENSIVE LINE**, second position, and this time we are behind it, and
we can see what they are looking at down the length of the street.

Nothing is coming.

They wait. Somebody laughs, badly. They keep waiting.

Cut. And now, coming up that same street toward the camera, unhurried, in a line
that does not break:

**THEM.**

Not monsters. Not armour. Not weapons.

**Men and women in grey civil-service coats, walking, at a normal pace, carrying
folders.** And where they have passed, the street behind them is orderly and
undamaged and completely empty.

One of them, in passing, stops. Looks directly into the lens — mildly, the way
you look at a parking meter — and reaches toward it.

**A GLOVED HAND FILLS THE FRAME.**

Not violently. He simply puts his hand over the lens, the way you would put a
cloth over a birdcage.

The camera operator runs. The frame goes to sky, to legs, to gravel, to a
shopfront, to a shoe, to nothing.

And then — for eleven frames, and only if you are watching — **a face**, turned
back over a shoulder in the moving line. No cap. Silver hair. Not a day younger.

The reel runs out and the tail flaps around the take-up, slap, slap, slap, until
Clara reaches over and stops it with one finger.

> **NOTE (重看结构 —— 与 1977 洁净室背景里那一格同类):**
> 那十一帧**观众第一遍绝对看不见**，而且不许用任何手段提示。第二遍看，
> 它是全片最冷的一格。**剪辑上必须真的只有十一帧**，不许延长到「刚好能看清」。
>
> **NOTE —— 那只手套。** 他们对镜头做的事，和他们对整场战争做的事，
> 是同一个动作：**不是砸碎它，是盖住它。** 这一格是全片主题最经济的一次表达，
> 而且它不需要一个字。

> **NOTE (圣经 v2 §三·场面 1) —— 全片第一个史诗场面，而它的核心是一个反转：**
>
> 我们花了十二分钟给观众一场真正的战争 —— 城市、难民、防线 —— **然后揭示
> 敌人是一队拿着文件夹、按正常步速走路的公务员。** 街道在他们走过之后是
> **完好的**、整齐的、空的。
>
> **这一格必须与序章开场的火星城市网格是同一件事的两个尺度。**
> 观众要在这一秒把两张图接上。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 火星人**不许有武器、不许有装甲、不许跑**（铁律 6：没写的会被类型套路填满 ——
>   不写死一定会出战术背心和动力甲）。
> - 街道在他们身后**完好无损**。破坏一处，整个概念就塌了。
> - 这一整段是**幸存的胶片**：划痕、闪烁、跳帧、光晕。质感就是论点 ——
>   **它之所以还在，正因为它是模拟的。**

Silence in the screening room. The projector lamp ticks as it cools.

**CLARA**
*(barely)*
What was that.

Ethan gets up and goes to the projector and lifts the can and turns it to the
light and reads the number stamped in the metal.

And then he reads the four digits after it.

He puts the can down. He sits back down. He does not look at her.

**ETHAN**
We were twenty-six.

**CLARA**
*(and she has heard him, and she is refusing it)*
No.

**ETHAN**
We were twenty-six years old, and that happened, and we were **here.**

**CLARA**
*(standing up, hard)*
Ethan, I don't remember **any** of this. Not one frame of it. I'd remember a
**war.**

Beat.

**ETHAN**
No.
*(and he says the rest of it very gently, because he has just understood it
himself, and because it is the worst thing he has ever said to her)*
But you've been sad about it for thirty years.

She stops.

And across her face — not memory. Memory is gone. Something underneath it, that
was never written down and therefore was never edited, and has had nowhere to go
for three decades.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
The fourteenth of every year. The day everybody takes off and nobody can tell
you what for.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**

> **PAYOFF (§3 埋的伏笔) —— 全国默哀日。**
>
> **NOTE (圣经 §2.3 第三样不可改写的东西 —— 亲历经验):**
> 这是全片对这条规则最好的一次使用，也是它的解法：**亲历经验确实改不掉，
> 但它可以被切断出口。** 那场战争还在每一个人身上，只是没有地方去。
> 于是整个国家为一件说不出名字的事悲伤了三十年。
>
> **这一句解释了全片此前所有的「不对劲」，而且它一句台词都不用多。**

> **PART ONE ENDS → PART TWO: `THE ADMINISTRATION`**
>
> 两条分线自此分岔：
> - **`earth`** —— Ethan 与 Clara 带着十一个罐子，试图在记录内部取胜。
> - **`mars`** —— June Hale 在白宫内部，亲眼看见那套系统**真的在减轻痛苦**。
