## 1. THE OATH

**EXT. THE CAPITOL — INAUGURATION DAY — MORNING**

Cold, bright, enormous. A crowd that goes back further than the frame.

**xÈth**, bare-headed in the cold, puts his hand down and takes the oath. He says
it perfectly. He does not stumble on a syllable of it.

**xÈth**
—that I will faithfully execute the Office of President of the United States,
and will to the best of my ability **preserve, protect and defend** the
Constitution of the United States.

Applause like weather.

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — FACULTY LOUNGE — CONTINUOUS**

A dozen people watching it on a wall screen with coffee. **ETHAN** at the back,
not sitting down.

On screen, the network's caption crawls under the picture. It reads:

> …WILL TO THE BEST OF MY ABILITY **PRESERVE, PROTECT AND CORRECT** THE
> CONSTITUTION…

Ethan's head comes up.

He looks around the room. Nobody else has moved. The caption is already gone.

**ETHAN**
*(to the man beside him, carefully)*
What did he say? The last part.

**COLLEAGUE**
*(not looking away from the screen)*
Preserve, protect and defend.

**ETHAN**
Right.

> **NOTE —— 全片第一次改写，故意做得极小、极便宜、而且无害。**
> 观众可能没看清，可能以为自己看错了。**这正是目的。** 我们在这里教的不是
> 「他很邪恶」，是「**这件事发生时的样子就是这么小**」。
>
> **禁止**：不许配音效强调；不许推镜到字幕；不许让 Ethan 说出他看到了什么。
> 字幕在画面上停留不超过一秒。

## 2. A HISTORY LECTURE, INTERRUPTED

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — LECTURE HALL — DAY**

Two hundred nineteen-year-olds. Ethan down at the front, no notes, walking.

**ETHAN**
—so the number matters. It always matters. Whenever anybody tells you a number
went **down**, ask who counted it the second time.
*(beat)*
Nineteen thirty-one. The mine at Kellerman. How many.

**A STUDENT**
Eighty-four.

**ETHAN**
Eighty-four. It was in every paper in the country for a week. Now —

And then the floor moves.

It does not move. Nothing moves. But Ethan stops in the middle of a word, one
hand slightly out, like a man who has just heard a door close somewhere behind
him in an empty house.

He turns and looks at the screen behind him.

The slide says **SIXTY-ONE**.

He turns back to two hundred faces, all of whom are writing down *sixty-one*.

**ETHAN**
*(and his voice is completely normal)*
Sixty-one. Which was in every paper in the country for a week.

He keeps teaching. He teaches for another eleven minutes and he does not get
anything else wrong.

> **NOTE (铁律 5 —— 必须 i2v):** 全片最重要的一个「变化本身」。
> 静帧拍不了这场戏：**恐怖在于八十四变成六十一的那一瞬间，以及两百支笔
> 毫无迟疑地写下新数字。** 一定要有笔在动。
>
> **NOTE —— 表演铁律：他必须继续讲课。** 这个人独自扛着这件事已经很多年了，
> 他早就有了一整套应对流程。惊恐、颤抖、摔粉笔 —— 全错。**他只是接着讲。**

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — CORRIDOR — LATER**

Ethan walks out. On the wall, a printed notice among a hundred others:

> **UNIVERSITY CLOSED — TUESDAY 14th — DAY OF OBSERVANCE**

He stops. Reads it. Reads it again.

**ETHAN**
*(to a passing student, lightly)*
Hey — the fourteenth. What is that, do you know?

**STUDENT**
*(shrugging, friendly, already walking)*
Day of Observance.

**ETHAN**
Right, but for what?

The student turns round, walking backwards, genuinely trying to answer, and
finding nothing there.

**STUDENT**
…Huh.

She shrugs and goes.

> **PLANT —— 这是第一部最重要的一个伏笔，第 8 场兑现。**
> 全国性的默哀日，没有一个人说得出是为了什么。埋的时候要**完全随手**，
> 不给任何强调，不给音乐，不给反打。

## 3. THE CUSTODIAN

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — SUB-LEVEL — DAY**

Cold. Dim. The hum of chillers. Shelf after shelf of **film cans, magnetic reels,
glass plates, bound ledgers** — everything that is a *shape* rather than a number.

**CLARA SINCLAIR** walks a trolley down an aisle with the unhurried competence of
someone in her own house.

**ETHAN**
*(following)*
It happened again. During the lecture.

**CLARA**
Which one.

**ETHAN**
Kellerman. Eighty-four to sixty-one.

**CLARA**
*(not stopping)*
And nobody.

**ETHAN**
Nobody.

She stops at a shelf, pulls a flat grey box, opens it. Inside: a contact sheet.
Black and white. Men at a pithead in 1931. Rows of covered shapes on the ground.

She hands it to him. He counts. He counts again.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Eighty-four.

**CLARA**
Eighty-four.
*(she takes it back, and boxes it, and shelves it)*
That's why I'm down here and not up there.

**ETHAN**
Clara —

**CLARA**
I took an oath. It's four sentences long and one of them says *in the condition
received.*
*(beat)*
Everybody upstairs thinks that's about humidity.

> **NOTE (圣经 §2.3 —— 模拟形状不可改写):** 这场戏是全片的军械库介绍。
> **不要让任何人解释这条规则。** 让 Clara 拿出一张照片、数一遍、放回去。
> 观众自己会明白：他改得了数字，改不了形状。

She moves on. Ethan doesn't.

**ETHAN**
He asked us to come and see him.

**CLARA**
*(a beat, back still turned)*
I know. It's on my calendar. **He** put it on my calendar.

## 4. MARGOT

**INT. STATE UNIVERSITY — DEPARTMENT OFFICE — DAY**

Late afternoon, low sun, two desks, a kettle. **MARGOT** (60s) is at hers with a
mug, reading glasses up on her head, doing the crossword she does every day.

Ethan comes in and sits on the edge of the other desk and asks her something he
has clearly asked before.

**ETHAN**
Margot. The '09 flood. How many died?

**MARGOT**
*(not looking up)*
Two hundred and eleven.

**ETHAN**
You're sure.

**MARGOT**
Ethan, I was **there.** I was standing on the ridge road with your mother
watching the water come up the valley. Two hundred and eleven.
*(beat, and now she does look up, fond and exasperated)*
You ask me that about once a month. Why do you ask me that about once a month?

**ETHAN**
*(getting up)*
Because you always know.

He goes to the kettle. His back is turned. Nothing happens.

Nothing happens **at all** — no sound, no light change, no camera move.

**MARGOT**
*(behind him, mildly, going back to her crossword)*
Then it's a hundred and forty-nine, love.
*(beat)*
And I misremembered.

Ethan stands with the kettle in his hand.

He does not turn round for a long moment. When he does, his face is completely
normal.

**ETHAN**
Tea?

**MARGOT**
Please.

> **NOTE —— 全片最恐怖的一下，而且它必须拍得毫无戏剧性。**
> 已拍素材 `S2-3` / `S2-4`（`docs/timeempire-plates/b_open-coldopen/`）可直接沿用。
>
> **铁律 5 —— `S2-4` 必须是 i2v。** 恐怖在于**转变本身**：她说完前一句到说出
> 后一句之间，脸上什么都没变。静帧拍不了这个。
>
> **禁止**：不许有音乐进；不许推镜；不许让她露出困惑。**她一点都不困惑 ——
> 她只是在纠正一个笔误。**

## 5. THE OLD SCHOOL

**EXT. THE OLD UNIVERSITY — QUADRANGLE — DAY**

Brick, elms, a long lawn. Motorcade discreetly parked on the road outside;
two agents at fifty metres and no closer.

**xÈth** walks the quad with **Ethan** and **Clara**, in an overcoat, hands behind
his back, entirely at ease. He is showing them around a place all three of them
already know.

**xÈth**
They've moved the library.

**ETHAN**
Nineteen years ago.

**xÈth**
*(delighted)*
Nineteen years! And I'm still walking to the old one.

They pass students who do double-takes and then decide they must be wrong.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(to Clara, and it is not small talk)*
He'll have told you what I am.

**CLARA**
He has.

**xÈth**
And what he is.

**CLARA**
*(evenly)*
He's my husband.

**xÈth**
*(a real laugh)*
Yes. Good.

They walk.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Ethan. On this lawn, in the spring of the year we were both twenty-two, you
argued for two hours that a fact is a thing that happened, and I argued that a
fact is a thing that is **written down**, and neither of us moved an inch, and
then it rained.
*(beat)*
I'm going to make my version true. Not win the argument — **make it true.**
And I'd like you to come and work on it.

**ETHAN**
On what, exactly.

**xÈth**
On the largest project in the history of your species. There are four hundred
million people in this country and every one of them is carrying something that
happened to them, or to their parents, or to some strangers a hundred years
before they were born, and **not one of those things can be undone.**
*(he stops walking)*
I can undo them.

**CLARA**
You can **hide** them.

**xÈth**
*(turning to her, and this is the crux, and he is not remotely angry)*
Mrs Sinclair — what is the difference, to the person carrying it?

Silence on the lawn.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
I'm not being clever. It's a real question and I have never once been given an
answer to it by somebody who was, at that moment, in any pain.
*(gently)*
Every argument for keeping the wound has been made by people who weren't
bleeding. Show me one that wasn't.

Clara opens her mouth. Nothing comes.

**ETHAN**
*(quietly)*
Kellerman.

**xÈth**
Eighty-four men.

**ETHAN**
*(he looks up)*
Sixty-one.

**xÈth**
*(after a beat, and with something almost like respect)*
Ah. You saw that.

**ETHAN**
Twenty-three men, and their families, and everyone who ever grieved for them.

**xÈth**
No. **Twenty-three deaths.** The men were never in there — they went home, they
had lives, they died in their beds at eighty.
*(beat)*
You're mourning people I **saved.**

Ethan has no immediate answer. **The film should not give him one.**

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(walking on, easily)*
Come and see the numbers before you decide. That's all I'd ask. Come and see the
numbers.

He claps Ethan once on the shoulder and goes toward the road, and the agents
close in around him, and he is a president again.

> **NOTE —— 这是圣经铁律「反派必须堂堂正正赢下一场」的兑现场次，
> 而且它是全片最重要的一场戏之一。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - xÈth **一次都不许威胁**。他从头到尾都是在邀请。
> - Clara 答不上来，而且**电影不许替她答**。观众得自己憋着。
> - 「你在为我救下来的人哀悼」这句必须给它完整的空气。它是本片最强的一句
>   反方论证，若被急着反驳，全片就变成宣传。
> - **不许配阴森音乐。** 这一场的配乐（如果有）要暖。

## 6. THE BOOK ON THE SHELF

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — CLARA'S OFFICE — NIGHT**

Small, warm, a desk lamp, walls of ordinary books.

Ethan pulls one down without looking — the cloth spine from the prologue.

**ETHAN**
When did you last read this?

**CLARA**
School. Everybody read it at school.

**ETHAN**
*(opening it, reading)*
"Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls
the past."
*(he closes it)*
That's not a metaphor, Clara. That's a **method statement.**

**CLARA**
*(taking it from him, and putting it back, and she is not being flippant)*
Everybody's read it. That's the problem.
*(beat)*
It's been read so many times it can't be true.

> **NOTE (已发布设定 —— 那本被归档为虚构的书):** 全片三次出现的第二次。
> 第一次在序章 1948 年那间冷屋里，第三次在第三部。**三次都是同一本书、
> 同一个书脊 —— 确定性道具，不进生成**（铁律 7.6）。
>
> **不许**让任何人说出作者的名字，不许给书名特写。**观众自己认得出来，
> 而认出来的那一秒正是这场戏的全部效果。**

## 7. THE LAST PERSON IN THE ROOM

**INT. A CARE HOME — DAY ROOM — DAY**

Good light. A television nobody is watching. **DR. HELEN REESE**, 80s, in a
cardigan, entirely sharp, doing nothing at all and enjoying it.

**ETHAN**
Dr Reese. I'm sorry to —

**HELEN**
You're the historian. You wrote to me twice.

**ETHAN**
Three times.

**HELEN**
Twice arrived.

She likes him immediately.

**ETHAN**
July of seventy-seven. The clean room.

**HELEN**
*(lighting up completely)*
Oh, **that.**

And she tells it, fast, delighted, in the voice of a woman who has waited forty
years for somebody to ask.

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
Four of us in there that afternoon. Marion had the plating. Baz wanted the
whales in, and Ackerman said it was a greeting card, and they went round and
round —
*(she laughs)*
— and then the fourth one said the thing that shut everybody up.

**ETHAN**
What thing?

**HELEN**
He said: someone is going to try to take it out.
*(beat)*
And we all **laughed.** God forgive us, we laughed at him. Somebody at the back
laughed first and then we all did.

**ETHAN**
*(very carefully)*
Who was he?

**HELEN**
*(without any hesitation at all)*
Daniel Vance.

The name goes through Ethan like cold water. He does not react.

> **CANON —— Ethan 的父亲。** 圣经 v2：「一个选了我们这边的不朽者的儿子」。
> 观众此刻**不知道**这是他父亲，只知道姓氏相同。
> **禁止**：不许推镜到 Ethan 脸上；不许配乐；不许让 Helen 注意到姓氏。

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
*(pushing herself up)*
I've got the picture. Everyone always thinks I haven't got the picture.

**INT. CARE HOME — HELEN'S ROOM — MOMENTS LATER**

She takes a framed photograph off the wall and puts it in his hands.

Black and white. Beige clean-room wall. A steel bench, a gold disc. **Four people
in white coats and paper caps**, squinting, arms round each other, 1977.

**HELEN**
That's me. That's Marion. That's Baz.
*(her finger moves to the fourth)*
And that's —

She stops.

Her finger is on him. She is looking directly at him. Nothing about her face has
changed.

**HELEN (CONT'D)**
*(mildly, curious)*
Who's that?

**ETHAN**
*(after a moment)*
I don't know.

**HELEN**
*(peering, entirely untroubled)*
Well, he's in it, so he must have been there.
*(she takes the frame back, looks once more, shrugs, and hangs it up)*
There were only three of us that afternoon, though. I'd remember.

She goes back to the day room. Ethan stays where he is, looking at a photograph
of four people on a wall.

> **NOTE —— 这是全片最重要的一场戏，因为它把两条规则钉在同一格画面里
> （圣经 §2.3）：**
>
> **照片没有变。** 上面还是四个人。**照片改不了 —— 模拟形状不可改写。**
> 变的是她。
>
> 于是我们得到全片最恐怖的一个画面：**一个老太太手里拿着一个她再也想不起来
> 的人的实物证据，而且她一点也不困扰。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 照片**逐像素不变**。这是确定性道具（铁律 7.6），不进生成，前后两次
>   出现必须是同一份文件。
> - Helen **不许**表现出困惑、恐惧或努力回想。她只是在看一张有个陌生人的
>   旧照片，这种事很正常。**她的平静就是这场戏的恐怖。**
> - Ethan **不许**告诉她。

## 8. THE WAR THAT WAS DELETED

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — NIGHT**

Ethan comes down the aisle fast, with the photograph under his arm. Clara is
already up, already reading his face.

**ETHAN**
If you can take a man out of a room — can you take a room out of a country?

**CLARA**
What?

**ETHAN**
Your catalogue. What's in here that **isn't** in it?

**CLARA**
Nothing's in here that isn't in it. That's what a catalogue **is.**

**ETHAN**
Clara. What's in here that isn't in it.

Beat. And she turns and walks, fast, and he goes after her.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEEP STACK — CONTINUOUS**

She pulls a drawer of index cards — paper, handwritten, decades old — and lays it
against a screen listing, and runs both with two fingers.

**CLARA**
*(flat, working)*
Cans go in by accession number. The number is stamped in the metal at intake.
Physical. Not typed.
*(she stops)*
There are eleven cans in this room whose numbers aren't in the catalogue.

**ETHAN**
Eleven.

**CLARA**
*(and now she is frightened, and it is the first time)*
Ethan, there are **eleven numbers stamped into metal** that the record says were
never issued.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — SCREENING ROOM — NIGHT**

A small room. Two chairs. A projector she has to switch on with a physical
breaker.

The leader runs. Numbers, scratches, dust.

And then:

**A CITY.** Aerial, black and white, and it is burning from one edge of the frame
to the other.

Cut. **A COLUMN OF PEOPLE** on a highway, carrying what they could carry,
going away from something, thousands of them, and the road behind them full for
as far as the lens can hold.

Cut. **A DEFENSIVE LINE** — ordinary men and women behind a wall of vehicles,
in ordinary coats, holding rifles the way people hold rifles when nobody has ever
taught them.

Cut. And now, coming up a street toward the camera, unhurried, in a line that
does not break:

**THEM.**

Not monsters. Not armour. Not weapons.

**Men and women in grey civil-service coats, walking, at a normal pace, carrying
folders.** And where they have passed, the street behind them is orderly and
undamaged and completely empty.

The camera operator runs. The frame goes to sky, to legs, to gravel, to nothing.

The reel runs out and the tail flaps around the take-up.

> **NOTE (圣经 v2 §三·场面 1) —— 全片第一个史诗场面，而它的核心是一个反转：**
>
> 我们花了十二分钟给观众一场真正的战争 —— 城市、难民、防线 —— **然后揭示
> 敌人是一队拿着文件夹、按正常步速走路的公务员。** 街道在他们走过之后是
> **完好的**、整齐的、空的。
>
> **这一格必须与序章开场的火星城市网格是同一件事的两个尺度。**
> 观众要在这一秒把两张图接上。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 火星人**不许有武器、不许有装甲、不许跑**（铁律 6：没写的会被类型套路填满 ——
>   不写死一定会出战术背心和动力甲）。
> - 街道在他们身后**完好无损**。破坏一处，整个概念就塌了。
> - 这一整段是**幸存的胶片**：划痕、闪烁、跳帧、光晕。质感就是论点 ——
>   **它之所以还在，正因为它是模拟的。**

Silence in the screening room. The projector lamp ticks as it cools.

**CLARA**
*(barely)*
What was that.

Ethan gets up and goes to the projector and lifts the can and turns it to the
light and reads the number stamped in the metal.

And then he reads the four digits after it.

He puts the can down. He sits back down. He does not look at her.

**ETHAN**
We were twenty-six.

**CLARA**
*(and she has heard him, and she is refusing it)*
No.

**ETHAN**
We were twenty-six years old, and that happened, and we were **here.**

**CLARA**
*(standing up, hard)*
Ethan, I don't remember **any** of this. Not one frame of it. I'd remember a
**war.**

Beat.

**ETHAN**
No.
*(and he says the rest of it very gently, because he has just understood it
himself, and because it is the worst thing he has ever said to her)*
But you've been sad about it for thirty years.

She stops.

And across her face — not memory. Memory is gone. Something underneath it, that
was never written down and therefore was never edited, and has had nowhere to go
for three decades.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
The fourteenth of every year. The day everybody takes off and nobody can tell
you what for.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**

> **PAYOFF (§2 埋的伏笔) —— 全国默哀日。**
>
> **NOTE (圣经 §2.3 第三样不可改写的东西 —— 亲历经验):**
> 这是全片对这条规则最好的一次使用，也是它的解法：**亲历经验确实改不掉，
> 但它可以被切断出口。** 那场战争还在每一个人身上，只是没有地方去。
> 于是整个国家为一件说不出名字的事悲伤了三十年。
>
> **这一句解释了全片此前所有的「不对劲」，而且它一句台词都不用多。**
