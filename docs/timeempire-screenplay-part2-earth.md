## 1. TEN CANS

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEEP STACK — MORNING**

Clara comes down the aisle with two coffees and stops.

The bench where eleven film cans were standing in a row now has ten.

She does not panic. She puts both coffees down and counts them, twice, with her
finger, out loud.

**CLARA**
Ten.

**ETHAN (O.S.)**
*(coming up behind her)*
What?

**CLARA**
There are ten.

She is already moving — to the terminal, to the door log, to the drawer.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
*(reading)*
Nobody's badged into this level since Thursday except you and me.

**ETHAN**
Then it's still here. It's been moved.

**CLARA**
*(and she says this like a woman reciting the only prayer she has)*
Nothing on this level gets moved without a docket.

She pulls the docket book — **paper, bound, sequential, biro** — and turns it to
the last page.

There is a docket. It is filled in correctly. It is signed.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
*(reading the signature)*
…Norris.

**ETHAN**
Who's Norris?

**CLARA**
Norris is a man who works four floors up and has never once been down here in his
life.

> **NOTE —— 本条线的第一记，而它是一个【好消息】伪装成坏消息。**
>
> 他们改不了那个罐子，**所以他们不得不派一个人下来把它搬走** ——
> 而人会签字，纸会留痕。**模拟介质逼着敌人现出实体。**
> 这一条是全片第三幕能成立的技术前提，此处第一次亮出来。
>
> **禁止**：不许有撬开的锁、不许有监控画面、不许有脚印。
> **他们没有闯进来。他们走正常流程，而且流程做得比 Clara 还标准。**

## 2. THE OTHER NINE

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — SCREENING ROOM — NIGHT**

Ten cans stacked on the floor. One already run. Clara threads the second.

**MONTAGE — WHAT ELSE IS IN THE ROOM**

**Can 2.** A city street in summer, 1962 by the cars. A parade going past a
reviewing stand. On the stand, waving, a man in a good suit — and beside him, in
the second row, unremarked, **a man with silver hair who does not wave.**

**Can 3.** Interior, a hall, black and white. A MAN AT A LECTERN delivering an
address to a full room. Sound is on the film and it is clear and he is very good.
He speaks for forty seconds about a decision he has taken and what it will cost.
He is plainly a president.

Neither Ethan nor Clara has ever seen him before.

**Can 4.** A harbour, 1911 by the ships. Something enormous burning on the water,
and small boats going out to it.

**Can 5.** Blank. Fogged, edge to edge, deliberately: somebody opened it in the
light.

**Can 6.** A field hospital. A queue of stretchers longer than the lens.

**Can 7.** Eleven seconds of a woman's face, close, saying nothing, looking
directly into the camera. Then it cuts.

**Can 8.** A classroom of children reciting something in unison. The camera is at
the back. It runs for a minute and a half and nothing happens at all.

**Can 9.** A wide shot of a valley with a town in it.

**Can 10.** The same valley. No town.

**BACK TO — THE SCREENING ROOM**

The last tail flaps round. Clara stops it.

They sit in the dark with the lamp cooling.

**CLARA**
Nineteen eleven. Nineteen sixty-two.
*(beat)*
It isn't one war.

**ETHAN**
No.

**CLARA**
How long has he been —

**ETHAN**
*(and he has done this arithmetic already, and it is the reason he has not slept)*
Helen Reese said he wasn't a day younger in seventy-seven than he is now.
*(beat)*
Nineteen eleven is the **oldest can in this room.** It isn't the oldest thing
he's done. It's the oldest thing anybody happened to point a camera at.

**CLARA**
*(looking at the stack)*
Ten cans.

**ETHAN**
Ten cans in one building, in one country, that survived because somebody stamped
a number into metal instead of typing it.
*(beat)*
How many buildings are there?

> **NOTE —— 规模的第二次跃升，而它是用【八个静止的镜头】完成的，
> 不是用一场大戏。**
>
> **第 9 罐与第 10 罐是全片最经济的一记：同一个山谷，有镇 / 无镇。**
> 两张几乎相同的图，一句台词都不需要。
>
> **第 3 罐那位无人认得的总统必须演得极好** —— 他不许可笑、不许模糊、
> 不许像资料片。他要好到让观众为一个从未存在过的人难过四十秒。
>
> **第 2 罐里检阅台第二排那个不挥手的人，第一遍不许被注意到。**

## 3. THE PHOTOGRAPH THAT CHANGED

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — MORNING**

The spiral notebook is open on the counter. Ethan is doing the ninety seconds.

He stops on the third line, and instead of the phone he picks up the **photograph
he took in Part One** — the photocopy of the 1977 attendance sheet, shot on his
phone in a care-home corridor.

He looks at it for a while.

Then he turns the phone round to Clara.

**ETHAN**
How many signatures.

**CLARA**
*(glancing)*
Three.

**ETHAN**
It was three when I photographed it.
*(beat)*
Look at the footnote.

She takes the phone and reads it and her face changes.

> **INSERT — THE PHONE**
>
> *…and the names of those present were entered in summary.*

**CLARA**
That wasn't there.

**ETHAN**
No.

**CLARA**
Ethan, that's a **photograph.** That's a photograph **you took.**

**ETHAN**
*(and he says it flatly, because he worked it out at four this morning)*
It's a file. It's a file on a phone, and it lives on a server, and it belongs to
them like everything else on a server.
*(beat)*
I've been collecting evidence for six weeks in the one place where evidence
doesn't stay put.

He puts the phone face down on the counter, and then, after a second, in a
drawer.

**CLARA**
*(already thinking)*
Then we print.

**ETHAN**
We print. We photocopy. We photograph on **film.** We write it out by hand.
*(beat)*
And we never, ever put a number in a machine again.

> **NOTE —— 第二记，代价：六周的工作。**
>
> 这一场教会观众全片最重要的一条操作规则，**而且是通过让主角损失掉东西
> 来教的**，不是通过有人解释。
>
> **NOTE (铁律 7.6):** 手机上那张照片是**确定性道具**，前后两次出现必须
> 是同一份文件的两个版本，逐像素可比对。绝不进生成。

## 4. THE MAN WHO SIGNED

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — CAFETERIA — DAY**

**NORRIS** (50s), a mild man with a lanyard and a tray, is being asked something
he does not understand.

**CLARA**
Thursday afternoon. Sub-level four.

**NORRIS**
I've never been to sub-level four.

**CLARA**
*(putting the docket book on the table, open, and turning it round)*
That's your signature.

Norris looks at it. He looks at it for a long time. Then he does something that
is far worse than denying it:

He takes out a pen and **writes his signature on a napkin**, next to it, to
compare.

They match.

**NORRIS**
*(quietly)*
That's mine.

**CLARA**
Do you remember —

**NORRIS**
No.
*(beat)*
Thursday I was — Thursday I —

He stops. He is not being evasive. He is looking for Thursday and Thursday is
not there.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — NORRIS'S OFFICE — LATER**

Small, tidy, a window. He takes a **paper diary** out of the drawer and opens it
at the week and turns it toward them without a word.

His own handwriting. Thursday:

> **2.15 — S/L 4 — collect (1) can, 4471 — auth. verbal**

**NORRIS (CONT'D)**
*(and his voice has gone strange)*
I write everything down. My wife laughs at me.

**ETHAN**
Mr Norris —

**NORRIS**
*(and now it comes, and it is the first genuine fear in the film)*
I don't remember writing that.
*(beat)*
I don't remember Thursday. I've got a diary that says I went somewhere I've never
been, in my own hand, and I —

He stops, because he has run out of sentence.

**CLARA**
*(gently)*
Where did you take it.

**NORRIS**
*(and he looks at the page, and then at her, and it is unbearable)*
It doesn't say.

Long beat.

**ETHAN**
Mr Norris. Don't tell anyone we asked.

**NORRIS**
*(a small, bad laugh)*
Who would I tell? I'd have to write it down.

> **NOTE —— 第三记，代价：一个无辜的人。**
>
> 这是全片第一次让观众看见**这件事发生在普通人身上是什么样子** ——
> 不是恐怖，是**一个把每件事都写下来的人，发现自己的笔迹在证明一件他
> 没有经历过的事**。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Norris **不许**是卧底、不许是被收买的、不许在后面的戏里揭穿是敌人。
>   他就是一个被用完的普通人。
> - **不许**给他任何解脱。他此后不再出现。
> - 那本纸质日记是**确定性道具**。

## 5. WE GO PUBLIC

**INT. A NEWSPAPER OFFICE — MEETING ROOM — DAY**

**PRIYA SHAH** (30s) has been listening for fifty minutes without interrupting,
which is why she is good.

On the table: the docket book. Norris's diary, photographed **on film**, printed.
A contact sheet from 1931. Ten film cans in two crates.

**PRIYA**
I want to say the thing I have to say, and then I want to say the other thing.

**CLARA**
Go on.

**PRIYA**
The thing I have to say is that this is the best-documented story anybody has
ever brought me and I have been doing this for eleven years.
*(beat)*
The other thing is that I don't believe a word of it.

**ETHAN**
*(without any heat)*
Good.

**PRIYA**
*(surprised into a laugh)*
Good?

**ETHAN**
If you believed it on a Tuesday afternoon in a meeting room you'd be no use to us.

Priya taps the docket book once.

**PRIYA**
I'm going to run the can. Just the can. One man, one signature, one afternoon he
can't account for, and a film canister that the catalogue says was never issued.
*(beat)*
No Martians. No president. Nothing anybody can laugh at.

**CLARA**
That's less than a tenth of it.

**PRIYA**
*(standing, gathering)*
Mrs Sinclair — that's how it's done. You don't hand them the whole thing and
watch them pick the silliest bit. **You give them one fact with a hole in it and
you let the country stare at the hole.**

## 6. WHAT HAPPENED TO THE STORY

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

They watch it land. It is a good story. It is careful and it is dull in the right
way and by ten o'clock two other papers have matched it.

Clara sits on the floor with her back against the sofa and lets out a breath she
has been holding for four weeks.

**CLARA**
There it is.

**ETHAN**
*(and he is allowing himself something, which is a mistake)*
There it is.

**INT. THE NEWSPAPER OFFICE — DAY**

Ethan comes through the newsroom with a coffee in each hand and finds Priya at
her desk, working.

**ETHAN**
Two more matched it overnight —

**PRIYA**
*(pleasantly, not looking up)*
Sorry — matched what?

Ethan stops walking.

**PRIYA (CONT'D)**
*(now looking up, polite, helpful, a professional talking to a member of the
public)*
Sorry, have we met?

Silence in a busy room.

**ETHAN**
*(very carefully)*
Ms Shah. You ran a piece on Sunday about a records office.

**PRIYA**
*(turning to her screen, typing, entirely willing)*
Let me look. What was the —
*(scrolling)*
I've got nothing on Sunday. I was on the housing thing all last week.

**ETHAN**
Your notebook.

**PRIYA**
*(a beat — and this is a woman being asked something odd by a stranger, and
being nice about it)*
Sorry?

**ETHAN**
Please. Your notebook. The one you had in the meeting.

Because she is a decent person, she takes it out of her bag and hands it to him.

He turns to the middle. Fifty minutes of shorthand, dense, dated, in her own
hand.

He turns it round to her.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Read it to me.

She looks at it. Her lips move.

And her face does not go pale, and she does not gasp. What it does is far worse:
she frowns, mildly — the frown of somebody who has found an old shopping list —
and turns two more pages, and then back, checking.

**PRIYA**
*(and she is puzzled, and that is all she is)*
That's my shorthand.
*(beat)*
I can read every word of it and I don't know what it's about.

She looks up at him, apologetic, ready to be helpful about somebody else's
problem.

**PRIYA (CONT'D)**
Is it important?

> **NOTE —— 第四记，代价：唯一一条出路。**
>
> **这是全片对「模拟形状不可改写」最残忍的一次使用：** 那本速记本没有变。
> 每一个字都在。**她能一字不差地读出来，而它对她不再意味着任何东西。**
>
> 圣经 §2.3 保住的是**形状**，不是**意义**。意义在人身上，而人是可以更正的。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Priya **不许**害怕、不许怀疑、不许被暗示成同谋。她一直是个好人，
>   而且一直在帮忙。
> - **不许**配乐。**不许**给 Ethan 特写。整场戏拍在一间正常运转的新闻编辑室里，
>   周围所有人都在忙自己的事。
> - 最后那句「Is it important?」之后**直接切**。

## 7. THE MAN ON THE PLATFORM

**INT. A COMMUTER STATION — PLATFORM — EVENING**

Ethan stands with a paper cup among sixty other people, going home.

Across the tracks, on the opposite platform, also going home, also among sixty
other people, in an overcoat, with no visible security anywhere:

**xÈth.**

He is not watching Ethan. He is looking at the departure board like everybody
else.

Then he looks across.

They see each other across four rails and about nine metres.

xÈth does not smile. He does not signal. He does not do anything at all except
**nod once** — the small, courteous, entirely unremarkable nod you give a
colleague you have already spoken to today.

Then his train comes in between them.

When it has gone, the opposite platform is empty.

Ethan stands where he is. His train comes. He does not get on it.

> **NOTE —— xÈth 在这条线里唯一一次出现，而且一个字都不说。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许**有随扈、不许有封站、不许有人回头看他。他在坐通勤火车。
> - 那个点头**不许**有任何威胁意味。它是打招呼。**这才是它可怕的原因 ——
>   他没有在警告 Ethan，他只是在下班路上碰到了熟人。**
> - **不许**配乐。**不许**推镜。两个人各站一边，隔着四条轨道。
> - 最后 Ethan 没上车 —— **不许**给他特写解释为什么。

## 8. THE FOURTEENTH

**EXT. A CITY — MIDDAY — THE FOURTEENTH**

A siren, single, long, from somewhere high up.

And the country stops.

**EXT. A MOTORWAY — CONTINUOUS**

Six lanes of traffic pulling onto the hard shoulder. Doors opening. People
getting out and standing beside their cars, and taking off hats, in the wind.

**EXT. A SCHOOL PLAYGROUND — CONTINUOUS**

Four hundred children standing absolutely still in rows.

**INT. A SUPERMARKET — CONTINUOUS**

Everybody stopped where they were. A woman with her hand still on a shelf. A boy
on a checkout, head down.

**EXT. A HARBOUR — CONTINUOUS**

Boats stopped. Men standing on decks with their caps off.

**EXT. A CITY SQUARE — CONTINUOUS**

Thousands of people. Not a movement. Not a cough.

And the camera goes slowly, slowly along their faces — and every one of them is
**genuinely grieving.** Old men with their jaws set. A young woman crying openly
and steadily without knowing why. A man holding a child's shoulder too hard.

**Nobody knows what it is for.**

Two minutes. The film gives it the whole two minutes.

Then the siren again, and it is over, and four hundred children go back to
running about, and six lanes of traffic pull out, and the woman takes the tin off
the shelf.

**EXT. THE CITY SQUARE — CONTINUOUS**

Ethan and Clara standing in the middle of it as it empties around them.

Neither of them speaks for a while.

**CLARA**
*(and she is not crying; she is furious in a way she has no outlet for)*
Every year of my life.
*(beat)*
My mother used to stand in the kitchen. She'd turn the radio off first.

**ETHAN**
I know.

**CLARA**
*(rounding on him)*
Then what is it for? You've seen the film — **what is it for?**

**ETHAN**
*(and this is the answer, and it is why the scene exists)*
It isn't for anything. That's the point.
*(beat)*
He took the war. He couldn't take what it did to us, so he left it, and it's been
standing in the road at midday every year for thirty years with nowhere to go.
*(beat)*
Four hundred million people observing a silence for something that isn't there.

**CLARA**
*(quietly)*
And he lets it happen.

**ETHAN**
He **schedules** it.
*(beat)*
It's the kindest thing he's ever done. He gave it a day.

> **NOTE —— 本条线的史诗场面（圣经 v2 §一·A 的兑现，第一部 §3 埋的伏笔）。**
>
> **这一场的规模是全片第二大，仅次于加冕**，而它一分钱的特效都不需要 ——
> 它需要的是**人**，静止不动，在很多个地方，同时。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **每一张脸上的悲伤都必须是真的。** 不许有人在偷看手机，不许有人不耐烦，
>   不许有孩子在笑。这个国家不是在走过场 —— **它是真的在难过。**
> - **不许配乐。** 只有风、旗绳、远处的一声车门。
> - 两分钟就是两分钟。**不许剪短。** 观众必须自己撑完那两分钟，
>   而在第九十秒左右，他们会意识到自己也不知道这是为了什么。
> - 最后一句「他给了它一天」之后**不许有反应镜头**。直接切。

## 9. HIS FATHER

**INT. COUNTY RECORDS — PUBLIC TERMINAL — DAY**

Ethan types a name into a public search. **DANIEL VANCE.**

It comes back at once. It is complete. It is boring.

> **VANCE, DANIEL R.** · b. 1938 · Insurance adjuster, Meridian Mutual,
> 1961–1991 · d. 1994, Kingsley County · survived by one son.

He reads it three times.

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — NIGHT**

The spiral notebook. Ethan has it open at a page from years back and he is
reading his own handwriting.

**ETHAN**
He was at the lab until seventy-nine. He kept a bicycle in the hall that he
never rode. He couldn't drink coffee after four or he'd be up all night, and he
was up all night anyway, most nights, and I used to come down and he'd be sitting
in the dark not doing anything.
*(beat)*
He was ninety-one when he died and he looked fifty.

**CLARA**
*(from the doorway, and she has been standing there a while)*
Ethan.

**ETHAN**
Insurance adjuster. Meridian Mutual.

**CLARA**
*(carefully)*
Love — I never met your dad.

Ethan turns round.

**ETHAN**
You met him four times.

**CLARA**
*(and she is not arguing; she is worried about her husband)*
He died before we met. You've told me that.

**ETHAN**
You sat in his kitchen. He gave you the blue bowl. **We have the blue bowl.**

Clara looks, involuntarily, at a blue bowl on the shelf. And there it is: a
perfectly ordinary bowl, which she is now certain came from a shop.

**CLARA**
*(and it comes out before she can stop it)*
…We got that in Portland.

Beat.

Ethan does not shout. He does not cry. He does the thing this man does, which is
that he sits down and he opens the notebook and he starts writing, fast, in his
own hand, everything he can remember about his father, while he still has it.

Clara watches him from the doorway, and she has never been so frightened in her
life, because from where she is standing there is **no way to tell** whether her
husband is being erased or losing his mind — **and there never will be.**

> **NOTE —— 第五记，代价：他自己。这是圣经 v2 §一·A 的最高一级，
> 而它的设计要点是【Clara 无法分辨】。**
>
> 观众知道 Ethan 是对的（因为观众在第一部听见 Helen 说了那个名字）。
> **Clara 不知道。** 而这才是这套系统真正的武器：它不需要说服你的敌人，
> 它只需要让爱你的人合理地怀疑你。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Clara **不许**表现出「我相信你」。她**做不到**，而这不是她的错。
> - **不许**让 Ethan 拿出任何能说服她的证据。他没有。
> - 那只蓝碗**不许**在后面的戏里被证明来自哪里。**永远不揭晓。**

## 10. IN THE CONDITION RECEIVED

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DIRECTOR'S OFFICE — DAY**

A pleasant room. A pleasant man, **BREWER** (60s), who has known Clara nineteen
years and is finding this unbearable.

**BREWER**
It's a routine access review. Everybody in the building gets one.

**CLARA**
Not in nineteen years I haven't.

**BREWER**
*(unhappily)*
No.

He slides a form across. She reads it. It is very short.

**CLARA**
Effective today.

**BREWER**
Effective today. It's not a suspension, Clara, it's a — you keep the post, you
keep the salary, it's the **level four** access that's under review.

**CLARA**
Sub-level four is the only level I have ever worked on.

**BREWER**
I know.
*(beat, and he is genuinely miserable)*
I fought it. I want you to know I fought it, and I want you to know it took
eleven minutes.

**CLARA**
*(evenly)*
Who signed it?

**BREWER**
*(turning the page round, and it is the worst thing in the scene)*
You did. Last March. There's a memo where you propose a review cycle for exactly
this.

Clara looks at her own name.

**CLARA**
*(after a moment)*
That's my signature.

**BREWER**
*(relieved, awful)*
That's what I said. I said, that's Clara all over, that's exactly the sort of
thing she'd propose.

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — LATER**

Clara comes down for the last time with a cardboard box for her own things: a
mug, a cardigan, a photograph of nobody in particular.

She packs it slowly. She checks two humidity gauges she is no longer responsible
for.

Then she stops at the end of an aisle and stands there for a long time.

And then she takes **one film can** off the bench and puts it in the box, and
puts the cardigan on top of it.

She stands there with the box in her arms.

**CLARA**
*(to the room, and it is the only time in the film she is not steady)*
…In the condition received.

She goes.

> **NOTE —— Clara 的转折，而它不是勇气，是【毁誓】。**
>
> 她宣的誓说「以收到时的状态保管」。她把一个罐子从库里拿出来 ——
> **为了保住它，她违背了那条使她成其为她的规则。**
>
> 这一场必须在第 12 场（「线还是你」）之前，否则那一场里她说「我会选那条线」
> 就是空话 —— 观众要知道**她已经付过一次同类的钱了**。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Brewer **不许**是敌人。他是个好人，他真的争取过，而他只争取了十一分钟。
> - 那份备忘录**必须真的是她的签名**（第 3 场的机关，第二次使用）。
> - **不许**有警卫送她出去、不许有人收她的门禁卡的特写、不许配乐。

## 11. NOTHING INSIDE THE RECORD

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — LATER**

Two piles of index cards on the table. One is very tall. One is empty.

**CLARA**
How many now?

**ETHAN**
Four hundred and nine.

**CLARA**
And how many can we prove?

He looks at the piles.

**ETHAN**
Nothing. Not one.
*(beat)*
Every proof I have is a document, and documents are theirs.

**CLARA**
The cans aren't.

**ETHAN**
The cans are ten pieces of metal in a room, and they proved it to **you**, and
they proved it to **me**, and they proved it to Priya Shah for four days.
*(beat)*
An object only convinces the person holding it. He doesn't have to take the
object. He takes the **holder.**

He pushes both piles together. It doesn't matter.

**CLARA**
Then we go public — properly. All of it, everywhere, at once.

**ETHAN**
With what? A man says the flood killed more people than the record says.
*(beat)*
Tomorrow the record says something else again and there was never a man.

**CLARA**
Then we take the Office. Physically. Servers, master index, all of it.

**ETHAN**
And he rewrites the raid.

She turns round.

**CLARA**
*(flat)*
So you're telling me he's already won.

**ETHAN**
I'm telling you he can't be beaten **inside** the record.
*(beat)*
So we stop trying to win the record.

> **NOTE —— 这一场是本条线三个任务里第一个的落点，而它之所以立得住，
> 是因为观众刚刚陪着他们输了四次。** 换成第一部就说这段话，
> 观众只会听见一句断言。

## 12. LIGHT THAT'S ALREADY LEFT

**INT. ETHAN'S HOUSE — KITCHEN — CONTINUOUS**

He stands.

**ETHAN**
Clara — what's the one thing on this earth he has never been able to touch?

**CLARA**
*(after a beat)*
Light that's already left.

Neither of them says anything for a moment, because they have both just heard it.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
It's already gone. That's the whole point of it. It's not **on** the earth.

**ETHAN**
No.
*(beat)*
And there's a hundred and forty years of us out there already. Every radio
broadcast since 1900, going out at three hundred thousand kilometres a second,
in every direction, and **he cannot reach one metre of it.**

**CLARA**
It's also useless. Nobody's listening.

**ETHAN**
*(and this is the pivot of the whole film)*
It doesn't matter whether anybody's listening.
*(beat)*
It matters that it **left.** It matters that there is one copy of us that is
outside his jurisdiction, permanently, and that the copy is **true**, and that
we can add to it.

## 13. YOU ASK METAL

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — ANALOGUE PRESERVATION — NIGHT**

On the bench under a lamp: a small flat coupon of dull grey metal, and a counter.

**CLARA**
What is it?

**ETHAN**
Depleted uranium. Two-thirty-eight.
*(he sets the counter beside it)*
Half-life four and a half billion years. It's been counting since the day it was
poured and it has never once been wrong and nobody has ever been able to argue
with it.

**CLARA**
*(understanding)*
You stop asking documents.

**ETHAN**
You ask metal.

## 14. ONE DISH IS ONE THING TO CORRECT

**INT. NATIONAL ARCHIVES — DEEP STACK — NIGHT**

A map of the world on the bench, weighted at the corners with film cans.

**ETHAN**
Seventy-metre dish. Ninety-minute uncommitted window on Thursday. I can get us
the window.

**CLARA**
*(reading the map, and she is the one who breaks it)*
And on Friday there's a report saying the window was used for a calibration
sweep.

**ETHAN**
The signal's already gone by then.

**CLARA**
*(and this is why she is the custodian and he is not)*
Yes. And there'll be one dish, one log, one duty officer and one recording ——
and every single one of those is a **document.**
*(beat)*
He doesn't have to catch the light, Ethan. He only has to correct the four
people who know it was sent. And then it's out there, true, permanent —
*(beat)*
— and nobody on this planet knows it happened.

Long silence.

**ETHAN**
*(slowly)*
…Then it can't be one dish.

**CLARA**
No.

**ETHAN**
It has to be enough of them that correcting them all is a **bigger job than the
thing itself.**

**CLARA**
*(already pulling a directory off the shelf)*
There are nineteen apertures over sixty metres. Eleven countries.
*(beat)*
And about four hundred thousand amateurs with a licence and a rooftop.

**ETHAN**
Can you reach them?

**CLARA**
*(and for the first time in the film, she smiles)*
Ethan. They print a **magazine.**

> **NOTE —— 全球第三幕的诞生，而它是被【失败】逼出来的，不是被灵感想到的。**
> 圣经 v2 §一·E：第三幕必须是全球的，而这一场是它唯一的入口。
>
> **NOTE:** 「他们印一本杂志」—— 这条线最后的武器是纸质业余无线电月刊。
> **不许**改成加密通讯、暗网、或任何数字方案。**数字是他的。**

## 15. THEY PRINT A MAGAZINE

**INT. A BUNGALOW — BACK ROOM — DAY**

A room that is ninety per cent equipment and ten per cent house. Racks, dials,
a straight key worn shiny, QSL cards pinned three deep over every wall.

**HAROLD BEAK** (70s) makes tea and does not stop talking.

**HAROLD**
—so I've been licensed since I was fourteen, which was before the moon, and in
that time I have spoken to two hundred and eleven countries and forty-one
people I actually liked.

**CLARA**
Mr Beak —

**HAROLD**
Harold.

**CLARA**
Harold. If somebody wanted to reach every licensed operator in this country ——

**HAROLD**
*(instantly, and he taps a slim printed monthly on the table)*
Page nine.

**ETHAN**
Just like that?

**HAROLD**
*(delighted to be asked)*
Son, we've been doing this since before there was anything else. There's no
server. There's no company. There's no **account.**
*(he picks up the magazine and flaps it)*
It goes in the post. It arrives in eleven thousand houses. If you want it to go
further you ring three people and they ring three people, and by Sunday it's in
Japan.

**CLARA**
And if somebody wanted to stop it.

**HAROLD**
*(and he laughs like a drain)*
Stop **what?** There's nothing to stop. There's a man in Leicester with a
duplicator.

He puts a mug down in front of each of them.

**HAROLD (CONT'D)**
*(and now, without any change of tone, he is completely serious)*
Now. You've both come a very long way to ask an old man about a newsletter, and
neither of you has asked me a single thing about radio.
*(beat)*
So what is it you actually want to send?

Ethan and Clara look at each other.

> **NOTE —— 第三幕的地基，而它是一间平房里的一位老先生。**
>
> **这是全片对「他们控制不了什么」最有力的一次说明**，而说明它的方式是
> 一个人在泡茶。没有服务器、没有公司、没有账号 —— 只有一本寄到一万一千户
> 人家的印刷月刊。
>
> **NOTE (铁律 6):** 死写。**不许**把业余无线电拍成地下抵抗组织：
> 有窗帘、有茶、有猫、有墙上钉了三层的 QSL 卡。**这是一项爱好。**
>
> **禁止**：不许有人压低声音；不许拉窗帘；不许有人往窗外看。

## 16. THE LINE OR YOU

**INT. A STAIRWELL — NIGHT**

Concrete, green paint, a round window. They sit on the steps like students.

**CLARA**
When it's finished there'll be one copy of everything.

**ETHAN**
And one copy is one thing to correct.

Beat.

**CLARA**
I'm going to say something and I want to say it now, in a stairwell, when nobody
has to do anything about it.

**ETHAN**
All right.

**CLARA**
Somebody has to be at that dish. On the day. In the room.
*(beat)*
And whoever is in that room is the one person the correction has to go through.

**ETHAN**
I know.

**CLARA**
*(and she does not soften it, because softening it would be a lie, and she has
taken an oath about those)*
I swore I'd hold the line. If it comes to the line or you, **I'm going to pick
the line.**

Ethan does not flinch. He takes it, and he sits with it, and what he says back is
the reason this marriage is worth two hours of anybody's evening.

**ETHAN**
Thank you.

**CLARA**
*(and now it cracks, a little)*
Don't.

**ETHAN**
No — I mean it.
*(beat)*
You could have let me find out on Thursday.

He puts his hand over hers on the step and they sit there in the green light.

**CLARA**
*(after a long time, barely)*
Whatever we're going to do, we should decide now.
*(beat)*
While we can still tell it was us who decided.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**

> **NOTE —— 圣经 v2 §二要求的对立，而它的形式是【提前告知】而不是背叛。**
>
> Clara 不会在第三幕临阵倒戈 —— **她现在就说了**，在一段楼梯上，
> 在还没有人必须为此做任何事的时候。这比背叛难受得多，
> 也比背叛更配得上这两个人物。
>
> **NOTE:** 沿用已拍素材 `S4-1-stairs-two` / `S4-2-her-hands` / `S7-1-two-lights`。

> **PART TWO (`earth`) ENDS → PART THREE: `THE CORONATION AND THE WINDOW`**
>
> 两条线在这里合流：同一座七十米天线，同一个星期四，同一个九十分钟窗口。
> 从这一侧进入第三部的观众，知道**为什么必须是十九座天线**；
> 从 `mars` 侧进入的观众，知道**xÈth 是怎么发现光的问题的**。
> **两边都不知道对方那一半。**
