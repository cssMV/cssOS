## 1. THE TWELFTH DAY

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — PUBLIC HALL — MORNING**

Not a ministry. A converted post office with the good bones left in: high
windows, a tiled floor, light coming down in slabs. Somebody has put flowers on
the counter and nobody has taken them away.

A queue of nine. A water cooler. A child under a chair.

> **NOTE —— 铁律 6（没写的会被类型套路填满）：死写。**
> 无制服、无黑色、无徽章、无金属探测器、无排队护栏。
> **有花、有饮水机、有小孩。** 引擎默认给冷色调政府大厅 —— 那是错的。

**JUNE** works a counter with her sleeves pushed up and a pen behind her ear. She
is fast and she is warm and she is on her twelfth day.

**JUNE**
*(sliding a sheet across)*
That's yours. Keep it. If anybody ever tells you a different number, that's the
one that's signed.

**AN OLD MAN**
And it's the only one now.

**JUNE**
It's the only one now.

He looks at it a long time. Then he folds it into his coat like money.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — STAFF ROOM — LATER**

Instant coffee. A radio. Four colleagues, all pleasant, all mid-conversation.

**COLLEAGUE**
Twelfth day. How's it going.

**JUNE**
*(honestly)*
Better than the job before.

**COLLEAGUE**
Everybody says that.
*(cheerfully, going)*
Nobody ever leaves.

June laughs. Then, half a second later, doesn't.

## 2. ELEVEN MINUTES

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — INTERVIEW ROOM 3 — DAY**

Two chairs, a table, a window with a tree in it. No recording equipment visible.

**MRS. AUDREY LEIGH** (60s) sits with her handbag on her knees, the way people do
when they have decided not to stay long.

**JUNE**
Mrs Leigh. There's no form. I'll write it after.

**AUDREY**
I've read what it says on the leaflet.

**JUNE**
The leaflet's not very good.
*(sitting)*
Just tell me what you'd like.

Audrey takes a breath and delivers something she has clearly rehearsed for weeks,
very steadily, and gets almost to the end of it.

**AUDREY**
My daughter was twenty-nine. Her name was Nell. On the eighth of March I let
myself into her flat because she hadn't answered her phone since the Friday.
*(beat)*
I want the eleven minutes.

**JUNE**
The eleven minutes.

**AUDREY**
From the door to the ambulance. Eleven minutes.
*(and here she becomes very precise, because precision is the only thing holding
her up)*
I don't want to lose her. I want everything. I want her at four with the tooth,
and the row we had at Christmas, and her voice, and her being **cross** with me,
I want all of it, I'd fight you for all of it.
*(beat)*
I want the eleven minutes gone.

Silence.

**AUDREY (CONT'D)**
Because that's what comes. That's what's there when I wake up. Not her.
Eleven minutes of a room.
*(beat)*
I've had four years of it and it hasn't got one second shorter.

June does not reach across the table. She does not say she's sorry. She writes
one line on a pad.

**JUNE**
Eleven minutes, from the front door.

**AUDREY**
From the front door.

**JUNE**
You'll know she died. You'll know the date. You'll know you were the one who
found her.

**AUDREY**
*(nodding hard)*
Yes.

**JUNE**
You just won't be able to go back in.

Audrey puts her hand over her mouth. It is the first time she has moved.

**AUDREY**
Can you actually do that?

**JUNE**
Yes.

---

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — INTERVIEW ROOM 3 — LATER**

Audrey sits alone for a moment with her coat already on.

She is not transformed. She is not glowing. She looks like a tired woman in a
municipal room.

She picks up her handbag. Stops. Puts a hand flat on the table.

**AUDREY**
*(to nobody, testing it)*
Nell.

And her face does something complicated — and what wins, in the end, is that she
**smiles.**

She goes.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — PUBLIC HALL — CONTINUOUS**

June watches her cross the tiled floor and go out through the doors into ordinary
daylight, and stop on the step, and look up at the weather like somebody who has
just noticed there is weather.

June stands there a while.

> **NOTE —— 这是全条线、也可能是全片最重要的一场戏，
> 而它必须被拍成一件不折不扣的好事。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许**有阴森配乐、不许有反打暗示代价、不许有人在门外交换眼神。
> - **不许**让 Audrey 事后显得空洞、迟钝、或「少了什么」。她**没有**少什么。
>   她少的是十一分钟的一个房间。
> - **不许**让 June 露出怀疑。她此刻百分之百认为自己做了一件好事，
>   **而观众必须同意她。**
> - Audrey 出场一次，此后全片不再出现。**不许**在第三部把她拿回来当反例。
>
> **理由（圣经 v2 §一·D）：** 天平上 xÈth 那一侧必须真的有重量。
> 如果观众从头到尾都知道那套系统是骗局，两个结局就不是两难，
> 而是一道有标准答案的题。**这场戏就是那个重量。**
>
> 「十一分钟」这个设计是刻意的：她要的不是遗忘女儿，是**外科手术式地
> 切掉一个房间**。所有人都会同意。

## 3. THE MAN WHO SAYS NO

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — UPPER FLOOR — DAY**

An open floor of desks and trolleys. Paper going one way, pulp coming the other.

**xÈth** moves through it slowly. Charcoal suit, plain tie, **no security on the
floor.** He stops at desks. He knows names. He is not performing it; he simply
does it.

He arrives beside June, who is elbow-deep in a trolley.

**xÈth**
Mrs Leigh.

**JUNE**
*(surprised)*
You read them?

**xÈth**
I read all of them.
*(he picks a folder out of her trolley, glances, puts it in a different pile)*
Eleven minutes. Not the daughter, not the grief, not the guilt — **the room.**
*(beat)*
Whose idea was that? Hers or yours?

**JUNE**
Hers.

**xÈth**
*(and he is genuinely pleased)*
Good. They're always better at it than we are. We'd have taken far too much.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — INTERVIEW ROOM 1 — LATER**

**HOLBROOK** (50s), a big competent man in a good coat, sits across from xÈth
himself. June is at the side of the room with a pad and has just realised what
she is watching.

**HOLBROOK**
—and it's not for me, I want to be clear about that, it's for my wife. She has to
live with me and I'm not fit to live with.

**xÈth**
Nineteen years ago.

**HOLBROOK**
Nineteen years in March.

**xÈth**
You were driving.

**HOLBROOK**
*(steady)*
I was driving.

**xÈth**
And you'd had two.

**HOLBROOK**
I'd had two.

xÈth nods slowly. He is not enjoying this and he is not hurrying it.

**xÈth**
Mr Holbrook. What is it you're asking me to take?

**HOLBROOK**
The whole of it. The road, the boy, the court. All of it.

**xÈth**
And the boy's mother? She keeps hers.

**HOLBROOK**
*(a long beat)*
That's not for me to —

**xÈth**
It isn't. No.
*(beat)*
I'm going to refuse you.

The room stops. June's pen stops.

**HOLBROOK**
*(and he is not angry; he is a man who expected this)*
Right.

**xÈth**
Do you want to know why? Most people don't. You can go without it.

**HOLBROOK**
Tell me.

**xÈth**
*(and he leans forward, and this is the most direct he is in the entire film)*
Because you've carried it correctly for nineteen years. Not well — **correctly.**
You didn't ask for it to be smaller. You asked to be able to stand in a kitchen
with your wife.
*(beat)*
A man who carries it correctly is the only kind of man I can't help. Because
what's hurting you isn't the memory. It's that you're **right about yourself**,
and I don't take that. I've never taken that.
*(beat)*
If I take it, there's a man in the world who did that and doesn't know. I won't
make one of those. Not while I can help it.

Holbrook sits with it.

**HOLBROOK**
So what do I do.

**xÈth**
*(and he says it very simply)*
You keep going. And you tell your wife I said no, and you tell her I said why.

He stands, and shakes the man's hand, and holds it a second longer than he needs
to.

> **NOTE —— 圣经铁律「反派必须堂堂正正赢下一场」在这条线里的兑现。**
>
> 他不但会拒绝，他的拒绝**比我们的道德还讲究**。这场戏之后，观众再也不能
> 用「他只是想抹掉一切」打发他 —— **他有界限，他执行界限，而他的界限
> 站得住。**
>
> **禁止**：不许让他这番话显得像话术；不许配乐；不许在门口给 June
> 「若有所思」的反打。**她只是在做记录，而她记录的是一件对的事。**

## 4. THE ONE SHE REFUSES

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — INTERVIEW ROOM 3 — DAY**

**DECLAN** (mid-20s) sits with his knee going, in a jacket he has put on for this.

**DECLAN**
It's not a big one. I know you get big ones.

**JUNE**
There's no size.

**DECLAN**
*(relieved, rushing)*
Right. Okay. So — eight months. Her name's Ríona, and I don't want her **gone**,
I'm not mad, I just want the — I want the twenty minutes on the stairs when she
told me. That's all. Twenty minutes.

June writes nothing.

**DECLAN**
*(faltering)*
That's the same as the woman. I read about the woman. The eleven minutes.

**JUNE**
It is nearly the same.

**DECLAN**
So —

**JUNE**
*(and she has not planned to say this; it arrives)*
Declan. What did you do the next morning?

**DECLAN**
*(thrown)*
…Went to work.

**JUNE**
And the morning after that?

**DECLAN**
Went to work.

**JUNE**
And how long have you been doing that.

**DECLAN**
Eight months.
*(beat, and it comes out annoyed, which means it is true)*
Every day. Eight months. That's the whole point, I'm —

**JUNE**
You're carrying it.

**DECLAN**
Yeah.

**JUNE**
*(quietly)*
Correctly.

Silence. He does not know what that means and he knows it is bad news.

**JUNE (CONT'D)**
I'm going to refuse you.

**DECLAN**
*(and he is a young man, so it comes out badly)*
You don't get to — that's **mine**, that happened to **me** —

**JUNE**
It is yours. That's why.
*(beat)*
In four years you will be able to hear her name in a room and not do anything
with your face. And you'll have got there **yourself**, and you'll know you can
do it, because you'll have done it once.
*(beat)*
If I take the stairs, you'll never have done it once.

He sits with it, furious.

**DECLAN**
The woman got hers.

**JUNE**
The woman had been carrying hers for four years and it hadn't got **one second**
shorter.
*(beat)*
Yours is getting shorter. You just can't feel it yet.

**DECLAN**
*(standing, hard)*
You don't know that.

**JUNE**
No.

He goes. The door doesn't slam, which is worse.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**

June comes out and stands against the wall for a moment.

And what is on her face is not doubt.

**It is that she is very good at this.**

> **NOTE —— June 这条线的转折，而它不是堕落，是【正确】。**
>
> 她刚刚用 xÈth 的推理拒绝了一个人，用得**比他还准**，而且她是对的 ——
> 观众也知道她是对的。她走出门时脸上的东西是**胜任感**，不是愧疚。
>
> **禁止**：不许配乐提示「她变了」；不许给她一个后悔的反打；
> 不许让 Declan 在后面的戏里回来证明她错了。**她没错。这就是这场戏的
> 全部恐怖 —— 她是被【正确】腐蚀的。**

## 5. THE FLOOR

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — SERVICE LIFT — DAY**

June, alone, a folder under her arm, punches a button. The lift has more buttons
than the building has floors. She has been told to go to **B4**.

It goes down for longer than four floors take.

**INT. THE FLOOR — GALLERY — CONTINUOUS**

The doors open onto a narrow steel gallery, and beyond the rail there is no wall
for a long way.

Below her:

**A HALL THE SIZE OF A STADIUM, AND IT IS FULL OF DESKS.**

Rank after rank after rank of them, in a strict grid, going away under a ceiling
of soft even light until the far end is simply haze.

At every desk, a person. Working.

Nobody is shouting. Nobody is marching. There are no uniforms and no insignia.
There is the enormous, soft, continuous sound of **ten thousand people doing
paperwork.**

June does not move for some time.

A MAN comes along the gallery with a trolley, sees her, and stops — courteous,
unhurried, entirely pleasant.

**MAN**
First time down?

**JUNE**
Yes.

**MAN**
*(nodding at the hall, fondly, the way you'd nod at your garden)*
It's the backlog. We're about eleven years into it.

He goes on. She watches him go, and then she looks back down at the hall, and
then — and this is the moment — she looks along the gallery at three more of them
coming the other way.

Same unhurried pace. Same courtesy. Same tilt of the head.

**And she realises she cannot tell which of the people she has worked beside for
twelve days are which.**

Not because they look alike. **Because they move alike.**

> **NOTE (圣经 v2 §三·场面 3) —— 本片唯一一次「舰队登场」，而它是一个办公厅。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **无制服、无徽章、无武器、无队列、无口号、无监工。**
> - 光要**好**。这不是地牢，是一间光线极佳的大办公室。
> - 那个推车的人**必须真的和气**。他没有威胁她，他在跟她聊工作进度。
> - 恐怖来自**规模**与**一致性**，不来自恶意。
>
> **这一格必须与序章开场的火星城市网格是同一件事的两个尺度。**
> 同样的严格网格、同样的整齐、同样的一望无际。观众要在这一秒把两张图接上：
> **那颗星球就是这个办公厅办完的样子。**

## 6. WHAT THE BACKLOG IS

**INT. THE FLOOR — GALLERY — CONTINUOUS**

June has not left the rail. The trolley man is coming back the other way.

**JUNE**
Can I ask you something?

**MAN**
*(stopping, happy to)*
Course.

**JUNE**
The backlog. Eleven years. How many applications is that?

**MAN**
*(genuinely puzzled)*
Applications?

**JUNE**
From the offices. From people.

**MAN**
*(and he laughs, kindly, the way you laugh when somebody has got the scale of
your job charmingly wrong)*
Oh — no. That's, what, four, five thousand a year? Upstairs handles that. That's
not **work.**

Beat.

**JUNE**
Then what's this.

**MAN**
*(turning and looking out over the hall with something like modest pride)*
This is everything before.

**JUNE**
Before.

**MAN**
Before there was anywhere to apply **to.**
*(he shrugs, cheerfully)*
Couple of hundred thousand years of people, and not one of them ever got asked.
*(beat)*
Doesn't seem right, does it? That you'd have to fill in a form to be let off.

He goes on with his trolley.

June stands at the rail above ten thousand desks.

> **NOTE —— 全条线最冷的一句话，而它是由一个和气的人当作工作量抱怨说出来的。**
>
> **申请是可选的。** 那个大厅在处理的不是申请者，是**所有人**，而且是
> 追溯性的。这一格把整套系统从「一项服务」翻成「一项工程」，
> 而说话的人从头到尾都觉得自己在讲一件仁慈的事 —— **他确实在讲一件仁慈的事。**
>
> **禁止**：不许有音效强调；不许推镜；不许让他意识到自己说了什么。
> 他说完就推着车走了。

## 7. THE ONE WHO LEAVES

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — STAFF ROOM — EVENING**

**PARRISH** (50s), coat on, is putting her pass and her lanyard on the table in a
neat little pile, the way you leave keys.

**JUNE**
*(coming in for coffee, stopping)*
…Parrish?

**PARRISH**
Nineteen years. They give you a card.
*(she holds it up; it is a card)*
They give you a card.

**JUNE**
You're leaving.

**PARRISH**
I'm leaving.

June sits down. Parrish, who did not intend to have this conversation, sits down
too, because she has been waiting eleven months for somebody to ask.

**PARRISH (CONT'D)**
Do you know what my number is? Four thousand one hundred and six.
*(beat)*
That's how many I've done. Personally. With my own hands.

**JUNE**
That's —

**PARRISH**
And do you know how many have come back?
*(beat)*
None. Not one. In nineteen years, not one single person has ever come back to
that counter and asked to have it returned.

**JUNE**
*(and she means it, and it is the obvious thing to say)*
That's the whole argument, isn't it. That's the proof it works.

**PARRISH**
*(and she looks at June with something almost like pity)*
June.
*(beat)*
**They can't.**
*(beat)*
They don't know there's anything to come back for.

Silence in the staff room. The radio is on somewhere.

**JUNE**
*(carefully)*
That's not the same as it not working.

**PARRISH**
No. It isn't. I've had eleven months to find the difference and I can't.

She stands and puts her coat straight.

**PARRISH (CONT'D)**
I did mine in year two. Staff are entitled, did they tell you that? Free. It's
one of the perks.
*(beat)*
I don't know what it was.

**JUNE**
*(quietly)*
No.

**PARRISH**
*(and this is the line that ends the scene, and she says it lightly, at the door,
because she has decided not to cry in this building)*
I'd like it back.

She goes.

June sits alone in the staff room with a coffee she has not made.

> **NOTE —— 本条线唯一一次反方陈词，而且它必须赢下这一场。**
>
> 「没有人回来要过」既是这套系统最强的证据，也是它最深的陷阱，
> **而电影不裁决。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Parrish **不许**说破、不许指控、不许有阴谋论。她只是一个做了十九年、
>   然后不想再做的人。
> - 她**不许**知道自己被拿走了什么。她永远不会知道。**这就是那个论点。**
> - June **不许**当场被说服。她只是坐在那儿。
> - **不许**配乐。

## 8. YOU MAY GO

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — MORNING**

He is signing things. June stands. He does not make her wait long.

**xÈth**
Parrish has gone.

**JUNE**
Yes.

**xÈth**
Nineteen years. She was very good.
*(he signs another)*
You may go too.

**JUNE**
*(caught)*
Sir?

**xÈth**
*(putting the pen down, and giving her his whole attention, which is worse)*
I'm not being rhetorical. There's no notice and no interview. I'll write you a
reference this morning and every word of it will be true, and nobody will ever
ring you about this building again.
*(beat)*
People come here and then one day they can't. It isn't a failure. Parrish did
four thousand of them; that's four thousand more than most.

June says nothing for a moment.

**JUNE**
Are you asking me to?

**xÈth**
No.

**JUNE**
Are you telling me I should?

**xÈth**
*(honestly)*
I don't know. That's why I'm offering.

Long beat. And when she answers, she is not defensive and she is not
brainwashed; she is a person who has seen something and is refusing to un-see it.

**JUNE**
Last Tuesday I watched a woman go out through those doors and stop on the step
and look up at the sky.
*(beat)*
Like she'd never noticed there was weather.

**xÈth**
Mrs Leigh.

**JUNE**
Mrs Leigh.
*(beat)*
Parrish is sad, and she's right, and I can't answer her.
*(beat)*
But I'm not going to un-see that woman on the step because I lost an argument in
a staff room.

Silence. He looks at her for a good while.

And then he picks the pen back up.

**xÈth**
Good.

He goes back to signing. That is the entire response.

June stands there half a second too long, waiting for something else, and there
isn't anything else, and she goes.

> **NOTE —— 「June 留在 xÈth 身边到最后」这个决定的承重场次（圣经 v1 §待拍板 2，
> v2 保留）。没有这一场，她的留下是默认；有了这一场，她的留下是「选择」。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 那扇门**必须是真的开着的**。他不许有任何挽留、暗示、条件、威胁。
> - June **不许**说出任何被洗脑的话。她的理由是**一个她亲眼看见的具体事实**，
>   而观众也看见了那件事。
> - 他最后只说「Good」然后继续签字。**不许给他反打，不许让他笑，
>   不许让他说任何深刻的话。** 那半秒的空落就是这场戏的余味。

## 9. HER FATHER'S KEY

**INT. JUNE'S FLAT — NIGHT**

Small, decent, under-furnished in the way of somebody who has moved recently and
is not sure she is staying.

She eats standing up. She washes one plate.

On top of the wardrobe there is a cardboard box with a strip of tape across it
that has gone yellow. She has looked at it before. She looks at it now.

She does not get it down.

She takes her keys out of her coat instead and sits on the end of the bed with
them in her hand.

Among her own keys — flat, office, a padlock she can't place — there is a **car
key.** Old. Rubber grip perished. Not for any car she owns.

She turns it over once and puts the keys on the bedside table and turns the lamp
off.

> **NOTE —— 三分钟，一句台词都没有。**
>
> 这是 June 唯一一场在建筑之外的戏，而它的全部内容是：**她把一样东西留在
> 钥匙圈上，四年了。**
>
> 与第 10 场（她被拒的理由）扣在一起 —— 那场争吵是关于一辆车的。
> **电影不许把这两件事连起来说给观众听。** 谁看见谁看见。
>
> **禁止**：不许闪回、不许照片、不许她哭、不许配乐。她只是关了灯。

## 10. WHY YOU WERE REFUSED

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — EVENING**

Not an office. A room with a desk, a green-shaded lamp, one chair for visitors, a
trolley of low-priority folders, four white walls — and on one of them, alone,
framed, badly lit: **the Pale Blue Dot.**

> **NOTE (§九·母题「一个像素」):** 那是**他的**圣像，不是人类的。
> 三十五年来他在哪儿办公它就挂在哪儿。**永远不解释。**
> 素材必须与 §6.1 结尾那四秒同源（`INVARIANT-pale-blue-dot.png`）。

June, summoned, in the visitor's chair, holding a folder nobody has asked for.

**xÈth**
*(not looking up from a folder)*
You applied. Four years ago, under your own name, at the Hartley office.

**JUNE**
*(caught completely off guard)*
…Yes.

**xÈth**
And you were refused.

**JUNE**
I was refused.

**xÈth**
By me.

Beat.

**JUNE**
I didn't know that.

**xÈth**
No. You wouldn't.
*(he puts the folder down)*
Do you want to know why? You've been in this building twelve days and you haven't
asked, which is why I'm going to tell you.

**JUNE**
*(and she is suddenly not a member of staff)*
I said something to my father. In a kitchen. About eight weeks before he died,
and it wasn't even a real argument, it was about a **car.**
*(beat)*
And I've thought about it every day for four years and it is the single most
useless thing I own.

**xÈth**
Yes.

**JUNE**
So why not.

**xÈth**
*(gently, and without any triumph at all)*
Because you'd stop being sorry.
*(beat)*
And you are the best person on that floor precisely because you are sorry.
I've watched you take eleven minutes off a woman and not one second more, and I
have men down there with fifteen years' service who'd have taken the whole
daughter and called it kindness.
*(beat)*
I need somebody in this building who is still paying for something. It's the only
quality control I have.

June says nothing.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(and now, and only now, something like apology)*
It's a monstrous thing to do to you, and I did it on purpose, and I would do it
again, and you may say so.

**JUNE**
*(after a long moment)*
…That's the first thing anyone here has said to me that wasn't kind.

**xÈth**
Yes.
*(going back to his folder)*
I thought you'd like it.

## 11. EVERYONE WHO EVER SAID ANYTHING

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

He unrolls something on the desk between them. Not a starfield — **a register.**
Columns of coordinates, and beside each one two dates.

**JUNE**
What am I looking at?

**xÈth**
Everyone who ever said anything.

She reads down the columns. Every row has a second date.

**JUNE**
And the second column is when they stopped.

**xÈth**
Yes.

**JUNE**
*(counting, and stopping counting)*
All of them?

**xÈth**
All of them.

**CUT TO — AND THIS IS WHERE THE FILM OPENS OUT:**

**EXT. SPACE — A SUCCESSION**

Forty thousand nine hundred and twenty-one worlds.

Not a starfield. **A list, made of places.** One after another after another: a
green one, a banded one, a small hot one close to its star, a moon with a sea on
it, a world of terraces, a world of towers, a world with nothing built on it at
all and one enormous transmitting dish standing in a field.

Each one holds for less than a second. Each one is inhabited, or was. Each one is
**quiet.**

And they keep coming, and they keep coming, and they do not stop coming for a
very long time.

> **NOTE (圣经 v2 §三·场面 4):** 二十秒。**不许配旁白、不许打计数器、
> 不许有音乐高潮。** 观众自己会明白这个数字有多大 —— 而他们明白的方式，
> 就是那个序列**结束得太晚**。

**BACK TO — INT. xÈth's ROOM**

June has not moved.

**JUNE**
Something's killing them.

**xÈth**
*(gently, almost sorry)*
No. They finished.

He starts to roll the chart up.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Every one of them worked out how to stop hurting. And then there was nothing
further any of them urgently needed to say.

> **NOTE (§五) —— 四句话。这就是本条线对费米寂静的全部处理。**
> **任何角色都不许讲授大过滤器。** 未来哪一稿在这儿加了一段，删掉那一段。
>
> **NOTE (§2.5 双重读法):** 下一句台词之后，这四万零九百二十一个
> `LAST HEARD` 就有了第二种读法 —— 他们**自己停下了**，还是**他走到了**。
> **电影永远不说是哪一种。**

June asks the only personal question anyone has put to him in a very long time.

**JUNE**
Where are you from?

**xÈth**
*(a beat — he is not evading; he is deciding whether she'll follow)*
Further along.

**JUNE**
Further along what?

**xÈth**
You think it runs in a line.
*(beat)*
It runs like a strip of paper with one twist in it. You can walk the whole way
round and come back on the other side without ever having crossed an edge.

She doesn't know what to do with that. He doesn't help her.

**JUNE**
…And Mars?

**xÈth**
Mars was the last place we stopped.

> **NOTE (§2.5 / §2.6 —— 已进 canon):**
> 两个短回答装下了他全部的宇宙论与全部的族史，**而电影两个都不予证实。**
>
> **禁止**：不许画示意图、不许有人用手比出一个扭转、不许有追问被回答、
> 全片不许有任何人反驳它。**演成一个人在陈述天气。**

## 12. 1977, FROM THE OTHER SIDE OF THE ROOM

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — LATER — NIGHT**

Alone. The trolley of low-priority folders. He works through it the way other men
do a crossword.

He stops.

CLOSE ON: **MINUTES — SOUNDS OF EARTH COMMITTEE — 1977.** An attendance sheet.

**Three signatures, and a footnote.**

He does not move for eleven seconds.

**MATCH CUT TO:**

**INT. ASSEMBLY CLEAN ROOM — DAY — JULY 1977**

*The same room. The same afternoon. The same argument. But we are standing where
we have never stood before — at the back, by the door.*

And from here we can see what we could not see from the bench: **the man in the
doorway.** Arms folded. No paper cap, because he is not here to work.

**THE FOURTH MAN (O.S.)**
Because someone is going to try to take it out.

And xÈth — *laughs.* Once. Quietly. Genuinely amused. The kind of laugh that
gives the others permission.

The room relaxes. The joke has been identified as a joke.

> **NOTE —— 观众此刻已经看过这一格两次**（序章一次，第一部战争胶片里
> 十一帧一次）。**这是第三次，也是唯一一次从他站的位置看过去。**
>
> 三次用的是**同一张基准板**（`scene-cleanroom-1977.png`），只换机位。
> 不重拍、不重新设计。

**BACK TO — INT. xÈth's ROOM**

He is still holding the attendance sheet.

**xÈth**
*(to no one)*
Ah.

That is the entire performance. One syllable.

> **NOTE (§四·节点一):** 他一生唯一的失算，而它五十年了。**演小。**
> 他还不害怕。他是**难为情**。
>
> 而观众现在知道那个音节意味着什么 —— 序章里冷岛小屋那场戏，
> 他说的是同一个字。**上一次说它，他是白赢了一场；这一次，是他发现自己
> 白输了一场。**

## 13. YEAR ONE

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — JUNE'S DESK — NIGHT**

A logistics packet. Room allocations, seating, an order of ceremony, transport
for four hundred and nine people.

June works through it fast — she is good at this and it is restful.

She stops at the header.

It reads:

> **ORDER OF CEREMONY · 1 JANUARY · YEAR ONE**

She types a correction, because it is obviously a typing error, and sends it
back.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — JUNE'S DESK — MORNING**

The packet is back in her tray. She opens it.

> **ORDER OF CEREMONY · 1 JANUARY · YEAR ONE**

She corrects it again, more carefully this time, and adds a note.

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — JUNE'S DESK — NIGHT**

The packet is back in her tray.

> **ORDER OF CEREMONY · 1 JANUARY · YEAR ONE**

She sits looking at it for a while with her hand on the mouse.

Then she takes her hand off the mouse, and files it, and does the next one.

> **NOTE —— 加冕的埋点，第三部兑现。全片最经济的一次结构反转：**
>
> **这一次是【她】在试图更正记录，而记录拒绝了她。**
>
> 三次同一个页眉，同一份确定性道具，逐像素相同（铁律 7.6）。
> **禁止**：不许有人来跟她解释；不许她去问；不许配乐。
> 她第三次把手从鼠标上拿开 —— 那就是全场戏的表演。

## 14. THE SCHEDULE

**INT. THE OFFICE OF REMEMBRANCE — UPPER FLOOR — NIGHT**

Empty floor. One light on over one desk. June, alone, cross-checking the
consolidation queue against public registers, because that is what she was asked
to do.

She finds something dull and flags it because it is her job.

**JUNE**
*(reading, bored)*
Deep Space Network. Ninety-minute uncommitted window, Thursday. Requested by a
federal records office.
*(beat)*
That's us. Isn't it?
*(beat)*
…No. That's not us.

## 15. THE FIRST TIME HE HURRIES

**INT. xÈth's ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

He reads the schedule note. Unhurried. Almost bored.

**xÈth**
Let them.

**JUNE**
Sir —

**xÈth**
A man is going to stand in a desert and read a list of dead people into a dish.
*(mildly)*
On Friday there will be a report about it, and the report will say something
else, and by the following Thursday there will not have been a Thursday.

He goes back to his folders.

June, who is not stupid, says the next thing because she genuinely wants to
understand the work.

**JUNE**
Where does it go? While we're correcting the report — the part that already went
out.

xÈth's hand stops on the folder.

Beat.

**xÈth**
*(not looking up)*
Say that again.

**JUNE**
The — the signal's already travelling, isn't it? By the time we file anything
it's a day out. Two.
*(beat)*
Do we have anything that reaches that?

He stands up.

It is the first time in this film that xÈth moves faster than a walk, and it
should be genuinely alarming, because until now he has moved like weather.

**xÈth**
Thursday.

**JUNE**
Nine-forty, for ninety minutes —

**xÈth**
*(already at the door)*
Get me the dish.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**

> **NOTE —— 这条线的枢纽，也是本片最讽刺的一处结构：**
> 那个把「已经离场的光」这件事第一次递到他面前的人，**是他自己选中的、
> 唯一一个他故意留着痛的人。** 她问那句话，正因为她还在真心想把工作做好。
>
> **他留着她的痛作为质检，而质检替他找到了他唯一的漏洞。**
>
> **NOTE —— 那份文件夹留在桌上没拿。** 一个一百四十年没快步走过的人，
> 走的时候把手里的活扔下了。

> **PART TWO (`mars`) ENDS → PART THREE: `THE CORONATION AND THE WINDOW`**
>
> 两条线在这里合流：同一座七十米天线，同一个星期四，同一个九十分钟窗口。
> **June 留在 xÈth 身边** —— 她没有被骗，她是真的认为那份仁慈有道理，
> 而这比让她倒戈更难受（圣经 v1 §待拍板 2，v2 保留）。
