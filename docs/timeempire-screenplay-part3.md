## 1. THURSDAY, 05:50

**EXT. A DESERT — BEFORE DAWN**

Cold. Blue. A seventy-metre dish standing against the last stars, tipped back,
looking at nothing yet.

A chain-link fence. A gate. One car parked outside it with two people asleep in
the front seats.

**EXT. A BUNGALOW — SAME TIME — DIFFERENT TIME ZONE**

**HAROLD BEAK** in a dressing gown, in a room that is ninety per cent equipment,
pouring tea and turning things on. QSL cards three deep on every wall.

He puts his glasses on and looks at the clock.

**HAROLD**
*(to the cat)*
Right.

**INT. A GOVERNMENT BUILDING — CORRIDOR — SAME TIME**

Trolleys of folded chairs going one way. Florists going the other. A man on a
stepladder adjusting a light over a doorway.

**JUNE** walks through it with a clipboard, doing the job she has done for eleven
days: rooms, seating, transport for four hundred and nine people.

She stops at a table where the printed programmes have arrived in boxes.

She takes one off the top.

> **INSERT — THE PROGRAMME**
>
> **ORDER OF CEREMONY · 1 JANUARY · YEAR ONE**

She looks at it for a moment. Then she puts it back on the pile, squares the
pile, and goes on.

## 2. NINETEEN APERTURES

**EXT./INT. THE WORLD — MORNING — MONTAGE**

Not a control room. Nineteen of them, and none of them look like a control room.

**A dish in a green valley.** Two women in anoraks and a thermos, walking up a
track with a rolled paper chart under one arm.

**A dish on a plateau.** Four students and a lecturer who has told the university
it is a calibration exercise, which is nearly true.

**A radio club in a school gymnasium.** Forty people, average age sixty, folding
tables, urns of tea, a printed sheet going hand to hand.

**A rooftop in a city.** One man, one wire aerial, one photocopy.

**A fishing boat.** A set bolted to a bulkhead, and a boy holding the sheet
because his grandfather's hands aren't good in the cold.

**A dish in a desert at first light.** The gate. The car.

> **NOTE (圣经 v2 §三·场面 6) —— 全球第三幕的形态，而它必须【一点也不像
> 军事行动】。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 没有一个地点是控制中心。没有大屏幕、没有倒计时钟、没有戴耳机的技术人员
>   报读参数。
> - **全部是普通人在一个普通早上做一件他们看过说明书的事。**
> - 每一处都要有**日常物件**：保温瓶、折叠桌、茶炉、猫、孙子。
> - 那张纸**必须是同一份印刷品**（确定性道具），在十九个地方以不同的磨损
>   程度出现。

**INT. THE BUNGALOW — CONTINUOUS**

Harold puts on headphones and picks up the printed sheet — the same sheet — and
lays it flat, and smooths it once with the side of his hand.

**HAROLD**
*(to nobody, quite happily)*
Well. Fifty-six years for this, apparently.

## 3. THE CORONATION, FIRST PASS

**INT. THE GREAT HALL — MORNING**

It is beautiful, and it is legal, and it is not frightening at all.

Four hundred people in good coats. Winter light through high windows. No banners,
no eagles, no massed ranks. A string quartet doing its job well at the side.

People are pleased to be here. A woman in her seventies has brought her
granddaughter and is pointing things out to her. Two men who evidently loathe
each other are being cordial by a window. Somebody's phone goes off and eight
people smile about it.

A YOUNG USHER moves along a row handing out programmes, and runs out three seats
short, and mouths an apology, and goes to get more.

> **NOTE —— 这一段的功能是让观众【想在场】。** 如果观众看这一场时的感觉是
> 「我不想在那间屋子里」，第三遍那一记就废了。

The quartet finishes a phrase. The room settles by itself, without being asked.

A CLERK OF THE COUNCIL, elderly and nervous, reads from a folder at a lectern.
He has clearly practised. He is going to get through it.

**CLERK**
My lords, ladies, members of the Assembly. Item four.
*(he adjusts his glasses, and the room is entirely quiet, and it is a nice
moment for him)*
The Assembly having considered the report of the Committee on the Instrument —

**CLERK (CONT'D)**
—and the Assembly, having sat in continuous session, and being satisfied as to
the instrument and the seals, resolves that the office and the style be
**consolidated**, and that the term be —

He turns the page.

**CLERK (CONT'D)**
— **indefinite.**

A murmur, not a gasp. People look at each other. Somebody near the back nods, the
way you nod at a sensible amendment.

The quartet plays. People applaud politely.

At the front, one chair on the dais is empty.

Nobody looks at it.

> **NOTE —— 加冕第一遍。**
>
> **死规矩：这一场必须【美】。** 好大衣、好光线、好音乐。没有一个人害怕，
> 没有一个人被强迫，没有一个卫兵。**这是一场四百人自愿出席的、
> 程序完全正确的典礼。**
>
> 「indefinite」这个词从一位紧张的老书记员嘴里念出来，而全场的反应是
> **礼貌的点头**。**恐怖全部由那个点头承担。**
>
> **不许**给空椅子任何特写、任何推镜、任何反打。它在画面里，仅此而已。

## 4. THE EMPTY CHAIR

**INT. A CAR — MOTORWAY — MORNING**

xÈth in the back, coat on his knees, reading a single sheet of paper. **June**
beside him, because he asked her this morning and gave her ninety seconds to
decide.

Out of the window: a country going to work.

**JUNE**
*(finally)*
Sir — it's today.

**xÈth**
It is.

**JUNE**
You're supposed to be —

**xÈth**
*(pleasantly, not looking up)*
I'll have been there.

He turns the sheet over.

**JUNE**
*(and she is not being clever; she genuinely wants to know)*
Then why are we in a car?

**xÈth**
*(and now he does look up, and he answers her honestly, which he almost always
does)*
Because that hall is a room full of paper, and I can be in it whenever I like.
*(beat)*
And what is going to happen in the desert this morning is the only thing in one
hundred and forty years that I will not be able to attend afterwards.

He goes back to the sheet.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
*(mildly)*
Also I should like to see him.

## 5. 09:40

Older than you expect. Green paint, rack after rack of equipment from four

**ETHAN** at a desk with a spiral notebook, a printed sheet, and a small flat

**CLARA** at the door, which she is holding open with her foot because it locks.

On the wall clock: 09:39.

**CLARA**
Nineteen?

**ETHAN**
*(reading a paper list)*
Seventeen confirmed. Two didn't answer and I'm not waiting for them.

**CLARA**
Harold?

**ETHAN**
*(a small smile, the last one in the film)*
Harold was up at five.

09:40.

Something enormous outside begins, very slowly, to move.

## 6. THE NAMES

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Ethan pulls the microphone toward him. He does not make a speech.

**ETHAN**
This is a list of people and things that were taken out.
*(beat)*
We can't prove any of it. That's why we're saying it out here.

He begins to read.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Kellerman colliery, nineteen thirty-one. Eighty-four men. The record says
sixty-one.
*(beat)*
The valley at Ardenne. There was a town in it.
*(beat)*
Priya Shah wrote eleven hundred words on a Sunday.

**MONTAGE — NINETEEN PLACES, ONE LIST**

And now the film opens all the way out, and stays open.

**The green valley.** One of the women reading from the sheet, plainly, into a
handset, while the other holds a torch on it because the sun isn't up on the page
yet.

**The gymnasium.** Forty people taking a line each, going round the folding
tables. An elderly man reads his line, hands the sheet on, sits down, and puts
his hands flat on his knees.

**The rooftop.** The man reading in his own language, in a coat, on a roof, with
the city waking up under him.

**The boat.** The boy reading it out loud while his grandfather watches the
horizon.

**The plateau.** A student reading, and getting to a line, and stopping, and
having to start it again.

> **NOTE —— 全片第二个史诗场面，而它是【很多普通人在很多普通房间里念一张
> 纸】。零特效。**
>
> 所有人念的是**同一份名单**，而画面在语言之间、纬度之间、时区之间切换，
> **不加字幕，不解释**。观众要听见的是**同一件事被同时说出来**。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许有配乐。** 只有人声、静电、风、茶炉、海。
> - **不许**有人念得慷慨激昂。这是一份名单。名单要念得像名单。
> - 那个念到一半停下来重念的学生，**是这一段唯一的情绪**。

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Clara, at the door, watching the dish through the window.

She is not looking at her husband.

## 7. THE NAME ON THE LOG

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — 10:41**

Ethan is thirty lines into the list. His voice is already going.

Behind him, through the window in the door, headlights swing across the yard.

Clara sees them first.

**INT. TRANSMITTER BUILDING — OUTER CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**

**MERRITT** (40s), the facility's duty officer, comes in out of the cold pulling
off gloves, entirely reasonable, holding a phone.

**MERRITT**
Right — somebody's going to tell me who booked this window, because the sheet
says calibration and that dish has been on the same bearing for an hour.

**CLARA**
*(coming out, closing the door behind her with her back)*
It's a records office allocation.

**MERRITT**
Which records office.

**CLARA**
*(and she does not lie, because she has never once lied in this building)*
None. It isn't allocated. We took it.

Merritt stops. He looks at her. He is not a villain and he is not stupid and he
has forty seconds to decide what kind of morning this is.

**MERRITT**
…Is anybody in danger.

**CLARA**
No.

**MERRITT**
Is anything being damaged.

**CLARA**
No.

**MERRITT**
*(a long breath through the nose)*
Then somebody signs the log. Right now, in front of me, with a name in it.
*(beat)*
Because at four o'clock somebody is going to ask me who was in this building, and
I am going to answer them, and I am not going to be the only name on that page.

He puts the log book on the sill and uncaps a pen and holds it out.

Behind the door, muffled, Ethan's voice goes on reading names.

Clara looks at the pen.

**CLARA**
Who does it go to? The name. Afterwards.

**MERRITT**
*(not understanding the question, answering it anyway)*
Site security. Then the agency. Then whoever asks.

**CLARA**
*(quietly)*
Yes.

And she takes the pen and writes her own name in the log, in full, clearly,
the way she has signed nineteen years of dockets.

**CLARA (CONT'D)**
Sinclair. Clara. Custodian, analogue preservation.
*(beat)*
I'm the one in the building.

**MERRITT**
There's a man in there.

**CLARA**
*(and she says it evenly, and it is the largest thing she has ever done)*
There's equipment in there.

Merritt looks at her for a moment. Then he caps the pen, and takes the log, and
tucks it under his arm.

**MERRITT**
Ninety minutes was the window. I'll be at the gate till eleven-ten and then I'm
locking it whether you're finished or not.

He goes.

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Clara comes back in and closes the door and stands against it.

Ethan is still reading. He does not look up. He cannot stop and they both know
it.

She watches the back of her husband's head for a while.

Then she picks up the printed sheet and holds it steady for him, because his
hands have started to go.

> **NOTE —— Clara 的兑现，而它是【签一个名字】。**
>
> `earth` 线的观众听过她说「如果是那条线还是你，我会选那条线」。
> 这一场就是那句话，而它的形态不是背叛、不是牺牲、不是挡子弹 ——
> **是她在一本值班簿上端端正正写下自己的名字，让更正从她这里过。**
>
> `mars` 线的观众没听过那句话，但这一场**照样成立** ——
> 他们看见的是一个女人在承担一件事。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - Merritt **不许**是敌人。他是个讲道理的人，他问的每一个问题都是对的，
>   而他要的东西合情合理。
> - **不许**有对峙、不许有拖延、不许有人挡门。
> - 「There's equipment in there.」这句之后**不许**给 Clara 特写。
> - 她回屋后**不许**跟 Ethan 说任何一句。她只是把纸拿稳。

## 8. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — LATER — 10:02**

The door opens.

**xÈth** comes in, alone, with his coat over his arm, the way a man comes into a
room he has been invited to. June behind him, and she stops just inside and does
not come further.

Nobody screams. Nobody draws anything. Ethan does not stop reading — he finishes
the line he is on, and then he takes his thumb off the key.

**xÈth**
Don't stop on my account.

**ETHAN**
I wasn't going to.

**xÈth**
*(looking round the room with real pleasure)*
God, look at it. Nineteen sixty-eight, most of this.
*(he touches a rack)*
There's no server in this building at all, is there.

**ETHAN**
No.

**xÈth**
No.
*(beat)*
That's very good, Ethan. I want you to know that I think that's very good.

He sits down on a swivel chair, uninvited, as he did in a cold house on a
northern island a hundred years ago.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
May I ask you something? You needn't answer.
*(beat)*
How long have you known about your father?

Silence. Clara, at the door, goes very still.

**ETHAN**
Nine weeks.

**xÈth**
*(nodding slowly)*
He was the only one of us who ever said no to me. In front of other people, in a
clean room, in nineteen seventy-seven.
*(beat)*
And I laughed at him. Because he was right, and I had no answer, and there were
four of them watching.
*(beat)*
It is the only genuinely stupid thing I have ever done, and I have had fifty
years to look at it.

**ETHAN**
Is that an apology?

**xÈth**
*(honestly)*
No. It's an account.

He looks at the clock. 10:07.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
You've eighty-three minutes. I'm not going to touch the breaker and I'd like you
to believe that.

**ETHAN**
I do believe it.

**xÈth**
*(pleased)*
Good.
*(beat)*
Because it wouldn't help me. That's the part you got right and nobody has ever
got right before. It's already left. Every second you've been talking, it's
already four hundred thousand kilometres out and there is not one thing in this
universe I can do about it.

**ETHAN**
Then why are you here?

xÈth thinks about it properly.

**xÈth**
Eleven.
*(beat)*
That's how many times I've done this. Eleven worlds. And on every single one of
them there was somebody in a room like this one, and none of them worked out the
light.
*(beat)*
You're the first.

**ETHAN**
And?

**xÈth**
*(and he asks it genuinely, which is what makes it terrible)*
And I would like to know what you think happens to **me** now.

Long silence. The counter beside the uranium coupon ticks.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
I'm not being rhetorical. I have never once been in a room where the answer
wasn't obvious.
*(beat)*
There is going to be a true account of me travelling outward at the speed of
light, forever, and I cannot reach it, and I cannot correct it, and in about
nine hundred years somebody is going to hear it.
*(beat)*
What am I then?

**ETHAN**
*(quietly)*
You're a man who did something.

**xÈth**
Yes.
*(beat)*
I haven't been one of those since eighteen ninety-four.

> **NOTE —— 全片的枢纽场次。它【不许解决任何事】。**
>
> 五个结局全部从这一场之后分岔，因此这一场必须把五种可能**同时**留在桌上：
> 他可以走、可以留、可以求、可以签、可以什么都不做。
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许有暴力，不许有威胁，不许有人挡门。** June 站在门口，
>   而她挡的什么都不是。
> - xÈth **不许**说任何足以被当成认罪的话。「It's an account, not an apology」
>   是他能给的极限。
> - 那个问题（「那我现在算什么」）**必须是真问题**。他真的不知道。
> - **不许配乐。** 房间里唯一的节奏是那个计数器。

## 9. THERE'S A WOMAN ON YOUR LIST WHO ASKED

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — 10:58**

Ethan reads. xÈth sits with his coat on his knees, listening the way you listen
to music you know.

**ETHAN**
*(hoarse, working down the sheet)*
—Ardenne. Kellerman. The fourteenth of every year.
*(beat)*
Eleven minutes, from a front door, on the eighth of March.

**JUNE**
*(from the doorway)*
No.

Everybody in the room turns round. It is the first word she has said since the
car.

**JUNE (CONT'D)**
*(and she comes in, one step, and she is shaking, and she is not backing down)*
Take that one off.

**ETHAN**
I can't take anything off. It's gone. I said it four minutes —

**JUNE**
Then say it wasn't the same.
*(beat)*
Because the rest of your list is things that were **taken.** And that one is a
woman who sat in a room with me and **asked.**

Silence. Ethan turns round in the chair properly for the first time.

**JUNE (CONT'D)**
She wanted eleven minutes. Not her daughter — she'd have fought you for her
daughter. Eleven minutes of a room she couldn't get out of.
*(beat)*
And you've just put it in a list of atrocities and fired it at the sky.

**ETHAN**
*(carefully)*
She can't consent to something she can't examine.

**JUNE**
*(and this is the best sentence anybody says in the film against him)*
**Neither can you.**
*(beat)*
You don't know what was taken from you. You've told everyone that all morning.
So on what authority do you decide that what she chose doesn't count, and what
you lost does?

Ethan opens his mouth.

And the honest answer is that he doesn't have one, and he is too tired and too
honest to pretend otherwise.

**ETHAN**
*(after a long moment)*
…I don't know.

**JUNE**
No.

**ETHAN**
*(and he turns back to the desk, and picks the sheet up again, and this is the
worst thing he does in the entire film)*
I'm going to keep reading.

**JUNE**
I know.

She goes back to the doorway.

And xÈth — who has not moved, who has not intervened, who has watched the only
real argument of the day happen between two people who both work for the other
side — says nothing at all.

But he looks at June for a long moment, and what is on his face is not triumph.

It is that he has just watched his quality control work perfectly, one last time.

> **NOTE —— `mars` 线观众的兑现，也是把五个结局撑成【真两难】的最后一根柱子。**
>
> 这场戏必须做到三件事：
> 1. **June 赢了这个论点**，而 Ethan 认了。
> 2. **Ethan 还是继续念** —— 这不是他的失败，是这部片的诚实：
>    **正确的人也会做无法辩护的事。**
> 3. xÈth **一个字都不说**。他插一句嘴，这场戏就变成他的胜利，
>    而它必须是 June 的。
>
> **`earth` 线的观众不认识 Audrey Leigh** —— 不要紧。
> June 在戏里把她讲清楚了，而那个论点本身不需要认识她。
>
> **禁止**：不许配乐；不许给 xÈth 反打特写；不许让 Ethan 找到反驳。

## 10. THE CORONATION, SECOND PASS

**INT. THE GREAT HALL — SAME MORNING**

The same room. The same four hundred people in the same coats. The same winter
light. The same quartet, the same phrase.

The same elderly Clerk at the same lectern, turning the same page.

**CLERK**
—and the Assembly, having sat in continuous session, and being satisfied as to
the instrument and the seals, resolves that the office and the style be
consolidated, and that the term be indefinite, **and that the succession be
determined by the holder.**

The same murmur. The same polite nods. Somebody near the back nods at a sensible
amendment.

The quartet plays.

The chair on the dais is still empty.

> **NOTE —— 加冕第二遍。**
>
> **逐帧同源。** 同一批镜头，同一个剪辑节奏，同一段音乐 —— **唯一变的是
> 书记员念出的那一句，和文件上的那一行。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - **不许**让任何人的表情有一丝不同。不许有人皱眉、不许有人交换眼神。
> - **不许**用任何视觉手段提示「这是重播」——没有闪白、没有倒带、
>   没有音效。**观众必须靠自己听出那句话变长了。**
> - 技术上：第二遍与第一遍**必须使用同一批生成素材**，只替换文件插入镜头。
>   这是全片成本最低的一次史诗，也是最狠的一次。

## 11. ONE OF THE NINETEEN

**INT. A SCHOOL GYMNASIUM — RADIO CLUB — MID-MORNING**

Forty people, folding tables, an urn. The sheet going hand to hand. A woman in
her eighties reading her line carefully into a handset.

The door at the end opens.

Two people come in — a man and a woman, coats, lanyards, entirely polite. They do
not shout. They stand just inside the door and wait for a natural pause, which
takes a while, because nobody stops.

**THE MAN**
*(when there is a gap, pleasantly)*
Morning. Who's the licence holder here?

A silence. And then **NINE PEOPLE** put their hands up.

The man looks at the hands. He gets out a notebook.

**THE MAN (CONT'D)**
*(patiently, and he means it kindly)*
All right. Names, then.

And the club secretary — a man of about seventy in a cardigan, with a mug —
gets up, and takes the sheet from the woman who is halfway down it, and reads the
next line into the handset, and then hands it on.

**THE SECRETARY**
*(to the man with the notebook, entirely without heat)*
You'll want to write faster. There's a lot of us.

## 12. THE LAST NINETY SECONDS

**INT. THE TRANSMITTER ROOM — 11:08**

The list is nearly done. Ethan's voice has gone. He has been reading for
eighty-eight minutes.

Clara has come in from the door and is standing beside him with the printed sheet
because he cannot hold it steady any more.

**ETHAN**
*(hoarse)*
…Norris. Thursday afternoon.
*(beat)*
Charlotte. Born in April.

Clara's hand stops on the sheet.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Daniel Vance. Nineteen seventy-seven. **Four in the room.**

He stops.

11:09.

He picks up the small flat coupon of grey metal and holds it beside the
microphone, and reads off the counter, digit by digit, without any expression at
all.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
Uranium two-thirty-eight. Fourteen thousand, one hundred and six counts per
minute. Measured now.
*(beat)*
So you'll know when we said it.
*(beat)*
And so you'll know nobody went back and changed it afterwards.

He puts the coupon down.

**ETHAN (CONT'D)**
That's all of it. That's everything we know.

11:10. The window closes.

Ethan reaches out and opens the breaker.

The sound of something enormous outside, very slowly, coming to rest.

> **NOTE (圣经 §2.4) —— 铀钟的兑现，而它是全片最冷静的一笔。**
>
> 他们发出去的不是证据 —— **他们发出去的是一份说不出证据的名单，
> 外加一个谁也改不了的时间戳。**

> 而 Clara 的手在纸上停住。**她仍然不知道 Charlotte 是谁。她现在知道
> 有过一个 Charlotte。**
>
> **禁止**：不许配乐；不许给 Clara 特写；不许有人哭。他念完，拉闸，
> 天线停下。

## 13. YEAR ONE

**INT. THE GREAT HALL — LATE AFTERNOON**

The same room. The same four hundred people. The same light — except the light
cannot be the same, because it is four hours later, and it is.

The same Clerk turns the same page.

**CLERK**
—and the Assembly resolves that the office and the style be consolidated, that
the term be indefinite, that the succession be determined by the holder, and that
the present year be reckoned, in all records and in all future instruments, as
**the first.**

The murmur. The nods.

And this time the camera does something it has not done: it goes, slowly, along
the front row of faces.

Four hundred people, entirely calm, entirely willing, none of them frightened,
all of them **certain this is how it has always been done.**

The quartet plays.

**INSERT — THE PROGRAMME** on an empty seat:

> **ORDER OF CEREMONY · 1 JANUARY · YEAR ONE**

And the chair on the dais is **occupied.**

He is sitting in it. He has been sitting in it all day.

> **NOTE —— 第三遍，而这一遍是【记录】，不是现场。**
>
> 观众在这一秒必须自己得出结论：他们刚看的不是同一场典礼的三次重播 ——
> **他们看的是同一份文件被改了三次，而画面老老实实跟着文件走。**
>
> 那把椅子从空到满，**是全片对「编辑」最直白的一次演示，而它一句台词
> 都没有。**
>
> **死规矩：**
> - 那把椅子**不许**有任何转场、任何叠化。它就是满的。
> - 扫过前排那些脸的镜头要**慢**，要**每一张都平静**。
> - 节目单是**确定性道具**，与第 1 场 June 拿起的那一份**逐像素相同**。
>   她三次试图更正的那个页眉，现在是对的了。

## 14. THE FORK

**EXT. THE DESERT — GATE — LATE AFTERNOON**

Long light. The dish at rest.

Ethan comes out through the gate with the notebook under his arm. Clara ahead of
him, already at the car.

By the road, xÈth stands with his coat over his arm, looking at the sky in the
direction the dish was pointing. June a few steps behind him, holding a clipboard
she has not written on since morning.

The two men look at each other across about nine metres of desert, exactly as far
apart as two platforms.

Neither of them says anything.

And in the sky above both of them, invisible, already four hundred million
kilometres out and accelerating away in every direction at once, is a list of
names that nobody on this planet can prove.
