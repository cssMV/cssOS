## 1. THE QUIET PLANET

**EXT. MARS — SURFACE — DAY**

Black. Then red, edge to edge, and no sound at all.

A plain. Rust-coloured, flat, going on further than the frame can hold. The
light is thin and clean. Nothing moves. Nothing is moving anywhere.

The camera does not move either.

Slowly we register that something in the plain is **straight.** A line, running
away from us to the horizon. Too straight for geology.

**CUT TO:**

**EXT. MARS — HIGH ABOVE — DAY**

The straight line is a road. The road is one of thousands. Below us, filling the
frame and running off all four edges, is a **city grid.**

It is not ruined.

There are no craters. No collapsed spans, no scorch, no rubble, no bones. Every
wall is standing. Every road meets every other road at the angle it was built at.
The whole thing is in **perfect order**, and it is **completely empty**, and it
has been for a very long time.

> **NOTE (bible v2 §三·场面 2) —— 这是全片最重要的一张图，不许拍成废墟。**
> 他们不摧毁，他们编辑。一颗**什么坏事都没发生过**的星球，因为所有发生过的
> 坏事都被移除了。**恐怖来自完好，不是来自破损。** 任何一处塌方、任何一具
> 尸骸、任何一道烧痕，都会把这一场戏毁掉。

Hold. Long enough to be uncomfortable.

**TITLE CARD:**

> **MARS**

**TITLE CARD:**

> **Some empires are built on land.**
> **This one is built on time.**

## 2. THE FIRST ARTEFACT — A BOOK

**INT. A COLD HOUSE ON A NORTHERN ISLAND — 1948 — DAY**

A room with the weather in it. Peat smoke, one paraffin lamp, rain going sideways
past the window.

A MAN in his forties sits up in bed with a typewriter on his knees. He is very
thin and he is very ill, and he types anyway — badly, fast, two fingers,
stopping to cough and not stopping for anything else.

> **CASTING / ERA NOTE:** 原型式，**不点名、不影射任何真人**（本片对外声明：
> 人物与事件均为虚构与原型）。要素只有三样：一座冷岛、一个正在死的小说家、
> 一本关于「一个会修改过去的国家」的书。

A knock. He doesn't stop typing.

**MAN**
It's open. It's always open. Nobody comes.

The door opens. **xÈth** steps in out of the rain — thirties, hatless, a good
coat, entirely dry. He looks around the room with what is unmistakably
**affection.**

**xÈth**
You've no fire on.

**MAN**
I've no wood.

xÈth crosses, crouches at the grate, and builds one. He does it well and quickly,
like a man who has built ten thousand fires. Neither of them remarks on it.

**MAN (CONT'D)**
You're from the publisher.

**xÈth**
No.

**MAN**
Then you're from the Ministry.

**xÈth**
*(pleasantly)*
There isn't one.

**MAN**
*(still typing)*
There will be.

The fire catches. xÈth sits down in the one chair, uninvited, and warms his
hands, and looks at the man with a directness nobody has used on him in months.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
You're forty-five. You've got perhaps two years, and the last one will be
appalling, and you know it, and you're typing through it because you think this
matters more.

**MAN**
It does.

**xÈth**
I know. That's why I came.
*(beat)*
I can take it out of you. All of it. Not the pain — the **illness.** You'd be
well by the spring, and you'd have thirty more years, and you'd write nine more
books.

The typing stops. Rain on the glass.

**MAN**
And in return.

**xÈth**
Nothing dramatic. I'd want that one to be a **novel.**

Long pause. And then — and this is the beat the whole film turns on — the man
**laughs.** Wetly, painfully, but genuinely.

**MAN**
Son. It **is** a novel.
*(he taps the page)*
Nobody would print it otherwise. Nobody would **read** it otherwise. I put it in
the fiction so it would get **out.**

xÈth is quiet for a moment. He has just been handed something for free.

**xÈth**
…Ah.

**MAN**
They'll all read it. Every one of them. It'll be on every shelf in the world.
*(he starts typing again)*
And not one of them will think it's about **now.**

**xÈth**
*(standing, gently)*
No. They won't.

He puts his coat back on.

**MAN**
You're not going to take the illness, then.

**xÈth**
*(at the door, and he means it kindly)*
I'd have taken it either way. You've simply saved me the trouble of asking twice.
*(beat)*
Keep the fire in. It'll go out if you don't feed it.

He leaves. The man types.

> **NOTE — 这场戏必须演成 xÈth 的胜利，而且是零成本的胜利。**
> 他没有威胁、没有收买、没有动手；他到的时候，警告**已经被作者本人归进了
> 虚构类**。这是他一百年里最省力的一次，而且它奠定了全片的规则：
> **他真的会给你止痛，而且他给的是真的。**
>
> **禁止**：不许让他露出冷笑；不许配音乐提示「反派得逞」；不许让小说家
> 意识到自己面对的是谁。观众要在下一场戏才反应过来刚才发生了什么。

**INSERT — a book, on a shelf.** Cloth spine, worn. A hand slides it back in
among the other novels.

We will see this shelf twice more.

## 3. THE SECOND ARTEFACT — A RECORD

**INT. ASSEMBLY CLEAN ROOM — JULY 1977 — DAY**

Hard cut into fluorescent white. Beige walls, a steel bench, four men in white
coats and paper caps. On the bench, face up: **a gold-plated disc.**

They are arguing, and they have been arguing for an hour, and they are enjoying it.

**MAN A**
It's a greeting card. You don't put a funeral on a greeting card.

**MAN B**
Put in the thunder. Put in the whales. Put in **all** of it.

**MAN A**
Why?

**MAN B**
Because if we only send the good half, we haven't sent **us.** We've sent an
advertisement.

> **NOTE:** 这一段与已拍摄的 `b_open` 冷开场同源、同基准板
> （`docs/timeempire-plates/scene-cleanroom-1977.png`）。**已拍的镜头全部继续
> 有效**，序章重用，不重拍。

CLOSE ON: gloved hands laying a strip of plating onto the cover.

**MAN C (V.O.)**
Uranium two-thirty-eight. A clock nobody can argue with.

**MAN A**
A clock for **whom?**

**MAN C**
For whoever finds it. They'll measure what's left and know exactly how long it's
been flying.

**THE FOURTH MAN**
*(who has not spoken until now)*
And they'll know we didn't lie about **when.**

**MAN A**
Why would we lie about when?

**THE FOURTH MAN**
Because someone is going to try to take it out.

The room goes still for exactly one second.

And then, from the back — by the door, arms folded, no paper cap because he is
not here to work — **xÈth** laughs. Once. Quietly. **Genuinely amused.**

The kind of laugh that gives everybody else permission.

The room relaxes. The joke has been identified as a joke. Someone else starts to
smile. Somebody turns away. The moment closes over.

> **PLANT (v1 铁律，v2 保留) —— 观众第一遍不该注意到他。** 失焦、不特写、
> 不给台词。第二遍看，他不可能被认错。
>
> **而现在这个笑有了第二层意思**：观众十分钟前刚看着他用同样的轻松处理掉
> 第一件遗物。他正在用同一种方式处理第二件。**这一次他错了**，
> 而他要过五十年才会知道。

Hands close the cover. Bolts go in. The disc is inside now, and nobody in the
room will ever see it again.

**EXT. LAUNCH COMPLEX — SEPTEMBER 1977 — DAY**

A Titan IIIE on the pad, seen small, across water, in real weather.

It goes.

Not triumphantly — just enormously, and then it is a spark, and then it is
nothing, and the birds come back.

> **NOTE:** 不用凯旋乐。这一场戏是**离开**，不是成就。

**TITLE CARD:**

> **The twentieth century left two things behind.**
> **One of them he corrected by doing nothing at all.**
> **The other one is now outside the solar system, and accelerating.**

## 4. A FALSE BIRTH

**INT. COUNTY RECORDS OFFICE — THE SOUTH — 1961 — DAY**

Ceiling fan. A ledger. A CLERK writes a line in it, bored, in fountain pen.

CLOSE ON the line as it is written: a date, a weight, a name.

The hand that pays the fee is xÈth's. We see only the hand and the cuff.

> **NOTE (已发布设定):**「a false birth in the south」。**一行字，一支笔，
> 十五秒。** 不要闪回、不要解释、不要配旁白。观众要在第一部才明白刚才看到的
> 是他给自己办的出生证明。

**MATCH CUT — the same ledger line**, sixty years later, on a screen, in a
verification query, with a green tick beside it.

## 5. NEAR-FUTURE AMERICA

**INT. CAMPAIGN HOTEL — BALLROOM — ELECTION NIGHT — NIGHT**

Noise. Enormous noise. Confetti in the air and a floor packed to the walls, and
on eight screens at once, the same face.

**xÈth** — not a day different from the man who built a fire on a northern island
— comes out onto the stage and lets it go on for a long time before he lifts a
hand.

He is not a demagogue. He does not shout. When he finally speaks, the room has to
go quiet to hear him, and it does.

**xÈth**
Thank you.
*(and he waits, because he means it)*
I'm not going to make you a promise about your taxes tonight.

*(laughter)*

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
I'm going to make you one about your grandchildren.
*(beat)*
Everything that has ever been done to you is still being done to you, because you
remember it. That's all history is. It's a wound that nobody is allowed to close.
*(beat)*
I intend to close it.

The room does not entirely understand him. The room roars anyway.

> **NOTE —— 这是本片最重要的一条铁律，从 v1 原样继承：他必须堂堂正正赢。**
> 这一段不许配阴森音乐，不许给观众任何「他是反派」的提示。观众必须在这一刻
> **想投他一票**。如果这场戏演成了独裁者集会，全片就塌了。

## 6. THE OTHER ONE IN THE ROOM

**INT. CAMPAIGN HOTEL — SERVICE CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**

Away from the noise. Bare bulbs, trolleys, a fire door propped open with a chair.

**ETHAN VANCE** (40s) stands with a drink he hasn't touched, listening to the
ballroom through the wall.

> **CANON (v2 §二):** Ethan 是 xÈth 的**老同学**，半人半火星，父亲是一个
> **选了我们这边的不朽者**。他能感觉到时间被改写过；他会死。
> 这两件事在他身上同时为真，而且互相为敌。

The ovation crests. Ethan closes his eyes — not in disgust. In **concentration.**
Like a man listening for a note that's slightly out.

He opens them.

**ETHAN**
*(to himself, quietly)*
…That's new.

**INT. CAMPAIGN HOTEL — SERVICE CORRIDOR — MOMENTS LATER**

xÈth comes through the fire door alone, still applauded through the wall,
loosening his collar. He sees Ethan. He is not remotely surprised, and he is not
remotely hostile.

He is **delighted.**

**xÈth**
Vance.

**ETHAN**
Mr President.

**xÈth**
*(waving it off)*
Don't. Not from you.
*(he looks at him properly)*
You've gone grey.

**ETHAN**
That happens.

**xÈth**
*(without any edge at all)*
Yes. I've read about it.

Beat. Two men who have known each other for forty years, one of whom has not
changed in any of them.

**ETHAN**
Something moved. Tonight, during the speech. Something behind us moved.

**xÈth**
*(gently, almost fondly)*
Ethan. Something is always moving.

He pats him once on the arm, on his way past, the way you touch an old friend
you'll see next week.

**xÈth (CONT'D)**
Come and see me. Properly. Bring Clara.

And he's gone back into the noise.

Ethan stands in the corridor with the applause coming through the wall.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.**

**MAIN TITLE:**

> **EMPIRE OF TIME**

> **PROLOGUE ENDS → PART ONE: `FILED AS FICTION`**

## 已拍的两张基准板（2026-08-12）

### `scene-mars-city-grid.png` —— 一次通过

引擎默认会给废墟星球。压住它靠的不是「不要废墟」，而是**互斥的正面物理事实**
（铁律 7），其中**遮挡那一条最硬**：

> each wall meets its own roof along a clean unbroken line, and each wall casts
> **ONE CONTINUOUS UNBROKEN SHADOW** across the ground for its full length ·
> every road runs unbroken from edge to edge and meets every cross-road at an
> exact right angle · a thin **even** layer of rust-red dust lies undisturbed
> over the entire grid, **the same depth everywhere**

**一道破损的墙投不出一条完整的影子。** 这一句比十条否定都管用。

造型上写死了「**像一个从上方看的档案柜**」（low flat-roofed blocks, all the same
size, a strict repeating grid, like a filing system seen from above）——
所以这颗星球不是异星奇观，它是**一套办公系统**，跟 §三·场面 3 那个体育场
办公室是同一个东西的两个尺度。
