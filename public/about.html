What makes it different

Watch, instantly

A finished MV plays with sound the second the app loads — no feed-scrolling, no setup.

Create, in one tap

Turn any idea into a full music video — visuals, vocals, and lyrics — generated for you.

Any language & voice

Every song can be sung in multiple languages and voices, each its own complete track.

The world's first emotion subtitles

CSS Studio renders the world's first emotion subtitles: lyrics aligned to the singing voice word-by-word at human-level precision, where each word carries its own emotion, color, and size, and bursts onto the screen in time with the music. Subtitles are no longer plain text — they have feeling. The same technology can extend to film, television, and short-form drama.

How it works

  • You describe a song, a story, or a theme — in any language.
  • CSS Studio generates the lyrics, the vocals, the music, and the video.
  • The finished music video plays back in an immersive, full-screen cinema — with emotion subtitles.
  • You can watch others' creations, remix ideas, and publish your own.

The company

CSS Studio LLC is an independent, digital-native software company, founded in 2026 and registered in Washington, D.C. (USA). It is the maker of CSS Studio — a fully online consumer platform delivered entirely through the web and native apps (web, iOS, Apple TV, and visionOS). The company designs and operates the end-to-end product: the generation pipeline, the immersive playback cinema, the emotion-subtitle technology, and the creator marketplace. There are no physical goods and no offline operations — the entire business is created, delivered, and monetized online.

Business model

CSS Studio is a digital-native consumer platform with a creator economy. Revenue is generated online through:

Per-work licensing

Listeners pay to listen to or buy out individual music videos created on the platform; creators earn from their work.

Tips & support

Audiences can tip creators directly, fueling a sustainable creator economy.

Subscriptions & credits

Memberships and creation-credit packs unlock more generation capacity for active creators.
