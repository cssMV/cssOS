What it is

Open cssstudio.app and a fully-produced music video plays instantly — sound, full-screen, zero clicks. Anyone then creates their own from one prompt: visuals, vocals, lyrics, in any language and any singing voice, without leaving the player.

The breakthrough — emotion subtitles

Lyrics are aligned to the singing voice word-by-word at human-level precision. Each word carries its own emotion, color and size, and bursts onto the screen in time with the music. Subtitles that feel — extendable to short-form drama, TV and film: a new global subtitle standard.

The stack (the moat)

Forced alignment

whisperX + wav2vec2 CTC, word-level (~20–50ms).

Stem separation

Demucs — vocals vs. instrumental, per track.

Speech-emotion

wav2vec2 SER (arousal/valence) → per-word emotion.

Generation

Multi-model video / music / voice, orchestrated.

Today this runs self-hosted on CPU. It works — and it's exactly the inference layer that becomes 10–100× more capable on NVIDIA GPUs.
