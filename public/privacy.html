Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-08 · CSS Studio · cssstudio.app

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email, display name, optional avatar, password hash (we never see plaintext).
  • Content you create: music videos, lyrics, comments, collections, templates, KYC tax info (encrypted at rest).
  • Usage data: pages visited, features clicked, performance metrics (Web Vitals), error reports — anonymized aggregates.
  • Device data: browser type, language, country (from IP), OS (no precise location).
  • Push subscription tokens: when you opt in to notifications.

2. What we DON'T collect

  • Your phone book, calendar, health data, financial data (cards are handled by Stripe / NihaoPay — we never see them).
  • Precise GPS location.
  • Browsing activity outside CSS Studio.
  • We do NOT sell your data. Ever.

3. How we use it

  • Provide the service: render your music videos, deliver notifications, save your work.
  • Improve the service: aggregate usage analytics drive design + perf decisions.
  • Communicate: weekly digest emails (opt-out one-click), product updates.
  • Legal compliance: tax records for affiliate / Premium revenue payouts.

4. Third parties we share with

  • Payment: Stripe (international), NihaoPay (China) — processes your card / Alipay / WeChat Pay.
  • Email: Resend — sends digest + transactional emails.
  • Analytics: Sentry (error tracking), self-hosted Web Vitals (no third-party cookies).
  • AI engines: OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, fal.ai, Replicate, Mubert, Cloudflare R2, etc. — strictly the prompts + media you submit, never your account info.

5. Data retention

  • Active accounts: kept until you delete the account.
  • Deleted accounts: purged within 30 days (logs may persist for security audit up to 90 days).
  • Database backups: rolling 30-day retention, encrypted at rest in Cloudflare R2.

6. Your rights

  • Access / Export: settings → "Download my data" (one-click ZIP of everything we have).
  • Deletion: settings → "Delete account" (irreversible, 30-day grace period).
  • Correction: edit your profile any time.
  • Email opt-out: every digest has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Block / Report: any content / user from anywhere.

7. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (cssos.sid) to keep you signed in. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We do not respect Do Not Track because we don't track you in the first place.

8. Children

CSS Studio is not directed at children under 13. If a parent learns their child has signed up, contact admin@cssstudio.app and we'll delete the account on receipt.

9. Security

All traffic is HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. Sensitive fields like tax IDs are AES-256-GCM encrypted. Database backups are encrypted at rest. We monitor uptime + cert expiry + slow queries 24/7 and have a documented disaster-recovery process.

10. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we'll email registered users 30 days in advance. Latest version always at cssstudio.app/privacy.
