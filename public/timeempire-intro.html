EMPIRE OF TIME
also known as “Empire of America” · a synopsis

Some empires are built on land. This one is built on time.

For over a century, an immortal visitor from Mars has walked among us — wearing human faces, claiming human births. He holds to one truth: whoever can rewrite the timeline owns the past and commands the future. His edits are written into our wars, our elections, and our quietest catastrophes.

A novelist once stumbled onto his secret and warned the world in a single famous book. The warning was filed away as fiction.

Now, in a near-future America, a new Envoy — xÈth — has surfaced from a false birth in the south, climbed through politics, and slipped into the highest office in the land. He means to crown a republic an empire, and to declare the first year of a new age.

Standing against him: Ethan, his old classmate — half human, half Martian, son of an immortal who chose our side — and the woman he loves. Across years of wits and shadows, the timeline forks toward a set of endings. In one, time is saved. In another, the Empire of Time begins.

This is a work of science fiction. Characters, an envoy, a president, and events are invented and archetypal. Any resemblance you feel to real people or history is your own — and it is yours to keep.
