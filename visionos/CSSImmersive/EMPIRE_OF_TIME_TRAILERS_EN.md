## TRAILER 1 — "EMPIRE OF TIME" · Feature Trailer

`COLD OPEN — a ticking, like a clock made of glass.`

**É** *(V.O., calm, not quite human):* "Every empire believes it invented the future. None of them remember who wrote it."

`CUTS: a forgotten war's trenches · a mushroom cloud blooming in reverse · a typewriter's keys · a red planet rising over a city skyline.`

**SUBTITLE:** *For a hundred years, someone has been editing your history.*

**ETHAN:** "My father chose to stay human. That's the only reason I can feel it — the exact second the world gets rewritten."

**CLARA:** "If we don't hold the line, no one will even remember there was a line."

**É** *(a gentle smile):* "I am not here to conquer you. I am here to spare you. Pain is simply a bug — and I intend to patch it."

`A presidential seal. A nation's flag, quietly recolored.`

**SUBTITLE:** *Whoever controls the rewrite of time controls the past — and the future.*

**ETHAN** *(whisper):* "Then we take the pen back."

**TITLE:** **EMPIRE OF TIME**
**SUBTITLE:** *Some empires are built on land. This one is built on time.*

## TRAILER 2 — "Constellations" · Trailer

`Star-fields. The camera falls through them like through pages of a book.`

**É** *(V.O.):* "You were promised a world without suffering. I have run that timeline nine thousand times. You always say yes."

**SUBTITLE:** *Every life you could have lived — already filed, already chosen for you.*

**CLARA:** "These aren't stars. They're decisions. Every one of them, someone else made for us."

**ETHAN:** "Then somewhere out there is the one they couldn't predict."

`Two hands almost touching across a table of light.`

**É:** "Love is the only variable I have never been able to fix."

**SUBTITLE:** *One thread he did not write. One choice still ours.*

**TITLE:** **EMPIRE OF TIME · CONSTELLATIONS**

## TRAILER 3 — "The Gambit" · Trailer

`A board. Not chess — a map of years. Pieces are nations.`

**É:** "Sit. We have played this game in every century. You have lost it in every century."

**ETHAN:** "I only need to win the last one."

`Quick: a stolen election certified in silence · a leader who is not who he was yesterday · warships turning toward a southern coast.`

**SUBTITLE:** *He has worn a hundred faces. This time, he wants the only one with a flag behind it.*

**CLARA:** "He's not running for power. He's editing the country into something that can't say no."

**É** *(soft):* "By the spring after next, there will be no republic to defend. Only an empire to belong to."

**ETHAN:** "Make your move."

**SUBTITLE:** *Two endings remain. Only one of them is still being written.*

**TITLE:** **EMPIRE OF TIME · THE GAMBIT**
